# One of our clients has a technician who posts job site videos on his personal TikTok.

## Post

One of our clients has a technician who posts job site videos on his personal TikTok.

His 30-second clips of the work get a few thousand views each.

Didn't think much of it until I checked their branded search volume in Search Console.

Branded searches for the company name spiked 70% over 4 months. And the spike correlated almost exactly with when the tech started posting consistently.

Here's why that matters for your Google rankings.

When people see a video, get curious, and Google your company name, that's a branded search. And branded search volume is one of the strongest trust signals Google uses to determine authority in your market.

More people searching for your business by name = Google trusts your brand more = you rank higher for the non-branded keywords that actually bring in jobs. "Plumber near me." "HVAC repair [city]."

It also feeds AI search.

ChatGPT and Gemini both want to recommend companies that real people actively search for by name. Branded search volume helps you show up across Google Maps, organic results, and AI recommendations at the same time.

The best part is it doesn't require a marketing team or a content strategy. One person on your crew posting quick clips from job sites a few times a week can move the needle on your SEO without you spending an extra dollar.

Social media is incredibly connected with SEO.
