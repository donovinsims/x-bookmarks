# We built an AI agent for an HVAC company that responds to every lead in under 60 seconds.

## Post

We built an AI agent for an HVAC company that responds to every lead in under 60 seconds.
 
Their CSRs were averaging 22 minutes and losing jobs because of it.
 
Here's what changed:
 
When a homeowner submits a website form, sends a message through LSAs, or reaches out on Thumbtack, the clock starts immediately. That person is actively looking for help. Most of the time it's urgent. AC out in July, furnace dead in January, hot air blowing instead of cold.
 
By the time your CSR calls them back 25 minutes later, they've already contacted 2-3 other companies. The first one to respond usually wins (assuming the price isn't absurd).
 
This HVAC company knew their response times were slow, so we built an agent to handle first response automatically.
 
Here's how it works.
 
When a lead comes in from any channel, the agent reads the message and responds in under 60 seconds with a personalized text. Not the generic "thanks for reaching out" auto-reply you've seen before. Something actually relevant to their issue.
 
The texts go out as blue iMessages, not green SMS. That alone makes a huge difference. Green SMS from an unknown number gets ignored or marked as spam. A blue iMessage feels like a real person texting you back, which is the entire point.
 
If someone submits a form saying their AC isn't cooling, the agent responds with something like "How old is the unit? Have you noticed any unusual sounds or is it just blowing warm air? I want to make sure we send the right tech."
 
It asks probing questions, gathers qualifying info, and keeps the lead engaged until a CSR can pick up.
 
By the time the CSR makes the call, they already know what the customer needs and the lead is warm instead of cold. The agent doesn't replace their team. It just makes sure no lead sits going cold while the CSRs are tied up.
 
21 days of results:
 
Response time went from 18-35 minutes down to under 60 seconds across Yelp, Thumbtack, LSA, and website form leads. Conversion rates jumped 35%+ in the first week compared to when CSRs were handling first response manually.
 
The reason this matters so much for home service businesses is that your leads are almost always urgent.
 
The problem I see all the time is we help a company get more consistent leads through SEO or ads, and their sales becomes the bottleneck.
 
The company that responds first wins the job the majority of the time.
 
If your average response time is over 20 minutes, you're paying to generate leads and then letting them walk to a competitor who got there faster.
