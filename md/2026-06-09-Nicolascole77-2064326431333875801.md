Step 1: Your book's Main Title/Subtitle is 80% of the work.
Before you write a single word of your book, you should nail down your title and subtitle.
The title helps guide you (and your reader). Without it you have almost no idea what you’re writing and your book will take you 100x longer.
To nail down your title, ask yourself these questions:
What question (of the reader's) are you answering?
What problem are you solving?
What solution are you unlocking?
How are you going to get there?
Don't start writing until you can answer these 4 questions.
Step 2: Make a list of all the Main Points you want to make in your book.
Now you have your title.
But you shouldn’t start writing! Instead, list out:
The Big Ideas that matter most
The order in which you wish to present them
If you can do this, with clarity, your book is 90% done.
Why?
Because these Big Ideas become your chapters. By planning out their order, you know the exact structure of your book.
 
Now, let’s expand each of these ideas.
Step 3: Under each Main Point, list out all the little sub-points.
Again, don't start writing yet.
Under each Main Point, list out:
What smaller ideas do you want to include?
What examples?
What insights?
What stories?
If you’re still stuck on how to expand your Main Point, use my “10 Magical Ways.”
This is a simple framework I came up with to expand any idea in your writing.
The 10 Magical Ways are:
Tips
Stats
Steps
Lessons
Benefits
Reasons
Mistakes
Examples
Questions
Personal Stories
Once you’ve done this, your book is 95% done.
Step 4: Now just go down your list of Main Points, section by section.
All your sub-points are your "notes" of what to write about.
This makes the writing 100x easier. Why? Because you literally have the words right in front of you!
Your outline is telling you what to write where (which is half the battle when writing a book).
Now you're 99% done.
Step 5: Do a final spelling/grammar read through.
Since you took the time to organize all your thoughts FIRST, by the time you "finish writing," you're done.
Give your book a once-over for spelling/grammar.
And you're 100% done.
