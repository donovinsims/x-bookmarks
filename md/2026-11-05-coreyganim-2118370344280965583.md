## Post

A friend who owns a barber school wanted to pay me $7,000 to turn his textbook into a custom GPT for his students.

There's a free GitHub repo that does the same job and adds quizzes and question banks.

It's called DeepTutor (55K stars). https://t.co/8tt6JrgTbm

His need gave me and idea for how I'd package this repo into an offer for licensing companies/trade schools:

-The buyer: insurance and real estate CE companies, CDL schools, trade licensing schools with 50+ students a month
-My cost: about $30/mo in model usage
-How I'd get the buyer's attention: turn their public study guide into a free 20-question practice tutor and send it to the owner
