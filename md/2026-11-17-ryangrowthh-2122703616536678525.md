Part Two: Content Pillars & Execution 

I like using 𝕏 as a top-of-funnel platform. You can generate a lot of impressions fast, then move people to the middle and bottom of your funnel (your newsletter, YouTube, a product).  

This section of the workbooks takes you through building an entire content strategy. From content pillars to reply-guying & more. 

Think of your content as a customer journey with three layers:

 

Two things people get wrong: 

Skipping the top of funnel. Lots of product talk and personal takes, but never tapping into what's hot in their niche. 

Staying extremely niche. You need ~60% top-of-funnel content to bring people in. 

 

 

Imo, aim for roughly a 60/20/20 split on 𝕏. 

Be value-driven: you primarily want to post about real things you've done, unique insights, thoughts, etc. 

 

 

 

 

 

Rule of thumb: if it doesn't give value and it doesn't build trust, you probably need to rework the angle (it's fine to have ~10% of your posts as sh*t posts)
