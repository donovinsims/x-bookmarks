# Deep dive into the current state of the 𝕏 algorithm.

## Post

Deep dive into the current state of the 𝕏 algorithm.

Worth bookmarking if you create content on 𝕏:

How your posts get found now:

• Followers see you through "Thunder," which holds recent posts from accounts they follow

• Non-followers find you through "Phoenix" (an AI retrieval model) and "SimClusters" (interest clusters)

• There's also a shared list of popular recent posts that gets mixed in

• Everything gets ranked together by the same model

How your post gets scored:

• An AI model predicts the chance each viewer will take each action on your post
• Each action has a "weight" to propose a score, which pushes your post
• Copy link: 20x
• Reply, quote, share via DM: 5x
• Follow: 4x
• Share: 2x
• Repost: 1x
• Like: 0.5x
• Reports, mutes, blocks, and "not interested" are HEAVILY negative. One report equals 350 likes...

What gets filtered out:

• Posts older than 48 hours
• Posts the viewer already saw
• Replies and reposts from accounts they don't follow
• Muted keywords, blocked and muted accounts
• Extra branches of the same conversation
• Anything flagged by visibility filtering (spam, safety labels, etc.)

What's new (October update):

• A "user debias" model is being wired in, adjusting for how generous each viewer is with engagement (users who are picky with their engagement get heavier weights)
• Search improvements
• The model now knows whether a post has video or an image

My advice:

• Focus on creating content that is bookmarkable, shareable, and worth commenting on
• Focus on original, high-value content
• Spread your posts
• Your first ~2 hours of a post are everything
• Avoid negativity so you don't collect reports, blocks, etc.
• Create multiple formats (video, article, etc.)

Bookmark this so you don't lose it.
