# I built an SEO ROI calculator this week.

## Post

I built an SEO ROI calculator this week.

Turns out most home service businesses can predict almost exactly when they'll see a return on investment.

Here's the rough math:

Start with your average job value.

For most contractors this is somewhere between $500 and $5,000 depending on the service.

Then figure out your close rate on inbound leads.

Most home service businesses close somewhere between 30-50% of the calls that come through Google.

If you don't know yours, use 35% as a conservative estimate.

Now you need to know how many leads SEO will actually generate.

This depends on your market size and competition, but a reasonable expectation for a business ranking top 3 in the Map Pack is 30-60 calls per month in a mid-sized city.

Let's run the numbers on a real example.

Say you're an HVAC company with a $1,200 average ticket and a 40% close rate.

You're paying $2,500 per month for SEO and it takes 5 months before you're ranking well enough to generate consistent leads.

That's $12,500 invested before the leads really start flowing.

Once you're ranking, let's say you're getting 40 calls per month from Google.

At a 40% close rate, that's 16 jobs.

At $1,200 per job, that's $19,200 in monthly revenue from SEO alone.

Your break-even point is somewhere around month 6.

Everything after that is profit on the SEO investment.

The variables that change this calculation are your average ticket size, your close rate, your market competition, and how fast your team can answer the phone and close the lead.

> Higher tickets break even faster.
> Better close rates break even faster.
> Less competitive markets rank faster which means ROI comes sooner.

The businesses that never see ROI from SEO usually have one of three problems: they're in a brutally competitive market and quit before month 8, their close rate is terrible because they don't answer the phone, or their average ticket is so low that the math never works.

If you want to run the numbers yourself, it's simple.

Multiply your expected monthly leads by your close rate by your average ticket.

That's your monthly revenue from SEO.

Divide your total SEO investment by that number to find your break-even month.

Most home service businesses in markets under 200k population break even somewhere between month 5 and month 8.
