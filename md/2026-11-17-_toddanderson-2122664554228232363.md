# We helped scale a fencing business with SEO and added $934k to their business in 90 days. 

## Post

We helped scale a fencing business with SEO and added $934k to their business in 90 days. 

This video broke down the website structure we used to do it and got 25k+ views on YT. 

Here's how you can copy what we did with AI (tag/give this to Grok Bot):

"You're a local SEO strategist for a home service company. Using my Google Business Profile, my website and my target keyword, audit and plan my website using this checklist. For each item, give a short pass/fail with the fix.

GUARDRAILS
- No rank tracker or grid scan tool? Use Google searches instead. Search "[service] near me" and "[service] [city]" for my city and 3–4 nearby towns. Note who shows up in the map pack and the top organic results each time.
- No browsing at all? Tell me, and ask me to paste the competitor names, their website URLs and a screenshot of the map pack.
- Count keywords from the live page text. If you can't load a page, say so. Don't guess.
- Never make up rankings, review counts, keyword counts or traffic. Label every number "measured," "estimated" or "unknown."
- If something you need is missing (my Google profile link, my site, my city), ask once, then work with what you have.

1. KEYWORD
- Pull the main keyword from my Google Business Profile's Performance tab (usually "[service] near me"). If you can't see it, use "[service] near me."

2. COMPETITORS
- Find the top 2–3 map pack profiles for the keyword, by grid scan or by the Google searches above.
- Skip any whose rankings come from a flood of new reviews.
- On the page each profile links to, count keyword uses (H1, H2s, title, internal links, area pages).
- Set my target at the #1 competitor's count plus a little more.

3. STRUCTURE
- Homepage → service pages + location hub → city pages → neighborhood pages. No page more than 3 clicks from the homepage.
- 1 Google profile links to the homepage. 2 or more profiles each link to their own service + city page.
- Number of location pages matches competitors (usually 30–50 per metro).
- URLs are /city-service, with no .html or .php.

4. HOMEPAGE
- Keyword in the H1 and H2s, not "Home" or a slogan.
- Every page links back to the homepage. All outside backlinks point to the homepage.

5. SERVICE PAGES
- One page per service, covering the whole region: jobs, crews, process, photos, years in business, reviews. No city-specific copy.
- Links go up to the homepage and across to other service pages, never down.

6. SERVICE + CITY PAGES (the page each profile links to)
- One service and one city per page; 50–80 keyword uses.
- Title tag: service + city + business name. Meta description around 140 characters, ending in a call to action.
- Sticky call button. H1 with service + city at the top. Contact form in the hero. Trust badges right below.
- Service + city in every H2: About, Services, Reviews, Map + neighborhood links, FAQs, Past jobs, Call to action.
- Schema that includes the business description, services, FAQs and offers.

7. NEIGHBORHOOD + SUB-SERVICE PAGES
- 4–6 per main location, aimed at the towns where I drop out of the map pack in the searches above.
- Each page is unique and built from real jobs, photos and neighborhood names from my CRM. No cloned pages, no zip code lists.
- Release 5–10 at a time, confirm they're indexed, then wait 2–4 weeks before judging the results.

Output: a pass/fail table for every item above (marking each number measured, estimated or unknown), then my top 5 fixes in priority order."
