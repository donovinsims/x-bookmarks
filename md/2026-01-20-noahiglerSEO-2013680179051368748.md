# Google ranking test:

## Post

Google ranking test:

If you're an emergency plumber or HVAC tech, go run a Google search for your main service at 9pm tonight and see where you rank.

Most of your competitors have their GBP hours listed as 8am to 5pm, which means Google deprioritizes them for after-hours searches because it assumes they're closed and can't help the customer.

That's free real estate for you.

The person searching "emergency plumber" at 9pm isn't price shopping.

Their toilet is overflowing or their AC died in August and they need someone NOW.

These are some of the easiest closes you'll ever get.

If you want to capture these leads, list your hours as 24/7 on your GBP and set up a system to actually handle the calls, whether that's an answering service, an AI voicemail, or just forwarding to your cell.

You don't need to answer every 11pm call yourself, but you do need Google to think you're available so you show up when your competitors don't.
