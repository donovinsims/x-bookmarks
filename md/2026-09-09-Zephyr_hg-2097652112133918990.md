> Most one-person businesses are five jobs done badly by one tired person. Five skills fix that, one per department.
>
> Marketing is /week-of-content. It reads your voice file and your last thirty posts, asks you three questions, then drafts the week. The mistake is skipping the questions.
>
> Sales is /proposal. It reads the catalog, the customer profile and your five best closed proposals, then writes the new one in the client's words. Put your price floor in the file or it will discount for you.
>
> The front desk is /client-reply. Inbox rules live in a file, not the prompt, so fixing one rule improves every reply after it. It drafts. It never sends.
>
> Operations is /weekly-close, on a Friday schedule: what shipped, what the numbers say, the three decisions you owe. Then it updates CLAUDE.md with what it learned.
>
> Quality control is /guardian. The other four call it before you see anything, which most people don't know a skill can do.
>
