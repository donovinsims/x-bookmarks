# your AI services lead magnet should help a prospect see a problem worth paying you to fix.

## Post

your AI services lead magnet should help a prospect see a problem worth paying you to fix.

5 options much better than a generic PDF:

– a 5-minute AI opportunity finder
– a workflow bottleneck scorecard
– a speed-to-lead calculator
– a prompt that identifies repeated manual work
– a free mini-assessment

the goal is to create the first useful result and make the paid next step obvious.
