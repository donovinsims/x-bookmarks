# What becomes more important as AI becomes more powerful?

## Post

What becomes more important as AI becomes more powerful?

Attention spans.

I actively feel my brain less able to focus on something for a long period of time, despite building more things than I ever have before.

If you’ve spent time building things with heavy AI assistance, you’ve probably experienced this:

1. You give a set of instructions for a task you want completed

2. It responds with a proposed plan of action

3. You review the plan and sign off on it for it to build

4. Then you immediately pull out your phone and start scrolling until it comes back to you with its progress

I do this more often than I’d like to admit.

A few ways I am thinking about combatting this:

• Do more of my work while walking
• Write things down by hand while I work
• Orchestrate multiple windows at a time

I don’t have a perfect solution to this one yet.

But I know that the person who can maintain their ability to focus, while also using AI to build tons of things, will beat the person who loses theirs.
