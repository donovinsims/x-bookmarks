# Most high ticket home service companies only ask for reviews after jobs.

## Post

Most high ticket home service companies only ask for reviews after jobs.

This applies to roofing, water damage restoration, construction/remodeling, and anything else over $10k average job size.

5-10 jobs per month caps your review potential.

Here's how to 3x that number:

Shift your perspective.

Inspections are your new review generation system.

You're doing 30 to 50 free inspections per month.

Maybe more.

Find ways to make these free estimates feel massively valuable.

Even if they don't hire you, they still got value.

That's a reviewable interaction.

Train your guys to ask on the spot after every inspection.

"If the inspection was helpful today, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Takes 30 seconds."

Hand them a QR code right there.

In-person asks convert way better than text or email follow-ups.

Then set up automated follow-ups for anyone who forgets. SMS at 12 hours. Email at 24 hours.

Most roofing companies get 3 to 5 reviews per month.

With this system you can hit 15 to 20 easily.

Imagine zooming past your competitors because of this strategy (while they beg roofing replacement customers for reviews).
