Why Claude needs to see social media first

Marketing engineers DON'T just automate content.

Posting on a schedule is part of the job, sure.

But it's a task.

Good marketing engineers are paid to decide which videos are even worth making.

And social automation only works once you understand social intelligence.

Social intelligence means knowing which videos, hooks and trends are winning with your buyers before you make anything.

Claude is REALLY good at pattern recognition.

Even when we treat it like a "writing tool"… it's predicting which words are likely to come next.

But it can't scroll TikTok, so it has no way of knowing which videos your buyers finished this week… it needs something to fill that gap.

So when you set up agents that are actively watching social media… they feed Claude better ideas to make decisions from.

It follows your niche: on repeat, adding search words as your niche moves and catching breaking stories the day they land.

It plugs into Claude: so every agent on your team works from this week's winners, with a link behind every claim.

Week 1: the research layer (departments 1-2)

This week is important… because every other agent runs on the videos these two departments find.

1. Niche research

It turns your context file into the exact words your agents search for on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, so they only pull videos your buyers would actually watch.

Why does that matter so much? Because an agent can only find what you tell it to look for.

The 5 agents

niche-definition: writes your niche in one line, like "pumpkin spice coffee for people who brew at home"

audience-and-offer: maps who you post for and your product, so every agent knows who it's looking for

leaders-and-competitors: lists the 3-5 brands your buyers already follow, because their names pull in the creators talking about them

search-phrase-finder: picks 7-12 short phrases people really type into the TikTok search bar

double-meaning-excludes: blocks words with a second meaning, so "latte art" never pulls in painting tutorials

Here's the task recipe. Claude plans four agents from your context file and waits for your OK before it builds anything:

The kit

skill: the niche brief, which turns your context file into one goal sentence and a keyword list

workflow: interview you → draft it → you approve → build the agent

template: the niche context file, six headings long

task recipe: the prompt above, which plans all four of your agents

audit: cut any keyword nobody would ever film a video about

Build your first agent →

2. Competitor tracking

It watches the 5-10 accounts you compete with every day, so you see their winning posts the morning they take off, not a month later.

(Pro tip: watch the brands your buyers actually follow… not whoever you're jealous of.)

The 5 agents

industry-agent: an agent on your whole category with no brand names, to surface competitors you didn't know you had

creator-tracker: puts a competitor's account on Tracking and flags any video doing far better than their typical views

answer-video-planner: plans the video that answers a competitor's winner, with the same hook and format

The kit

Week 2: the intelligence layer (departments 3-6)

This week is the fun one.

This is where social intelligence actually happens: four departments that tell you which videos, creators, hooks and formats are winning, and why.

3. Outlier videos

It finds the videos in your niche getting far more views than their creators usually get, which is the clearest sign the idea itself works.

The 5 agents

outlier-finder (run first): pulls the videos doing far better than their creator's typical view count

niche-match-filter: keeps real, on-niche posts and drops reposts, AI clips and paid ads

views-plus-engagement-ranker: ranks videos on views and engagement combined, so a big number with no reactions sinks

saves-shares-ranker: re-sorts the list by saves and shares for every 1,000 plays, because a save means someone wants it again

dead-views-flag: flags the big-number posts nobody saved or shared, so you never copy a fluke

4. Outlier creators

It spots the creators who go viral again and again, because one viral video can be luck and five in a row is a method you can study.

A creator with five outliers is basically a free course on your niche… with a new lesson every week.

The 5 agents

creator-finder: finds the creators in your niche who go viral over and over

engagement-per-1k: scores them on engagement per 1,000 views, whatever their follower count

two-outlier-minimum: only counts a creator with two or more outliers

30-day-history-flag: labels anything posted over a month ago as old news

creator-watchlist: puts the top five on Tracking so you see their next post first

The kit

skill: the creator study

workflow: find → score → filter → add to Tracking

audit: keep only accounts your buyers actually watch, not anyone who happened to blow up

Find the creators to study →

5. Hooks

It collects the first few seconds of every winner word for word, so you never write an opening line from a blank page again.

The 5 agents

hook-bank: copies the opening words of 20 winners exactly as they're spoken or shown on screen, never the caption

structure-grouper: sorts them by the shape underneath, like "a small disaster, then the product survives"

structure-counter: counts how many winners use each of those

median-views-ranker: ranks the shapes by their typical views

first-film-picker: picks the one to film first this week
