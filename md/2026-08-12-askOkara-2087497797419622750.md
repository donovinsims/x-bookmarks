# b2b seo content tier list

## Post

b2b seo content tier list

s tier
- bottom of funnel pages
- comparison + alternative pages
- high intent how to posts
- use case / integration pages
- free tools, calculators, templates and generators

a tier
- long-tail posts that answer what your icp is looking for
- landing pages built around high-intent searches
- original research, proprietary data, and industry benchmarks

b tier
- broad how to posts
- thought leadership with no search intent (post it on linkedin bro)
- generic best x listicles
- weekly roundups

c tier
- product updates (only work when you've crossed a big milestone - funding / revenue. when that happens a lot of people search for it)
- anything written for shareability with no search intent
