Biggest mistake: building an audience first.

It's procrastination, and it makes you a broke content creator begging for likes.

Build a digital business instead. Think like a business owner from the start. Forget being an influencer.

Here's how:

### 3/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087492562227441858

Step 1: Create your core high ticket offer

If you don't know what you're selling, it'll be impossible to build the right audience. Rules:

• Must link to your skills + experience
• Must solve a real problem that has an ROI
• Must not be nice-to-have
• Must be clear and simple to understand

### 4/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087492818742751680

Don't try and be smarter than the market

Sell something that is already selling. Make a list of similar people who you admire and run the business you ultimately want.

Funnel hack them. See what they're selling. Then sell your version of the same offers.

### 5/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087493073450172610

Step 2: Act fearless

Assume you can figure it out. Assume there is no competition. Assume you'll hit 6-figures.

There will be no certainty at the start. You must create it in your mind.

Treat building this business like a project. If you succeed, you make money. If you fail, you learn.

There's no such thing as a waste of time.

### 6/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087493568969458122

Step 3: Build low ticket p0rn

Taking leads straight from social media to a $20K offer is dumb. People don't buy like that.

Create a low ticket offer like a workshop, community, or digital product.

Use it as a way to see who has a problem and who doesn't. Focus your sales energy on the people who buy.

$100 is a great price point.

### 7/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087494073003167902

Step 4: Create an uns*xy lead magnet

The selling happens in email and on Zoom calls, not on social media.

A lead magnet is a free thing you give away in return for a person's email.

Most people f*ck this up.

### 8/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087494441925832979

A profitable lead magnet looks like this:

• Solve a small desirable problem for free
• Deliver as an email course or GPT
• Save people time as the core ROI
• Make it more valuable than the paid equivalents

You won't launch one and get rich. You must A/B test several until one converts at 35% or higher

### 9/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087494834143527358

Step 5: Start selling like a crazy person

• Launch high ticket and low ticket offer every month to the email list

• Cap spots and have a deadline

• Start conversations then take them to Zoom. High ticket is sold on Zoom. Low ticket is sold via a landing page

• Treat it like a repeatable system

• Expect results to be minimal on the first run. Aim to beat your last record every time.

### 10/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087495336843514278

Step 6: Goal is one client

The first client changes your mindset. It gives you the confidence.

A small goal is easier to hit. You'll land this client through conversations and ruthless follow up.

Once you land them, get them a result. Then ask them for referrals.

### 11/13

https://x.com/Tim_Denning/status/2087495599847268819

Step 7: Avoid complexity

The temptation is to:

• Listen to gurus
• Stack lots of offers
• Price low out of fear
• Overanalyse the data

Simple is good. One core offer with one funnel is all you need to hit $1M a year.

Doing anything else is setting you up for failure. You're only one person. You can take far fewer actions than you think.

Simple is hard... and that's why it's easy.
