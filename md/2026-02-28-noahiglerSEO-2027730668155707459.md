# There are two types of Google Business Profiles: service area businesses and physical location businesses.

## Post

There are two types of Google Business Profiles: service area businesses and physical location businesses.

A good amount of smaller businesses pick the wrong one.

Here's why it matters:

A service area business is a profile where you hide your address and just list the cities you serve. This is what Google recommends for businesses that go to the customer's location, so pretty much any home service business.

A physical location business is a profile where your address is visible and customers can visit you. Think auto repair shops, dental offices, restaurants.

Google trusts physical location profiles significantly more than service area profiles when it comes to Map Pack rankings. A business with a visible, verified address in a city will almost always outrank a service area business targeting that same city, all else being equal.

The reason is simple. Google can verify you're actually where you say you are. With a service area business, you could claim to serve a city 3 hours away and Google has no way to confirm it. With a physical location, your address is pinned on the map and Google knows exactly where you are relative to the searcher.

This is why some contractors open small offices in the cities they want to rank in. A 400 square foot space in a strip mall costs $500-700 a month, which is the price of a single job for most trades. But it gives you a physical location profile in that city, and that changes your Map Pack visibility completely.

You don't need to rent 5 offices tomorrow. But if there's one high-value city where you're struggling to rank and the search volume is there, a small physical presence can do more for your rankings than 6 months of link building and content creation.

If renting isn't realistic, at minimum make sure your GBP is registered at an address as close as possible to where your actual customers live. An office in an industrial park 15 miles outside your target neighborhoods is hurting you every single day. Google measures the distance between your address and the searcher, and even a few miles can be the difference between the Map Pack and being invisible.

One more thing: if you currently have a service area profile but you do have a physical location where customers could visit, even if most of your work is on-site, switch your profile type. You can still list service areas on a physical location profile. You get the best of both worlds and Google gives you more trust in the rankings.
