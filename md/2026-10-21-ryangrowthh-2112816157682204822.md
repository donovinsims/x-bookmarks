# Grok Bot is basically AGI for 𝕏 content.

## Post

Grok Bot is basically AGI for 𝕏 content.

It helped me generate ~2M views in my first 2 weeks on the platform.

Here are 10 cool use cases:

1. 𝕏 Researcher Bot: Scans outlier content overnight and delivers a full report in the morning with content ideas that align with my niche.

2. Chief of Staff: My main bot that manages all my MCP connections, routines, etc.

3. X Article Tracker: A Bot dedicated to tracking all my article ideas (I can just text my bot instead of logging stuff in Notion).

4. Analytics Coach: Sends me a daily report of my impressions (+ all my replies included)

5. Competitor Watch: Tracks my X lists for alpha

6. Bookmarks Brain: Logs all my bookmarks + sends me a daily summary of posts I might've forgotten about (also summarizes articles/long-form content)

7. Quote Post Scout: Finds high-performing posts in my niche worth quote-posting with my own take.

8. Fact Checker: Double-checks stats and claims in other people's posts

9. Sparring Bot: A Bot dedicated to "sparring" 𝕏 ideas

10. Banner and Visual Prompter: Writes image prompts for my article banners and post graphics

It would also be cool to launch a Bot to hunt paid promos/sponsors.
