# A contractor I work with has exact match Google profiles for two of his main services.

## Post

A contractor I work with has exact match Google profiles for two of his main services.

He's outranking companies with 4x his reviews on Google Maps.

The strategy is stupid simple. He filed DBAs for his three highest volume services and created a separate Google Business Profile for each one.

So instead of just one profile called "Smith's Electrical," he now has "Smith's EV Charger Installation & Repairs" and "Smith's Home Surge Protection."

Each one targets a different set of searches, ranks independently, and gets calls from a way bigger radius than his single generic profile ever did.

Google treats your business name as a direct relevance signal. So when someone searches "drain cleaning near me," a profile literally named "Smith Plumbing and Drain Cleaning" will almost always outrank "Smith Home Services."

It's not even close.

His competitors have more reviews, more years in business, and bigger marketing budgets.

Yet none of that really matters.

He shows up above them because his profile name matches exactly what people are searching for (and we did some really good SEO).

The part that surprised me is how much it changed his call quality too.

When your profile name includes the specific service, the people calling already know what you do.

Less tire kickers, more ready to book jobs.

A DBA costs about $45 and takes a week to process. You tie it to your main LLC so it's still your business.

This is completely within Google's guidelines as long as it's your legal business name.

Probably the cheapest ranking boost in local SEO.
