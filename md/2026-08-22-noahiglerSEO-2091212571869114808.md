# My bet: social media becomes a large part of our local SEO strategy within two years.

## Post

My bet: social media becomes a large part of our local SEO strategy within two years.

Ask ChatGPT or Google AI Mode for the best HVAC company in a major city.

A lot of the recommendations are older brands that have been visible everywhere for years.

They have hundreds of reviews, radio ads, billboards, TV, sponsorships, local news coverage, and people searching the brand by name.

AI systems can find years of public evidence connecting that company to HVAC in the market.

A younger home service brand can build the modern version of that footprint through social media.

The goal is not to post random dances or hand the account to someone who has never stepped inside a service business.

The content should document the company doing real work:

> Finished jobs with the service and city explained naturally
> Techs walking through what they found and how they fixed it
> The owner answering the questions customers ask every week
> Team training, hiring, and what the operation looks like behind the scenes
> Community work, local sponsorships, and the people involved

Then distribute the same raw material across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.

YouTube gives the company longer videos, transcripts, and pages that can show up in search. Short clips create repeated exposure. Facebook keeps the brand visible inside the local market where customers already ask for recommendations.

Three reels will not move a map grid.

A year of people seeing the company, sharing the jobs, searching the brand, and mentioning it across the web creates a very different footprint.

It also makes every other channel work better. Someone sees the truck, hears the radio ad, finds a short video, then searches the company and sees strong reviews. Google gets the branded search. The customer gets more reasons to trust the name.

I would run a simple weekly system:

> Break down one finished job
> Put the owner or a technician on camera to teach
> Show the team or community once a week
> Turn the strongest monthly topic into a longer YouTube video

Give it 6 to 12 months before judging it.

We're testing branded search, direct traffic, local mentions, and AI citations alongside the normal SEO numbers.
