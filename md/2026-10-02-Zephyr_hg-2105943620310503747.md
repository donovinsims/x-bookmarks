# I made a prompt that finds what's actually breaking your business, using the method Toyota built its factories on.

## Post

I made a prompt that finds what's actually breaking your business, using the method Toyota built its factories on.

It's called the 5 Whys. Toyota made it standard on the factory floor: when something goes wrong, you ask why five times before you fix anything.

The first answer is almost always a symptom. "The client got the wrong invoice." Why? "I sent it late at night." Why? "I do invoices after the real work is done." Keep going and you reach the thing you can fix: nothing checks an invoice before it goes out.

Most one-person businesses fix the symptom and meet the same problem next month.

This prompt runs the 5 Whys with you one question at a time, won't accept a guess, and turns the root cause into a rule or a job Claude checks every week.
