# How I made $3,000 in 60 days selling "Mini-SaaS" to local restaurants (0 coding, 0 meetings)

## Post

1/ A lot of people think "AI Business" means building the next OpenAI. They’re wrong. The real money is in the "Digital Plumbing": finding 2015-era businesses and giving them a 2026-era brain. I focused on restaurants. No meetings. No cold calls. Just pure execution.

Here is the full $3k Blueprint. 👇

2/ The Opportunity: The "PDF Trap" 90% of high-end restaurants have terrible websites. They spend $100k on interior design but their menu is a 15MB PDF that takes 10 seconds to load on a smartphone. In the attention economy, that’s a death sentence. I decided to solve this using "Vibe Coding."

3/ Step 1: The Zero-Cost Lead Machine I don’t pay for Apollo or ZoomInfo. I use my MacBook and a Chrome extension called "Instant Data Scraper" on Google Maps. Search: "Restaurants in [Province Name]". I filter for 4.5+ star reviews but 1.0 star websites. Result: An Excel file with 200+ qualified leads in 5 minutes. Cost: $0.

4/ Step 2: The "Data Heist" (Ethical Version) I need their menu, but I’m not typing it manually. I open their old site, hit Option+Command+U to see the source code, and feed the mess to Claude. The Prompt: "Extract every dish, price, and the ORIGINAL image URL for each item. Format as a clean JSON." Now I have their entire business brain in my clipboard.
A luxury site needs high-end visuals. If the restaurant's site has blurry photos, I head to their TripAdvisor "Management Photos" or "User Photos" section. The Hack: I use the "Image Downloader" extension to bulk-save the most mouth-watering, real-life shots of their signature dishes. Authentic photos = Instant Trust. I upscale them for free using AI to make them pop.

5/ Step 3: Rapid Prototyping with @lovable_dev This is where the magic happens. I don't "design"; I describe. I use Lovable to build a high-performance Single Page Application (SPA). The Prompt: "Build a luxury dark-mode site for [Restaurant]. Use these real URLs for images. It must be 10x faster than the original. Add a sticky 'Book Now' button." Total build time: 12 minutes.

6/ Step 4: The "Ghost" Outreach Strategy I don't do meetings. I hate them. I use a Cold Email stack (Brevo + Mailtrack). The email isn't a sales pitch; it's a "Look what I made for you" moment. I send a link to the Lovable preview. When they see their own Salmon Tartare looking like a masterpiece on a $500 website, the psychological "Endowment Effect" kicks in. They already feel like they own it.

7/ The Financial Breakdown:

Month 1: 2 clients closed at $500 each. Total: $1,000.

Month 2: 4 clients closed at $500 each. Total: $2,000.

Grand Total: $3,000. Expenses: $0 (I used free tiers for everything). Profit Margin: 100%.

8/ Why this is better than a SaaS: A SaaS takes months to build and has 90% churn. This is "Productized Service." I sell the "Identity" of the restaurant. Once I host it, they pay me a $20/month maintenance fee just to keep the lights on. That’s $120/month in passive income from just 6 clients.

9/ The "2026 Tech Stack" for this Side Hustle:

MacBook (M-series preferred for local AI).

Google Maps + Instant Data Scraper (Leads).

Claude/GPT (Data Extraction).

Lovable/V0 (Development).

Brevo (Mass Outreach).

Mailtrack

10/ Every local business is a goldmine waiting for an AI-powered upgrade. Stop overthinking and start scraping. The tools are free. The information is everywhere. The only thing missing is your execution.

11/ Final thoughts.

You can easily copy my method and apply it to any local market. The screenshots I’ve shared are from the Italian market where I’ve been testing this, it’s a goldmine if you know where to look.
