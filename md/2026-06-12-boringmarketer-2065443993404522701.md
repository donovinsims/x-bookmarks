# there's no reason for an SMB to have a leaky marketing funnel anymore.

## Post

there's no reason for an SMB to have a leaky marketing funnel anymore.

- ask Fable to map your entire customer journey
- share your tools (google analytics, google business profile, google ads, phone system, crm, invoicing system, etc)
- get a google cloud project setup
- give Fable API access to everything
- ask it to automate the data flow, build dashboards, whatever

here's what I did:

1) I gave it a google cloud project, it used the browser to enable the tools I need setup.
2) I gave it access to Quo (phone system), Quickbooks (invoices)

It now analyzes everything from call volume, to call transcripts, etc through the whole journey and then uploads customer data back into the ads for better targeting.

It all runs on a schedule via GitHub actions and keeps the data in my database.
