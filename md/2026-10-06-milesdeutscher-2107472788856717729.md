How to Forward-Test Your Winners

Everything up until this point has been a backtest. While backtesting helps validate an idea, we need to actually forward-test before risking any real capital.

The process I use to forward-test with low stakes:

Step 1: Understand Why It Works

Before you test anything, figure out why the strategy made money in the first place.

Claude can help here:

Step 2: Optimise the Variants

Once you understand the strategy, start making tweaks.

What happens if you add max drawdown protection? Tighten the stop? etc.

One warning: be careful not to over-optimize. If you tweak a strategy until it fits the past perfectly, it will almost certainly fail in the future. Every change should have a logical reason behind it.

Step 3: Paper Forward Test

Now run the strategy live, with fake money.

Step 4: Small Live Test

If the paper test holds up, move to real money. But small. An amount you'd be completely fine losing.

Step 5: Scale Up Over Time

Only once the strategy has proven itself live do you increase the size.
