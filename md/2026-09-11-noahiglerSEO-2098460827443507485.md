# Copy the service page structure we use for home service businesses.

## Post

Copy the service page structure we use for home service businesses.

Start with one service and one search intent. An AC repair page should focus on AC repair instead of trying to rank for every heating and cooling service the company offers.

Build the page in this order:

> A clear H1 naming the service
> A hero section with the service area, phone number, and booking button
> Reviews, years in business, licenses, guarantees, and financing near the top
> The common problems that cause someone to need the service
> What the repair or installation includes
> Real photos and examples from completed jobs
> Reviews that mention the same type of work
> Equipment and brands the company services
> Answers to the questions customers ask before booking
> Links to related services and the markets where this service is available
> A final call button and short booking form

Use the same service in the URL, title tag, H1, and main copy so the page has one obvious subject.

Link to it from the main service hub, relevant location pages, completed projects, and useful articles on the same topic.

Put the call button and strongest proof near the top. A customer should not have to read the entire page to understand what the company does, why they should trust it, or how to book.
