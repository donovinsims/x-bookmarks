# Every review tactic I've used across 100+ home service shops.

## Post

Every review tactic I've used across 100+ home service shops.

Tiered by how many reviews each one actually produces.

(Bookmark this)

S-TIER (Build these first)

> The in-person ask at job completion. The tech asks while the customer is standing there happy, then texts the link on the spot. Nothing else on this list comes close.

> Tech incentives. $20 to $40 per review that names the tech. Pay it fast and pay it publicly so the whole crew sees it work.

> One review link everywhere. Invoice, follow-up text, email signature, QR on the truck. Same link, zero friction, no hunting.

> The weekly chase list. Every completed job with no review gets a follow-up call or text from the office. Most reviews die from nobody asking twice.

A-TIER (High return)

> Customers mentioning the service and the city. Ask them to say what you did and where. Those words are ranking content you can't write yourself.

> Photo reviews. A picture of the finished job inside the review stands out on the profile, builds instant trust with the next customer, and gives Google real proof the work happened.

> Steady weekly pace over batch blasts. 10 a week looks like a busy company. 100 in one weekend looks like something else.

> Texting the link within an hour of job completion, while the experience is still fresh.

B-TIER (Worth having)

> Responding to every review within 24 hours. It's not a ranking factor, but it shows Google an active business, shows future customers you care, and people read your responses more than the reviews themselves.

> QR signs at the counter and on leave-behinds.

> Email requests. Low hit rate, but they cost nothing.

> Yelp, BBB, and Facebook reviews. Light weight for map pack rankings, but AI search pulls from these platforms hard now, so they matter more than they did a year ago.

F-TIER (These get profiles suspended)

> Buying reviews. Google's filters catch it now, and the penalty can wipe out years of real ones.

> Blasting your entire past customer list in one day. The velocity spike gets flagged.

> Friends and family with no service history.

Pace is what Google ranks. A shop adding 10 a week will pass one sitting on 400 old ones. Everything in S-tier exists to make the pace automatic.
