# A plumbing company we work with generated almost $200,000 in revenue from SEO in the past 90 days.

## Post

A plumbing company we work with generated almost $200,000 in revenue from SEO in the past 90 days.

Their monthly SEO budget is $3,000. That's roughly a 22x ROI.

I want to be transparent here... a huge part of this result comes down to the client.

They had a solid business before we ever got involved.

Good reputation, good team, and they already happened to have an exact match business name with their core services in it. That's one of the biggest ranking advantages you can have on Google Maps, and they had it from day one.

What we did was come in and build the system around what they already had going for them.

Here's the breakdown of what the past 6 months have looked like:

Month 1-2: Foundation + reviews

We started with an audit of everything. Their Google Business Profile, website, citations, backlink profile, and competitors.

Their GBP needed work. Categories were off, service areas needed to be tightened up, photos were outdated, and they weren't posting at all. We cleaned all of that up and built out a consistent posting schedule.

Citations were inconsistent across the board. Their business name, address, and phone number didn't match across dozens of directories. Some had old addresses, some had the wrong phone number, some had a slightly different business name.

We cleaned up every single one and built out new citations on every relevant platform. Sounds boring, but inconsistent NAP data is one of the most common things holding local businesses back from ranking.

Reviews:

They were already getting 5 to 10 per month but we tweaked the copy being sent and they now average 15+ per month. This alone moved them past a handful of competitors who had been sitting on more total reviews but weren't generating new ones. 

This has been a massive needle mover .

Month 2-3: Content and link building

We built out dedicated service pages for every high-ticket service they offer. Each page was optimized around the keywords people in their metro are actually searching for, with real content that answers the questions homeowners have before they call.

We started a consistent link building campaign focused on local relevance. Local directories, industry-specific sites, partnerships, and digital PR. Transparently, this is where a good portion of the budget goes to each month.

Month 4-6: Compounding

This is where SEO starts to get fun.

All the foundational work from the first few months started compounding in addition to ongoing off-page work. Rankings improved across nearly every keyword.

They went from barely showing up for their high-ticket services to consistently ranking in the top 3 in the Map Pack across their metro (12 mile radius).

The result:

Almost $200,000 in revenue tracked directly from SEO in the past 90 days. From a $3,000/month investment.

I want to be clear about something... this is not possible without having a good business and patience for SEO to compound.

Not every client is going to see these numbers.

But this is what's possible when everything lines up and you give SEO the time it needs to work...
