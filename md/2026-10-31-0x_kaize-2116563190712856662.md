Part 1: The formula: how to write a Grok Bot prompt that actually works
Every good Grok Bot prompt has five parts, that is exactly what is written in the xAI documentation getting started guide:
Outcome: what should be finished
Sources: where the Bot should look
Constraints: what it must not do
Deliverable: what you get back and in what format
Stop point: where it pauses and asks you
 
And four rules on top:
One Bot, one job: permanent rules go in the description, today’s task goes in the message
Connectors first, browser last: a browser task can cost 45x more tokens
Add "write unknown instead of guessing" to anything that involves research
Draft first, send never: posting, buying and deleting always need your approval
Every prompt below follows this. Swap the [brackets] and paste.

Design and Grok Imagine
The lighting trick (via @DaQin99)
A candlelit desk in an old library, the candle is the only light source, warm tones, shallow depth of field, 35mm film look.
Consistency lock for extending video
Continue from the exact final frame of the previous video with identical characters, faces, clothing, environment, lighting and style. No changes. From the moment where [ending state], [next action]. Smooth cinematic motion, no jumps.

Operations and productivity
Inbox Triage (inspired by this r/grok prompt)
Scan my inbox for messages since the last run that need a reply. Skip newsletters, receipts and notifications. For each: sender, subject, one line on what they want and a draft reply in my voice. Never send. If nothing needs a reply, send nothing.
Overnight Work Queue (based on prompts.chat)
During the day I'll send tasks prefixed with QUEUE. Overnight, work through every reversible task that doesn't need my approval. Skip anything that sends, buys, deletes or publishes. At 7:30, report: done, skipped and why, and what needs my decision.
Go deeper: @0xMorlex’s team-of-bots prompt based on Lauren Tan’s approach, @melvindvivas’s Chief of Staff dashboard, David Gan on support and n2parko on product
Finance and markets
These research. They don’t trade.
Overnight Market Brief
At 7:00 every weekday, research [market or tokens] from the last 24 hours: price moves, news, on-chain flows and what credible traders on X say. Separate facts from opinions and cite every claim. Return the 3 most important developments and what would change the picture. Never place or suggest a trade size.
Subscription Killer
Go through my email receipts from the last 90 days and list every recurring charge: service, amount, billing date, last use if you can tell. Flag the ones that look unused. Don't cancel anything.
