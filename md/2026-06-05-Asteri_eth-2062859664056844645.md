# One guy made $50,000 just by selling websites through Google Maps and Claude

## Post

The old playbook: cold calls, generic templates, weeks of follow-up to land one client

The new playbook: you walk in already holding a finished version of what they would buy, branded to them, with a video showing it in action

The prospect isn't deciding whether to start a project. They're deciding whether to buy this specific thing. That's a completely different conversation

 

The stack:

Google Maps - finds local businesses with bad or missing websites

Claude - writes personalized outreach and site briefs in one prompt

Lovable - builds a working landing page mockup in 5 minutes

Higgsfield - generates a 10-second cinematic walkthrough video

 

STEP 1: Find the right leads

Open Google Maps. Pick a niche where the owner isn't technical and the website is critical to revenue

Best niches: roofers, landscapers, plumbers, HVAC, dental practices, salons, law firms, photographers, event venues

Avoid: e-commerce, franchises, anything national, anything where the owner isn't the decision maker

 

Type narrow queries. not "dentists in Austin" but "cosmetic dentists in West Austin." the narrower the search, the better the leads

Skip the top 3-4 results. Those businesses are already winning. The leads you want sit just below them

 

The sweet spot:

Established business, 5+ years on the map under 50 reviews no website or a website built in 2014 solid reviews despite the bad online presence

These are the gold mines. Owners crushing it offline who never fixed their online presence. The gap between what they have and what you can show them is dramatic. That's exactly why the pitch lands

Collect 25-30 leads from one niche in one city. For each one copy: business name, current website, phone number, location, one specific detail you noticed

Then hand the entire list to Claude:

 

STEP 2: Run the list through claude

You now have a structured prospect list with personalized hooks for every business

One prompt, three deliverables per business:

 

Tell Claude explicitly what to avoid. The output should sound like a different person wrote each row

 

STEP 3: Build mockups in lovable

Don't build for all 30 leads. Build for your top 5-8

Pick leads with the most specific diagnoses, highest-rated businesses, most dramatic before/after potential. These are the ones who will reply

 

 

Hit publish. you now have a live URL. Save it

For everyone else on the list the message and mockup-on-request offer is enough

 

STEP 4: Generate demo videos in higgsfield

Static screenshots get ignored. a 10-second video of their site in motion gets replied to

Upload 3-5 screenshots from your Lovable mockup: hero, services, about, social proof, CTA

 

 

Vertical format because most prospects open messages on their phones. Vertical plays inline like content. Horizontal feels like an attachment

 

STEP 5: Send the outreach

the rule that breaks 90% of outreach: never mention AI, Claude, Lovable or any tool you used

the moment you say "I built this with AI" you sound like every other person in their inbox. the prospect cares about results, not your stack

 

Under 70 words. The video sells. The preview link proves you did the work. The soft close removes pressure.

Subject lines that work: "Built something for [business name]," "Quick mockup for [business name]," "Saw your reviews, made you something."

Subject lines that get deleted: "Quick question," "Improving your website," "Free consultation."

Channel by niche:

Email: most niches default

SMS: contractors, trades, plumbers

Instagram DM: salons, restaurants, visual businesses

LinkedIn: law firms, financial services, B2B

Phone calls: contractors, cleaners, anyone older demographic

Follow-ups: if no reply after 4 days, send one. If no reply after another 7 days, send a second from a different angle. Then archive.

For follow-ups, ask Claude:

 

 

What actually closes the deal

The mockup is the icebreaker. the Zoom call is what closes

When a prospect replies positive, get them on a 10-15 minute Zoom. Walk through the mockup with them on screen. ask what they'd change. Take notes. Quote them on the spot

By the time they're on the call they've already imagined themselves with the new site. They're not deciding whether to buy a website they're deciding whether to buy this specific one

Close rate from positive reply: 30-50%

the math:

30 outreach sequences per weekend 10-15% reply rate 3-4 positive replies 1-2 deals closed per weekend

One-time model: $2,500-10,000 cash per weekend monthly model: $2,000-4,000 new MRR every two weekends six months in: $12,000-24,000 monthly recurring

First batch: 4-6 hours for 5 polished mockups and videos once dialed in: 80 minutes for a full 30-prospect sequence

The window is open. The bar is low.
