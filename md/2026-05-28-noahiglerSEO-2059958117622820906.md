# Most home service operators run PPC traffic to their homepage or main service page.

## Post

Most home service operators run PPC traffic to their homepage or main service page.

That's why their cost-per-lead is brutal.

PPC landing pages and SEO landing pages have completely opposite jobs:

> SEO pages need word count, headers, structure, internal links, location signals, FAQs, and enough content for Google to understand what they're about. They're built to rank.

> PPC pages need none of that. The visitor already clicked your ad, so they already know what you do. Their only job is to get the call.

Here's what a high-converting PPC landing page looks like:

> Phone number in 200pt font at the top
> Click-to-call button that's bigger than your logo
> A 6-word headline that matches the ad
> 3 trust signals (reviews count, years in business, license number)
> Zero navigation menu (nothing to click except the phone)
> No footer with 40 links to internal pages
> URL is a random string nobody will ever type or remember

The page exists for one reason: to convert paid traffic into phone calls in under 4 seconds.

Most operators direct their $40 clicks to a homepage with 8 menu items, a hero video, a service grid, an about section, and a contact form 3 scrolls down.

That's a $40 click landing on a page that takes 30 seconds to figure out. By the time they do, they've already bounced to a competitor.
