# Here's the exact local SEO strategy I'd use to help an interior remodeling company show up #1 on Google and close more …

## Post

Here's the exact local SEO strategy I'd use to help an interior remodeling company show up #1 on Google and close more jobs.

(Bookmark this)

Interior remodeling is one of the most interesting niches in home services from an SEO perspective...

The competition is usually weaker than trades like plumbing or HVAC, but the average ticket size is massive. We're talking $8,000 to $800,000+ per job depending on the scope. That means every single lead that comes through Google is worth fighting for.

Here's how I'd approach it from scratch:

1. Understand the competitive landscape

Most interior remodeling companies have been around for 10 to 20 years. They've accumulated 150 to 300 Google reviews over that time just from being in business. Sounds intimidating until you look at their review velocity. They're getting maybe 1 or 2 new reviews per month. They coasted on their reputation and stopped actively asking.

That's your opening.

A company that builds a system to pull in 15 to 20+ reviews a month will close that gap faster than you'd think. Google cares about momentum. A company with 80 reviews getting 20 a month will outrank a company with 250 reviews getting 2 a month in most cases.

2. Nail your Google Business Profile name

This is where a lot of remodeling companies leave money on the table. In a high-ticket industry like this, brand matters 100%. You don't want some generic keyword-stuffed name. BUT you can balance brand identity with a strategic advantage.

Something like "Elevated Interiors - Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling Austin" gives you a real brand name while also including your core service and city. That exact match signal is one of the biggest ranking factors on Google Maps, and in an industry where most competitors just have their brand name with no service keywords... it makes a difference.

3. Build out service pages the right way

Interior remodeling covers a LOT of services. Kitchen remodels, bathroom remodels, whole home renovations, basement finishing, room additions, flooring, cabinetry, etc. Each one of these needs its own dedicated page on your website.

Each page should be built around the keywords people actually search for in your area. "Kitchen remodel cost in Austin" or "bathroom renovation company near me." Include real project photos, details about your process, timeline expectations, and a clear call to action. These pages need to be genuinely useful, not thin pages with 200 words.

4. Create location pages for your target areas

If you serve 10 cities or suburbs within your metro, you need 10 unique location pages. Each one should reference the specific area, mention neighborhoods or landmarks if relevant, and be built around localized search terms.

"Interior remodeling in Round Rock" and "kitchen remodel Georgetown TX" are different searches with different competition levels. You want a page targeting each one.

5. Leverage your project portfolio for SEO

This is where remodeling companies have a HUGE advantage over other home service businesses. You have before and after photos for every good job. You have detailed project descriptions. You have specific materials, timelines, and budgets.

Turn every completed project into a case study page on your site. "Full Kitchen Remodel in Cedar Park - $45,000 Budget - 6 Week Timeline." Include photos, a description of the scope, and what the homeowner wanted to achieve. These pages don't need to rank necessarily, but they build out your site's content, and they give potential customers a reason to trust you before they call.

6. Dominate the review game

In remodeling, reviews carry even more weight than in most trades. A homeowner about to spend $30,000+ on a kitchen remodel is going to read reviews more carefully than someone calling a plumber for a $200 repair. They're looking for details. Quality of work, communication throughout the project, timeline accuracy, budget accuracy.

Encourage your customers to be specific in their reviews. "They completely transformed our kitchen in 5 weeks, stayed on budget, and communicated with us every step of the way" is infinitely more powerful than "great work, highly recommend."

And don't just ask at the end of the project. Ask after major milestones. After the demo is done and they can see the transformation starting. After the install is complete and they're excited. Catch them at the emotional high points.

7. Content that your competitors will never create

Most remodeling companies post nothing on their blog. Maybe a generic article about "2024 kitchen trends" that their agency copied from somewhere.

Here's what actually works. Write about the real questions homeowners ask before they commit to a remodel. "How long does a kitchen remodel actually take?" "Is it worth remodeling before selling your home?" "How to choose a contractor for a whole home renovation." "What's the real cost of a bathroom remodel in [city]?"

Yes, I am usually talking about how blogs are totally overrated and don't move the needle for most trades. But for remodeling, blogs put your company in front of people who are in the research phase. They're not ready to call yet, but when they are... you're the company they've already been learning from.

That, and they let you build topical authority.

8. AI search optimization

This is where it gets interesting for remodeling. When someone asks ChatGPT "what's the best interior remodeling company in Austin," the model is pulling from all of the signals we've already talked about. Reviews, web content, third-party mentions, social presence, branded search volume.

Add FAQ sections to every service page that mirror the exact questions someone would type into ChatGPT. Publish press releases tied to major project completions. Get mentioned in local publications and "best of" lists. Build up a presence on Reddit and Houzz where homeowners are actively researching remodeling companies.

Most remodeling companies aren't even thinking about AI search. That's your window.

9. Speed to lead matters even more in high-ticket

When someone submits a form on your website asking about a $50,000 kitchen remodel, the first company to respond with a thoughtful, personalized message has a massive advantage. Not a generic "thanks for reaching out, we'll get back to you soon."

Set up a system that responds within 60 seconds. An AI agent, a dedicated team member, whatever it takes. In high-ticket remodeling, the close rate difference between responding in 1 minute vs 26 hours is staggering. These homeowners are reaching out to 3 or 4 companies at most. Be the first one to make them feel like you actually care about their project.

10. The long game

Interior remodeling SEO is a long game, but the payoff is massive. One new client from Google could be worth $30,000 to $80,000+ in revenue. If you're ranking in the top 3 for your core services in your area and pulling in even 10 to 15 qualified leads a month... that's a business-changing amount of revenue from a single channel.

The companies that win in this space aren't the ones with the fanciest showroom or the biggest ad budget.

They're the ones that show up first when a homeowner starts researching. And right now, most of your competitors aren't doing any of this...

My favorite industry to work with for a reason.
