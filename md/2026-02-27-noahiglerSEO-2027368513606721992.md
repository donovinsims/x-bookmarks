# Talked to an auto repair shop owner spending $10k/month on direct mail.

## Post

Talked to an auto repair shop owner spending $10k/month on direct mail.

His conversion rates were insane. He wanted to add Google Ads on top of it.

I asked him "What's stopping you from doubling your mailer spend?"

He'd been so focused on finding the next marketing channel that he never thought to just do more of the thing that was already printing money.

Texted me the other day that they've doubled their car count (and revenue) by tripling their mailer spend.
