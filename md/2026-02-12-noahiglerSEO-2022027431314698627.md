# Most home service businesses stop answering the phone at 5pm.

## Post

Most home service businesses stop answering the phone at 5pm.

Their competitors stop at 5pm too.

But a huge chunk of homeowners don't start searching for services until after they get home from work.

Between 6 and 9pm is when people are finally sitting down, noticing the leaky faucet, talking to their spouse about the AC not cooling right, or looking up that roofer their neighbor mentioned at the mailbox earlier.

They pull out their phone and Google it. Your listing pops up, they call... no answer.

They call the next business, no answer.

Third one picks up and books the job.

You spent months building your rankings and that lead went to whoever was willing to answer the phone at 6:30 on a Tuesday.

The fix doesn't have to be complicated. You don't need to be on call 24/7.

Set up a simple answering service that takes the caller's name, number, and what they need.

Some of these cost under $100 a month.

Or use an AI voicemail system that captures the lead info and texts it to you immediately so you can call back first thing in the morning.

Even just calling someone back at 7am the next day puts you ahead of every competitor who never picked up at all.

The businesses I've seen grow the fastest aren't always the ones with the best SEO or the most reviews. They're the ones who actually answer a majority of their phone calls.
