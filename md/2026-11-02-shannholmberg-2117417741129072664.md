# 24 distribution channels based on funnel stage and if you can build, partner, rent or acquire it

## Post

24 distribution channels based on funnel stage and if you can build, partner, rent or acquire it

before they know they need you:
1. founder & team audiences (build, partner)
2. YouTube channels (build, rent, acquire)
3. newsletters & email lists (build, rent, acquire)
4. creators & industry experts (partner, rent)
5. press, podcasts & industry publications (build, partner, rent, acquire)
6. events, webinars & workshops (build, partner, rent)
7. free tools, templates & open-source projects (build, acquire)
8. social, video & offline ads (rent)
9. direct outreach & sales (build)
10. education & certification (build, partner, rent)

while they compare options:
11. your website & search visibility (build, acquire)
12. review & comparison sites (build, rent)
13. marketplaces & app stores (build)
14. launch & discovery platforms (build, rent, acquire)
15. search & AI assistant ads (rent)

through tools and people they trust:
16. integrations & technology partners (build, partner)
17. AI agent surfaces (build, partner)
18. agencies, consultants & resellers (partner)
19. affiliates & referral partners (partner)
20. communities (build, partner, acquire)
21. businesses with an existing customer base (acquire)
22. bundles, pre-installs & default placement (partner, rent)

after they buy:
23. distribution built into the product (build)
24. customer referrals & word of mouth (build)
