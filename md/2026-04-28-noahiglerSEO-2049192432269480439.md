# The most underused section in Google Business Profile is Products.

## Post

The most underused section in Google Business Profile is Products.
 
98% of home service GBPs don't have it set up.
 
The ones who fill it in correctly can outrank competitors with more reviews and bigger backlink profiles.
 
Here's how it works.
 
You'll find Products in your GBP dashboard under Edit Profile. It's been there for years, but most owners scroll past it because they assume it's only for product-based businesses.
 
Categories handle the broad classification. Services tell Google what you do. The Products section adds something the first two don't: actual deliverables you sell, with photos, descriptions, and pricing. It's a separate signal and structured data that LLMs pull from.
 
Here's what I'd add for a home service business.
 
> A separate product entry for every main service, even if you've already added it under Services. Drain cleaning, water heater repair, sewer line replacement, etc.
> A custom photo for each product, taken from real jobs.
> A 2-3 sentence description per product written naturally for the keyword. "Drain cleaning in [city]" should appear in the description, not stuffed in 4 times.
> A price range or starting price where you can. Things like "From $150" or "Starting at $89" work fine. Don't put fake prices, just give Google a number.
> Update the products quarterly with fresh photos and refreshed descriptions.
 
What this signals to Google is that you're an active, organized, service-rich operation. A lot of competitors look like ghosts in this section, which means filling it in correctly puts you ahead before anyone else even shows up.
 
Common mistakes I see when owners do try to use it:
 
> Generic stock photos that match nothing on the site. Google cross-references images and stock photos hurt more than they help.
> Adding 2-3 services and stopping. The full picture of what you sell needs to be there.
> Writing descriptions like ad copy. Google's reading for relevance signals, not conversion language, so save that for the website.
> Letting it go stale for years. Like everything else in GBP, recency matters.
 
This isn't a magic ranking lever on its own. Stacked on top of properly built categories, services, photos, and reviews, it's the kind of detail that closes a 1-2 spot gap in the Map Pack.
 
If you've never opened your Products section, do it this week.

Takes about an hour, costs nothing, and most of your competitors have left the section completely empty.
