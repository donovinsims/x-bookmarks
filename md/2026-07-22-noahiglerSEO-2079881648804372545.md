## Post

Local Service Ads are some of the cheapest leads in home services, and there's a ton of confusion around getting the most out of them.

Only TWO things decide your rank.

Here's what they are, plus exactly what to do when leads run slow or too hot.

---

THE TWO RANK LEVERS

> Review velocity. LSA reads the pace of your reviews harder than the total. A company getting 5 new reviews a week outranks one sitting on 400 old ones. Steady weekly reviews are the single biggest input you control.

> Answer rate. Google tracks how many LSA calls you pick up, and it buries profiles that miss them. Aim for 98%+. If you can't cover nights and weekends, tighten your ad hours instead of eating missed calls, because every missed call teaches Google not to send you the next one.

---

WHAT DOESN'T DECIDE YOUR RANK

> Your budget. It caps how many leads you can receive. It doesn't earn you a better spot. Raising the budget on a profile with weak reviews and missed calls just raises the ceiling on leads that were never coming.

> Profile polish. The photos and bio matter for the customer's click, not for your rank. Worth an hour, not a weekend.

---

IF LEADS ARE SLOW

> Check your answer rate first. Missed calls quietly kill more LSA accounts than anything else on this list.

> Dispute every junk lead. Wrong service, wrong area, spam. Disputes get you credits and clean up the data Google uses to pick your next lead.

> Push a review sprint. Ten fresh reviews in two weeks lifts LSA rank faster than any other input you have.

> Widen your job types and service areas one notch at a time. Slow accounts are often just aimed too narrow.

---

IF LEADS ARE COMING TOO FAST

This is where owners torch their own account.

Do not pause it. When you pause, Google's system goes cold on you. Turn it back on and you enter a relearning phase where leads trickle in while the system rebuilds trust, and you can lose weeks of momentum to save a few days of budget.

Instead:

> Drop the weekly budget and keep it live. A low-budget account that stays on keeps its history and its rank.

> Turn off your lowest-value job types and let the profitable ones keep running.

> Shrink the service area down to your best neighborhoods until the schedule opens up.

The machine stays warm, the rank holds, and you scale the budget back up the day you have room on the calendar.

LSA rewards the shop that answers the phone and stacks reviews, which is why it's the one channel where the best-run company usually wins without the biggest budget.
