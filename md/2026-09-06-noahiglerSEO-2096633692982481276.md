# The last 60 days have been the most stressful but valuable period in my business

## Post

The last 60 days have been the most stressful but valuable period in my business

I felt completely unprepared for what went on these past 2 months

But I guess that's the beauty of business. You genuinely can't predict what will come

You don't know what you don't know

If you ride it out, you become the man who can handle those problems in the future

> Moved into a NYC apartment
> Employee fires and hires. Team restructuring
> Began shifting our service towards the future of search
> Working with larger clients than ever (took on a massive HVAC platform)
> Hit 50k views on a YT video
> Invested in a consultant/mentor

> Took on a fractional CTO to help build out our agentic backend

> Went to Vermont. Brought home 10 lbs of ribeyes from a farm
> Hired a new killer client-facing role

Enjoying the journey
