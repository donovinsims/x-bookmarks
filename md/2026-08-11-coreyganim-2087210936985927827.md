# if someone books a call about your AI service, do not wait silently for the meeting.

## Post

if someone books a call about your AI service, do not wait silently for the meeting.

send a short pre-call sequence that:

one: reframes the business problem

two: explains your assessment or delivery process

three: answers “why can't I just use ChatGPT?”

four: shows a relevant client example

five: tells them how to prepare

the sales call should not be the first time the prospect understands what you sell.

use the gap before the call to build clarity, not pressure.

if you do this right, many clients will show up to the call pre-sold.
