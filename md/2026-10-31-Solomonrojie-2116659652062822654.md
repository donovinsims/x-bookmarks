2. connect TradingView through its supported MCP tools. verify access to the screener, watchlists, and available market information before scheduling anything

9. connect an Alpaca paper account through the execution service. the Executor submits permitted orders, the Reviewer checks fills and outcomes, and the Desk Lead tracks unfinished work
