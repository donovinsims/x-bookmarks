## Post

Go to "System Settings" > "General" > "Storage" and check for the files taking up the most space. Move the files you wish to keep to an external hard drive, then empty the "Trash" (after verifying its contents). You can perform these steps without needing to install any additional software. Alternatively, you can use OpenNavo to clean up your computer—it is completely free and open-source. https://opennavo.com
