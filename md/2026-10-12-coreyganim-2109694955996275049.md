# Everyday people are making a KILLING as AI consultants.

## Post

Everyday people are making a KILLING as AI consultants.

Tips on how to take advantage of this gold rush:

-sell OUTCOMES, not AI
-the closer you can get to revenue, the more you can charge
-making a client more money will always be more valuable than saving them time
-sell a paid assessment first to understand a prospect's gaps. Then upsell implementation
-a huge number of business owners want to learn HOW to build with AI, not just have someone build things for them
-you can monetize via paid workshops, traditional consulting, direct implementation, managed agents, paid assessments, and tons of other models
-start by texting business owners you already know. Offer them a free mini-assessment, then upsell from there
-sell to rich people. Big companies need AI more than smaller ones, and their budgets are much bigger

There has never been a better time to be in the AI consulting game, there has never been more opportunity, and demand is virtually limitless.

Don't let anyone tell you otherwise.
