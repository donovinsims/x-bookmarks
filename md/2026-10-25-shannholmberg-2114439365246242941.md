# the future way of operating SaaS will be to sell outcomes

## Post

the future way of operating SaaS will be to sell outcomes

instead of letting users do the work themselves, you package your product in two ways

1. self-serve

the user runs your product themselves, in your app or with their own agent connected to your API

they own the result, and they pay for access and usage

2. outcome the user tells you the result they want, and you deliver it. behind the platform, a marketing engineer owns the work for that client and orchestrates the agents that produce the outcome. they pay a higher price, because someone is accountable for the result.

for an SEO and AI search tool, it could look like this:

self-serve
> the user signs up, tests the keyword research and gets an API key
> they give the key to their own agent with a task, like "get us ranking for these 10 keywords"
> the agent pulls the data through your API and writes the content itself
> priced by usage

outcome
> the user asks for a result, like "[N] articles in the top [X] for [topic] in [Y] days"
> a marketing engineer on your side runs your platform and agents to deliver it
> priced per result, at a premium over the platform alone

software gives the user the tools, and a marketing engineer with agents gives them the outcome (with the experienced from succesful outcomes prio)
