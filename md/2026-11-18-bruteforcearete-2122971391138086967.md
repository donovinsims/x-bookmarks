Layer 1: Three Weekend Utility Ideas & Their Verified Revenue Math (50%)

1. The "Meeting & Sales-Call Tab Highlighter + Instant CRM Note" Chrome Extension ($10,400/mo Model)

The Problem: Account executives, recruiters, and freelance consultants take rough bullet notes in Google Docs or Apple Notes during calls, and then waste twenty minutes cleaning them up into structured summaries and follow-up emails afterward.

The Business Model & Revenue Breakdown:
A Manifest V3 Chrome extension side-panel that turns messy bullet fragments typed during a call into a structured 3-part recap (Key Pain Points, Agreed Next Steps, Draft Follow-Up Email) in one click.
Revenue Math: Priced at $15/month (Individual) or $119/year. Reaching 695 active professionals at $15/month clears $10,425 MRR at a 96% profit margin because text processing costs fractions of a penny per call.

2. The "Screen-Time & Dopamine Friction Gate" Native Mac Menu-Bar App ($9,800/mo Model)

The Problem: Remote workers, writers, and software developers lose two hours every afternoon reflexively opening X, Reddit, YouTube, or news sites whenever they hit a tough problem, and standard website blockers feel too rigid when they actually need to look up a coding answer on Reddit.

The Business Model & Revenue Breakdown:
A native macOS utility (built with SwiftUI) that intercepts distracting domains with a 10-second breathing countdown and a 1-line intent prompt ("What specific answer are you looking for?"), automatically closing the tab after 5 minutes unless extended.
Revenue Math: Priced at $19 One-Time or $29/year Pro (with focus-streak analytics and calendar sync). Selling 420 licenses/subscriptions a month via Mac App Store search and productivity communities generates $9,870/month with $0.00 in server bills (100% gross margin).

3. The "Used-Car VIN, Service History & Fair-Price Negotiator" iOS & Android App ($11,100/mo Model)

The Problem: Anyone shopping for a used car at a dealership or on Facebook Marketplace feels anxious about overpaying or missing known mechanical defects for that exact model year.

The Business Model & Revenue Breakdown:
A single-thumb iOS and Android camera utility (built with Expo + RevenueCat) where buyers scan the windshield VIN barcode to instantly pull known model-year reliability recalls, fair private-party vs. dealer pricing bands, and a 3-bullet dealership negotiation script.
Revenue Math: Priced as a $7.99/week car-shopping pass or $29.99/year. Converting 1,390 active car shoppers a month at an $8 blended transaction value generates $11,120/month at an 89% gross margin.

Layer 2: Practical Tutorial How to Build the "Native Mac Intent-Gate & Auto-Close Tab Guard" in 48 Hours (30%)

You can build Utility #2 over a single weekend using SwiftUI and macOS AppleScript / browser extension messaging with zero cloud dependencies:

Step 1 — Lightweight Browser URL Interceptor (declarativeNetRequest / tabs.onUpdated):Pair your Mac menu-bar app with a tiny companion extension listening to chrome.tabs.onUpdated. Whenever changeInfo.url matches a user's distraction list (x.com, reddit.com, youtube.com), redirect the tab locally to chrome-extension://[id]/pause.html?target={encodedUrl}.

Step 2 — The 10-Second "Intent Prompt" & Countdown Timer (pause.js):Render a calm, minimalist dark-mode screen requiring a 10-second pause and a 5-word intent entry ("Checking Supabase auth thread") plus a time-box selector (3 mins, 5 mins, 10 mins).

Step 3 — Automatic Tab-Closing Alarm (chrome.alarms):When the user clicks "Start 5-Minute Session," create a chrome.alarms.create('close_' + tabId, { delayInMinutes: 5 }). When the alarm fires, automatically redirect the tab back to a clean "Session Complete Back to Deep Work" screen so a quick lookup never turns into a 45-minute scroll spiral.

Step 4 — Local Focus-Minutes Telemetry (NSUserDefaults / SQLite):Sync daily distraction-deflection counts to the native macOS menu-bar widget so the user sees "🛡️ 14 Doomscrolls Blocked Today (1h 10m Saved)" right next to their Mac clock.

Layer 3: Marketing Psychology & Growth Experiments: Store Search & The "First-Deflection" Hook (20%)

Apps that protect a user's time or money convert fastest when the user sees the exact dollar or minute value saved right inside the menu bar or home-screen widget.

Once your weekend utility is shipped, apply these two behavioral triggers to convert passive installers into paying customers:

The "Exact-Symptom" Store Title Formula (ASO & Chrome Web Store):Buyers searching the App Store or Chrome Web Store never type abstract brand names; they search their immediate need. Title your listing "Used Car VIN Scanner & Price Check" or "Distraction Blocker & 5-Minute Tab Timer" so your primary keywords sit inside the first 30 characters—where store ranking algorithms place maximum search weight.

The "Free 3-Day Deep-Work Challenge" Onboarding Loop:Let new installers use the Mac Intent-Gate or Chrome Call-Note Helper completely free for their first 10 sessions with zero login required. Once they see a concrete report showing "You saved 2 hours and 15 minutes of deep work over the last 3 days," locking unlimited daily domains behind a $19 one-time or $29/year unlock converts at 18%–25%.
