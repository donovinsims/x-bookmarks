# I've generated over 32M impressions on X.

## Post

I've generated over 32M impressions on X.

I've helped an account with 0 followers hit 1.5M impressions and 1.5K followers in 30 days.

I've helped an account with 300 followers get 800K impressions and 1,000 followers in 1 week.

Here's everything I know about X growth:

profile

show your face. faceless is hard mode

bio says what you do & who it's for

pin your best result or your story, whatever makes a stranger hit follow

what to post

the idea goes viral, not the format. figure out why people shared a tweet before you copy it

when something works post it again with a new angle. the biggest accounts on here run the same winner 3-4 times

under 5k followers keep most posts broad. you need reach before you can sell to it

1 article a week then QRT it a couple days later. one of our client QRTs did ~400K views, more than the article itself

what the algo rewards (straight from X's code)

a reply is worth almost 8 likes & a copied link 30

posts die after 48 hours

your 2nd post in someone's feed gets scored lower so space them out

what actually makes money

track profile visits, not just views. a profile visit is someone deciding if they trust you

1 case study in front of the right people books more calls than a viral post in front of the wrong ones

bookmark this. you'll need it around post 30 when nothing's working
