# If I was starting a home service business from scratch, here's exactly what I'd prioritize in the first 90 days.

## Post

If I was starting a home service business from scratch, here's exactly what I'd prioritize in the first 90 days.

(Bookmark this)

Reviews:
> Ask every single customer in person before you leave the job site
> Hand them a QR code that links directly to your Google review page
> Set up automated email and SMS follow-ups for anyone who didn't leave one
> Call happy customers yourself as the owner and ask directly (this converts at like 90%)
> Respond to every review within 24 hours, good or bad
> Aim for 10-20 reviews in your first 90 days minimum

Website:
> Use a template site that converts, doesn't need to be custom
> Add real photos from your actual jobs, not stock images
> Embed your Google reviews on the homepage
> Put a massive click-to-call button above the fold
> Add your service and city in the main headline
> Build out individual pages for each core service you offer
> Build out location pages for each city you want to rank in
> Make sure it loads fast on mobile

Google Business Profile:
> Claim and verify your GBP immediately
> Physical location profile, NOT service area
> Exact match business name (use a DBA)
> Choose the right primary category for your main service
> Add every service you offer with keyword-rich descriptions
> Upload 10-15 real job photos before you even launch
> Set your service area to a realistic radius
> Post a photo or update once a week to show activity

Citations:
> Sign up for Yext or a similar service to keep your listings synced
> Build a new batch of 20-30 citations every month or two
> Make sure your name, address, and phone number match exactly everywhere
