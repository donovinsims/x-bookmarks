# Every home service business I talk to asks me the same question on the first call.

## Post

Every home service business I talk to asks me the same question on the first call.

How long until this starts working?

I've heard of other agencies giving some pretty ridiculous answers.

Local SEO doesn't have a single turn-on date. Different parts of your ranking system move on completely different timelines, and most contractors get frustrated because they're expecting a linear result from a compounding system.

Across a typical 12-month engagement, this is how it plays out:

Months 1-2:
> Quick wins on your Google Business Profile if there's low-hanging fruit (wrong category, no target keywords in the business name, thin photo library)
> Map Pack position can jump inside 30 days in less competitive markets
> First batch of quality backlinks gets indexed

Months 3-6:
> Service and city pages start ranking in organic search
> Map Pack coverage expands beyond your immediate neighborhood
> Lead flow from Google typically doubles if the website is converting well

Months 6-12:
> Metro-wide Map Pack dominance for your main keywords (depending on how many GBPs you have//how large your metro is)
> AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) starts pulling your business consistently
> Competitors are now months behind on content and links you built earlier in the engagement

Month 12+:

The moat kicks in. New competitors can't out-rank you without outspending you heavily, and most of them won't.

If an agency tells you they'll get you top 3 in 60 days, either they're operating in a market with almost zero competition or they're planning to hit your profile with spam tactics that get it suspended by month 4.

Local SEO compounds.

The work we do in month 1 is still paying you in month 18. You just have to commit to it long enough for the curve to kick in.
