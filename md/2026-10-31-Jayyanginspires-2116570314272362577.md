# It hurt my soul to release this article.

## Post

It hurt my soul to release this article.

But in 2026, information is commoditized. Anyone can just get a ChatGPT summary or use the internet to find the best pieces of advice.

If information is commoditized, then the scarce resource is not attention, but trust. If we know that trust is the valuable resource, then it makes sense to give away as much as possible upfront to convince people, before they buy from you, that you are the right person to trust with the implementation.

The content that reaches the most people, converts the most people, and gains the most trust is always going to be the content that hurts your soul a little bit.
