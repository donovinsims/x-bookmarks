# This free AI agent builds a cold email offer for people who know how to do the work, but don't know how to sell it:

## Post

This free AI agent builds a cold email offer for people who know how to do the work, but don't know how to sell it:

- explains the industry in simple words
- finds the biggest problem you can solve
- shows how much that problem costs
- tells you who has the problem
- tells you exactly who to email
- picks the right company size to target
- turns what you do into a result people want to buy
- gives you a clear one-line offer
- builds the job-title list + search you need to find leads
- gives the next AI agent everything it needs to write the email

It also saves what it learns on your computer, so every new offer gets better.

The skill itself is 100% free + open source.
