# HOLY F*CK. you're still building AI agents from scratch while an entire army of ready-made bots is sitting on GitHub fo…

## Post

HOLY F*CK. you're still building AI agents from scratch while an entire army of ready-made bots is sitting on GitHub for free.

I found 20 GitHub repos that can save you weeks of building, configuring, and connecting your own Grok Bot team.

We're talking about ready-made agents for coding, research, sales, trading, marketing, operations, and even entire multi-agent companies.

And most people are completely sleeping on this.

Here are 20 projects worth bookmarking:

01. grok-skills
195 reusable playbooks for coding, debugging, research, writing, planning, and shipping.
https://github.com/jaskirat1616/grok-skills

02. botteams
Create named bots, group chats, and team routines using a simple installer prompt.
https://github.com/ellelion/botteams

03. grok-bot-templates
49 templates, 10 teams, 47 skills, and 8 routines. Ready-made roles with clear deliverables and boundaries.
https://github.com/cobusgreyling/grok-bot-templates

04. hypergrok-trading-desk
7 specialist bots and 17 skills for market research, strategy testing, risk, execution, and review.
https://github.com/galleonlabs/hypergrok-trading-desk

05. awesome-grokbot
A searchable collection of public Grok Bot configurations organized by job.
https://github.com/kydlikebtc/awesome-grokbot

06. grok-bot-profiles
Includes Spec Writer, Bug Reproducer, PR Producer, and a separate PR Verifier.
https://github.com/HAEGONG/grok-bot-profiles

07. root-agent-skill-framework
Builds specialist agents around specific tasks and delegates daily management to team leads.
https://github.com/MrBekoX/root-agent-skill-framework

08. grok-research
Persistent equity researchers, scout reports, reviewed views, and a shared research database.
https://github.com/jaredtrichard/grok-research

09. rosterroom
82 ready-to-use team setups for founders, operations, security, finance, and research.
https://github.com/codejunkie99/rosterroom

10. grokbot-for-gtm
Connects prospect research, email, LinkedIn outreach, replies, and meeting tracking.
https://github.com/bcharleson/grokbot-for-gtm

11. grok-bot-token-saver
Replaces huge conversation histories with compact role cards and handoffs between sessions.
https://github.com/Chakhdz/grok-bot-token-saver

12. gojiberryai-sales-os

https://github.com/romangojiberryAI/gojiberryai-sales-os

13. crew-contract
Gives every agent a mission, owner, evidence-based handoff, completion check, and stopping rule.
https://github.com/lsj210001/crew-contract

14. awesome-grokbot by mergisi
Ready-made engineering, sales, marketing, customer success, operations, and personal teams.
https://github.com/mergisi/awesome-grokbot

15. botskills

16. grok-bot-cli

https://github.com/ScriptedAlchemy/grok-bot-cli

Lets a coding agent create, message, and manage Grok Bot teammates.
https://github.com/adamanz/grok-bot-skill

https://github.com/majiayu000/awesome-grok-bot

A self-hosted alternative with named teammates, individual tools, instructions, and persistent memory.
https://github.com/opencoredev/akeru-bot

20. guaca
A desktop workspace where agents share conversations, delegate tasks, and work with MCP tools.
https://github.com/madebywelch/guaca

But here's what most people get wrong:

Having 20 agents doesn't mean you have a working AI company.

You need a system:
→ 1 coordinator to break down objectives
→ Specialist agents to execute tasks in parallel
→ Shared handoffs so work doesn't restart from zero
→ Independent verification before results are accepted
→ Clear stopping rules to prevent endless retry loops

That's how you turn a folder full of bots into an actual AI workforce.
The repositories are free. Running them may still require an AI subscription, API usage, integrations, or hosting.
Start with one workflow. Make it work. Then scale.

Bookmark this before you waste another week building something that already exists.
