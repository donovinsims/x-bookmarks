# If you're building a startup here are the 10 best GitHub repos that can save you dozens of hours and thousands of dolla…

## Post

If you're building a startup here are the 10 best GitHub repos that can save you dozens of hours and thousands of dollars:

1. gstack by Garry Tran

Use Garry Tan’s Claude Code setup: 23 tools for CEO, Design, Eng, Release, Docs, and QA.

https://github.com/garrytan/gstack

2. Last 30 Days

Researches any topic across Reddit, X, and other social media before you build.

https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill

3. Marketing skills for AI agents

CRO, copywriting, SEO, analytics, and growth engineering.

https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills

4. Novu

One system to send and manage all your app's user notifications across email, SMS, and push.

https://github.com/novuhq/novu

5. Papermark

See exactly who opened your deck, which pages they read, and what they skipped.
https://github.com/papermark/papermark

6. OpenReplay

Self-hosted session replay to see how users use your product and where they drop off.

https://github.com/openreplay/openreplay

7. shadcn/ui

Beautifully-designed UI components you copy into your app and own.

https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui

8. Cap Software

Make polished launch and product demo videos.
https://github.com/CapSoftware/cap

9. Dify

Open-source platform for building and running AI agents and workflows.

https://github.com/langgenius/dify

10. Devopness

One platform to replace many tools: Terraform + Ansible + Jenkins + PaaS (Vercel, Heroku, etc), and stack-specific server panels.

https://github.com/devopness/devopness
