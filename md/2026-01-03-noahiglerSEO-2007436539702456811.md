# A lead comes in at 2:14pm.

## Post

A lead comes in at 2:14pm.

You call them back at 4:30pm.

They already hired someone else.

This happens way more than you think.

Speed to lead is the most underrated factor in closing jobs. Not your reviews, pricing, or website.

How fast you pick up the phone is the single biggest factor when it comes to collecting cash.

The data on this is honestly brutal.

The first business to respond wins the job 78% of the time.

After 5 minutes, your odds of qualifying that lead drop by 80%.

After 30 minutes, most people have already called someone else.

Home service customers aren't always price shopping.

They have a broken AC in August or a garage door that won't open.

They're calling the first 2-3 businesses they find on Google and whoever picks up gets the job.

I see this constantly with clients.

They spend $4k/month on marketing:
> Their GBP is dialed
> Website converts well
> Reviews are great

Then I look at their call data.

35% of calls going to voicemail. Average callback time of 2 hours.

Leads from the website form sitting untouched for a full two days.

They're paying to generate leads and then giving them away to competitors.

Here's the fix:

1. Answer every call live during business hours.

If you can't do this yourself, hire a virtual receptionist or use an AI answering service. $200-400/month to make sure every call gets answered.

2. Respond to form submissions within 15 minutes. Set up text alerts so you see them immediately. A quick "Got your message, calling you in 5 minutes" text buys you time if you're on another job.

3. Call back missed calls within 10 minutes.

I recommend tracking your answer rate and response times.

CallRail or a similar tool shows you exactly how many calls you're missing and how long callbacks take.

Your SEO doesn't matter if you don't answer the phone.

Your ads don't matter if leads wait 3 hours for a callback.

Speed to lead is the easiest competitive advantage in home services (and honestly any business).
