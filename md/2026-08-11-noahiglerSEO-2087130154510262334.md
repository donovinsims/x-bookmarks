# This Google Ads trick literally tripled ROAS on a $35k/month HVAC campaign we manage.

## Post

This Google Ads trick literally tripled ROAS on a $35k/month HVAC campaign we manage.

Went from ~3.5x to 10.5x ROAS within a few months.

We taught Google which calls and forms were real opportunities instead of letting every lead count the same.

The HVAC company's office qualifies every lead, not us.

Every new call and form goes to the HVAC company's office team. They know the service area, the jobs they want, and whether the person actually has buying intent.

Within two hours, the office marks each lead as:

> Qualified

> Not qualified

> Booked

> Sold

They also add the service, ZIP code, and revenue once the job closes.

That information comes back to us, and we upload the qualified leads and sold jobs into Google Ads as offline conversions.

The setup needs four pieces:

> Capture the Google click ID or the customer's first-party data when the call or form comes in.

> Keep one lead ID through the CRM so the same job can't be uploaded twice.

> Create separate conversion actions for Qualified Lead and Sold Job.

> Upload the office's answers every day so Google gets fast feedback.

Don't make Qualified Lead the primary conversion action on day one.

Raw calls and forms stay primary while the account collects clean offline data. Qualified Lead stays secondary so we can confirm the uploads match correctly.

Once the account has around 30 qualified leads from the past 2 to 3 months, Qualified Lead becomes the primary conversion action. Raw calls and forms move to secondary.

Now Google can see which searches, ZIP codes, devices, and times of day produce real HVAC opportunities.

It stops treating a job seeker, an out-of-area caller, and a $15k replacement lead as if they're worth the same amount.

The ad platform gets smarter because the HVAC office tells us what happened within two hours, and we send that answer back to Google.
