# Here is the exact sitemap I would build for a plumbing and HVAC company that wants to dominate its market.

## Post

Here is the exact sitemap I would build for a plumbing and HVAC company that wants to dominate its market.

A homepage and one page listing every service will not cover all the searches a plumbing and HVAC company wants to rank for.

I would build the site like this.

Core pages:

> Homepage
> About Us
> Our Team
> Reviews
> Financing
> Maintenance Plan
> Specials
> Careers
> Contact

The homepage should explain that the company handles both plumbing and HVAC, show the primary service area, include strong reviews, and make the call or booking action obvious.

Then I would create a main plumbing page with an individual page for every important plumbing service:

> Emergency plumbing
> Drain cleaning
> Sewer line repair
> Sewer camera inspections
> Water heater repair
> Water heater installation
> Tankless water heaters
> Leak detection
> Repiping
> Sump pumps
> Gas line repair
> Fixture installation

The HVAC side gets its own main page and separate pages for the services people search for:

> Emergency HVAC service
> AC repair
> AC installation
> AC maintenance
> Furnace repair
> Furnace installation
> HVAC maintenance
> Heat pumps
> Ductless mini-splits
> Indoor air quality
> Ductwork

Each page should focus on one service. AC repair and AC installation belong on different pages because the customer needs different information and the jobs have different values.

Next comes the Service Areas section.

Create one main Service Areas page that links to a dedicated page for each priority market the company serves.

Every market page should include:

> The plumbing and HVAC services available there
> Real jobs completed in that area
> Reviews from nearby customers
> Photos from those projects
> Information specific to the homes, equipment, or common problems in that market
> Links back to the relevant service pages

For the largest and most valuable markets, I would also build pages for the strongest service and market combinations.

Examples:

> /service-areas/[market]/drain-cleaning/
> /service-areas/[market]/water-heater-repair/
> /service-areas/[market]/ac-repair/
> /service-areas/[market]/furnace-repair/

Do not build every possible combination. Start with the services that produce the most revenue in the markets where the company has real demand, reviews, photos, and completed jobs.

I would also add a Projects section.

Every strong project page should cover one real job, including the service, market, photos, equipment used, problem found, and work completed. That page links back to both the main service page and the relevant market page.

The Resources section comes last. Publish answers to questions customers ask while deciding who to hire, then link those articles to the service pages that generate revenue.

The main navigation stays simple:

> Home
> Plumbing
> HVAC
> Service Areas
> Reviews
> About Us
> Contact
> One clear call or booking button

The homepage links to the highest-value services and markets. Service pages link to relevant market and project pages. Market pages link back to the services available in that area.

Every important page should be reachable within 3 clicks from the homepage.

The website pages rank in the organic results. Each legitimate Google Business Profile ranks in Maps. Location pages help the website cover more searches, but they do not remove the proximity limits of a Business Profile.
