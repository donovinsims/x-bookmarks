# This HVAC owner called me 11 months ago ready to shut off Google Ads.

## Post

This HVAC owner called me 11 months ago ready to shut off Google Ads.

He was paying more than $600 per lead, and two more bad quarters would've killed the channel.

We pulled the last 90 days this week. $1.27M in revenue from search and paid.

We rebuilt the website, split replacement campaigns from low-ticket service, and stopped optimizing for the cheapest lead. Paid kept the schedule moving while SEO grew underneath it.

The same channel he was ready to shut off is now part of a search system producing seven figures in 90 days.

On last month's call he said they hired 5 new techs last quarter.
