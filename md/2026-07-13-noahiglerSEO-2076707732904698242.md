# Met a home service owner in a town of 70,000 people.

## Post

Met a home service owner in a town of 70,000 people.

He turns almost 80% of his jobs into Google reviews.

That habit is his entire marketing strategy.

He has over five times the reviews of anyone else in his market, ranks first for almost every term a customer could type, and gets basically all the calls in town.

The method could not be more old school. A few days after a job wraps, he drives back to the house, knocks on the door, and asks the homeowner how everything is holding up. Then he asks for the review while he's standing on the porch.

People almost never say no to the guy who came back to check on his own work.

Most companies pull a review from 1 in 6 jobs. He pulls 4 in 5, and he dominates literally every search.
