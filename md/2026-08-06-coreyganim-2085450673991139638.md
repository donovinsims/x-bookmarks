# Most people try to sell the $10,000 AI build first.

## Post

Most people try to sell the $10,000 AI build first.

Terrible idea.

Start with a $999 assessment.

Use the diagnosis to uncover the right next project, then move into $1K–$10K+ implementation work.

The assessment is the front door.
