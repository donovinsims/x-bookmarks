# How to rank #1 on Google Maps (simplified):

## Post

How to rank #1 on Google Maps (simplified):

> Get a physical location in the center of your target market
> File a DBA so your business name matches what people actually search
> Choose the right primary category for your highest volume service
> Add every service you offer with keyword rich descriptions
> Upload real job photos at least twice a month
> Fill out every single field on your GBP
> Double your review velocity, last 90 days matter more than total count
> Ask for reviews after inspections and estimates, not just completed jobs
> Train your team to ask in person with a QR code card
> Automate text follow up at 12 hours and email at 24 hours
> Respond to every review within 24 hours with your service and city mentioned naturally
> Link your GBP to a city specific landing page, not your homepage
> Build a dedicated page for every service you offer
> Build a dedicated page for every city you want to rank in
> Run a content gap analysis on whoever is ranking top 3 and make your pages better
> Internal link everything, service pages to location pages and back
> Build 80 to 120 citations with identical NAP everywhere
> Start with Yelp, BBB, Angi, Apple Maps, then add local directories
> Post weekly on your GBP with links to your service and location pages
> Use blog posts to internally link back to your money pages, not as lead gen
> Mobile load time under 3 seconds
> Embed Google Maps driving directions on location pages
> Get backlinks from local businesses you actually have relationships with
> Publish press releases monthly for backlinks and AI search visibility
