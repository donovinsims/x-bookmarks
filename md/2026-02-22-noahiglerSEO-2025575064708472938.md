# Here's 9 things I've noticed that separate service businesses doing $4M+ from the ones stuck under $1M.

## Post

Here's 9 things I've noticed that separate service businesses doing $4M+ from the ones stuck under $1M.

1). They don't depend on one lead source. Referrals built the business but they've added SEO, ads, or both so they're never at the mercy of word of mouth slowing down.

2). They answer the phone. Sounds obvious but the businesses under $1M are missing 20 to 30% of their inbound calls. The bigger companies usually all have well-trained CSRs that pick up 99% of calls and reach out to email and text leads within 5 to 10 minutes. Some are starting to use AI solutions for this too.

3). They treat their Google Business Profile like a sales asset. Updated photos, weekly posts, every field filled out, reviews coming in consistently, strong synergy from the website, and strong off-profile SEO (citations, PR, etc.).

4). They have actual systems for getting reviews. Not "we ask sometimes." A real process with QR cards, text follow ups, and someone on the team accountable for it. Most I've spoken with incentivize their techs to get reviews, whether it's a dollar amount per review or bonuses based on hitting a certain number each month.

5). They track where their leads come from. They know their cost per lead by channel, their close rate, and which marketing dollars are actually producing jobs.

6). They prioritize conversion rate over a pretty modern website design. At the end of the day they just want to convert as many leads as possible.

7). They hire for the role they need, not the role they can afford. The $1M businesses try to do everything themselves. The $4M+ ones hired an office manager, a dedicated dispatcher, or a marketing person way earlier than felt comfortable.

8). They think in terms of ROAS, not cost per lead. Factor in total returns and ROI including upsells.

9). They're not afraid to spend money in months where the ROI isn't immediate. They understand that SEO, brand building, and content take time, and content take time to compound.
