Content Architecture & Strategy

Before you post on social channels, you need to "craft" your brand. This is Phase 1 of setting yourself up for success.

Honestly I see so many people get this wrong, and it's why they post for months with zero results.

Market Research

Good market research answers four questions:

How to do market research:

1. Study the top 10 to 20 accounts in your niche. Look for commonalities across multiple social channels (YouTube, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, etc.) - your goal is to uncover the core principles/angles/messaging that work in your niche.

2. Read the replies. This is where your audience tells you exactly what they want.

3. Look at adjacent niches. Look at other niches and pull ideas into yours that people aren't yet executing.

4. Identify the gaps. What questions keep coming up without good answers? What's everyone doing the same way? What would you do differently?

5. Keep a swipe file. Start building a resource you can refer to that contains good content practices.

Note: Content is dynamic; pivot your brand based on what performs well and market demand.

Video vs. Written Media

At this stage, you've crafted a brand and understand your content pillars, but you still need mediums to express your brand.

There are two:

Video media

Written media

Each one breaks down into short-form and long-form content.

Video: Long-form (YouTube, 𝕏, podcasts)

• Best for: deep trust, authority

• Funnel: TOF / MOF / BOF

• Effort: Usually high

Video: Short-form (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

• Best for: raw reach and discovery

• Funnel: Usually TOF

• Effort: Low-medium

Written: Long-form (𝕏 articles, newsletters, Substack)

• Best for: depth, authority, reaching unique audiences

• Funnel: TOF / MOF

• Effort: Medium

Written: Short-form (𝕏 posts, threads, LinkedIn)

• Best for: testing ideas fast, daily presence

• Funnel: TOF / MOF

• Effort: Low

Mix and match strategy

I like the mix-and-match strategy. Your content mix should check off two things:

1. Every stage of the funnel (TOF/MOF/BOF)

2. Both mediums and both lengths

• Video + written, so you reach your audience however they like to consume

• Long-form + short-form, so you get discovered AND trusted

For example

You don't need all four at once. In fact, you can use the same social channel to meet all these requirements.

For example, I only post on 𝕏, but I create articles, long/short videos, and daily posts.

Picking Social Channels

Once you know your mediums, you need to decide where to post.

Start by asking yourself three questions:

1. Where does my audience already spend time?

• B2B, founders, tech → 𝕏, LinkedIn

• Consumers, lifestyle, broad audiences → Instagram, TikTok

• Education, deep dives, search → YouTube

2. What am I best at?

• Writing → 𝕏, LinkedIn, newsletter

• On camera → TikTok, Reels, YouTube

• Talking → Podcasts, YouTube

3. How much time can I realistically commit?

• Low → 1-2 channels

• Medium → 2-4 channels

• High → 5+ channels with repurposing

Every channel can tell a different part of your story. 𝕏 might be more product-focused, Instagram more lifestyle, and your newsletter more personal.
