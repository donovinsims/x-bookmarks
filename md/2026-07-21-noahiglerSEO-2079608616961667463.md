# Just partnered with an HVAC company doing over $2M a year.

## Post

Just partnered with an HVAC company doing over $2M a year.

They couldn't tell me which marketing channel makes their phone ring.

Here's the tracking setup we're installing before we touch anything else.

This is more common than you'd think at that size. The phone rings all day, jobs get booked, and nobody can say whether the call came from the Google profile, the website, the trucks, or a referral. Which means every marketing dollar gets spent on a feeling.

The fix is source-specific tracking numbers.

> One number for the website. Anyone who calls it found you online.

> One number for the ads. Now you know your true cost per booked job, not your cost per click.

> One number for the trucks and yard signs. This is the one that surprises owners the most. Wraps tend to drive way more calls than anyone expects, and now you can prove it.

> The Google Business Profile gets its own number too, and here's the part almost everyone gets wrong: whatever number sits on the GBP goes on every citation as well. Same number on Yelp, the BBB, Angi, all of it. Google checks that they match.

Every number forwards to the same phone your team already answers, so nothing changes for the office. But 30 days from now this owner will know exactly what each channel produces, and the marketing budget stops being a feeling and starts being math.
