# Talked to a painting company owner who's struggling to get leads.

## Post

Talked to a painting company owner who's struggling to get leads.

His only consistent source right now is Angi and Thumbtack, so every estimate he shows up to there are 2-4 other painters already in the driveway...

Naturally, I checked his Google profile. 35 reviews. His top competitors have 150-200+.

His bottleneck isn't his website, GBP settings, or citations. It's just reviews (at least for now). No amount of local SEO will rank him without having a stronger review profile.

He told me he'd spoken with 4 agencies before me. Three of them tried to sign him on retainers with timelines that made no sense for a business sitting at 35 reviews.

Only ONE agency gave him the same advice I did: to get his reviews up first, then invest in SEO once it can actually move the needle.

Always interesting to see who gives real advice and who just wants to win the deal...
