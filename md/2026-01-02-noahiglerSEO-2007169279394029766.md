# A garage door company was spending $3k/month on Google Ads.

## Post

A garage door company was spending $3k/month on Google Ads.

They were getting clicks but paying $960 per lead.

We changed one thing and their cost per lead dropped by 60%.

They were sending all their ad traffic to their homepage.

The homepage:
> Talked about their company history
> Listed all 8 services
> Mentioned 12 cities they serve
> Had a small contact form buried at the bottom

Someone clicks an ad for "garage door repair Dallas" and lands on a page about everything except garage door repair in Dallas.

They bounce but the business already paid for the click.

Here's what we changed:

Built dedicated landing pages for each service and location they were running ads for.

The "garage door repair Dallas" ad now sends people to a page specifically about garage door repair in Dallas.

Nothing else on that page, JUST that service in that city.

Each landing page followed this structure:

Above the fold:
> H1 with the exact service and city
> Star rating and review count
> Big tap-to-call button
> Three reassurances (licensed, same-day service, warranty)
> Short lead form with an service-specific offer

Below the fold:
> The process
> What the service includes
> Common pain points
> Real photos from jobs in that area
> FAQ section
> Embedded reviews mentioning that service

The page has one job: get them to call or fill out the form.

That's it...

No navigation menu distracting them.

No links to other services.

No "learn more about our company" rabbit holes.

Within 30 days their cost per lead dropped from $960 to $384.

The ads and keywords didn't change much.

If you're running Google Ads to your homepage, you're wasting money on PPC.
