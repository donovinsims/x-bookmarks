### 4/8

https://x.com/khookhar/status/2115137237234925584

1/ Seeing the full picture of every patient

The medical record is a fragmented view of a patient’s world that misses most of what influences their health.

To construct a fuller representation of each patient, we build digital twins that assemble their longitudinal history across every dimension that shapes their outcomes, including clinical, behavioral, social, and economic determinants.

These computational models draw on living patient context, updating as new information arrives to forecast how their health might unfold.

2/ Acting on what we see

The next decade of healthcare will be defined by AI that delivers care.

Our agents work alongside care teams, so when we can see where a patient is headed, we can do something about it.

But like any practicing physician, they have to earn the right to act. That’s what our Digital Residency is for: our simulation engine for validating clinical AI at scale.

And because they’re part of everyday work like intake, notes, and follow-ups, they capture a new class of data: not just what happened, but why, and how it helped the patient.

3/ Learning from what happens

This is what I wish my mom had. A hospital that remembered every woman who came before her.

Illness and care unfold in recognizable patterns. Symptoms often show up in a familiar order, and patients encounter barriers to treatment at predictable moments. We call these recurring patterns Archetypes.

We believe outcomes can be traced back to the Archetypes that produced them, providing a way to connect each patient's prior experience to probable futures.

The result is that every patient treated improves the care of the next.
