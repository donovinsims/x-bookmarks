# The biggest ranking signal on Google Business Profile is reviews.

## Post

The biggest ranking signal on Google Business Profile is reviews.
 
But not in the way most home service owners think.

Total review count gets the attention. Recency is what actually moves the rankings.

A competitor sitting on 600 reviews who stopped generating new ones 8 months ago is completely beatable by a profile with 150 reviews and 25 in the last 30 days.

Google's local algorithm weights review velocity heavily. The signal it's reading is "this business is active, customers are engaging right now, and the experience is current." A frozen review profile reads as a stalling business...
 
The math on this is wild once you see it.
 
If you're doing 60 jobs a month and 10% of customers leave a review, you're getting 6 reviews. If you can move that conversion rate to 35%, you're getting 21. Same job count, 3.5x the reviews flowing in every month.
 
The system that gets you there:
 
> QR code stickers in every truck and on every invoice, linked to the Google review URL
> Tech trained to ask in person right after the job: "If everything looks good, would you mind scanning this and leaving a quick Google review? Takes 30 seconds." Phone gets handed over.
> $25 paid out to the tech for having their customer leave a review (not mentioning their names though)
> Automated SMS at 2 hours after job completion as a backup for anyone who didn't leave one on site
> Owner personally calls 5 past five-star customers every Friday for unwritten reviews
> Reply to every review within 24 hours, signed by name
 
The ranking lift isn't about the total number. It's about Google watching the velocity month over month and deciding this is the active, real business that should show up first.
 
If your review count looks the same today as it did 6 months ago, that's what's holding back the ranking.
