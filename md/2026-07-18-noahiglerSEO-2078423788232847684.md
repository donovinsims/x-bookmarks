# We set up a Google Business Profile for a plumbing company and within 7 months it was doing 276 calls a month.

## Post

We set up a Google Business Profile for a plumbing company and within 7 months it was doing 276 calls a month.

Owners obsess over the wrong parts of their profile.

Here's every section, graded by what it actually does.

---

THE RANKING LEVERS

> Business name. The heaviest signal on the list. An exact match name like Katy Heating, Air Conditioning, and Plumbing has a real ranking edge over Smith & Sons for every service in the name, and the legal way to get one is filing a DBA. Do not keyword-stuff your existing listing, profiles get suspended for that.

> Primary category. One pick, massive weight. Check what the top 3 in your market use and recheck quarterly. Google adds new categories all the time and a tighter match can jump you overnight.

> Address. A real office inside the city you want to win. Physical address profiles outrank service area profiles in nearly every competitive market.

> Reviews. Google reads the pace and the words. Steady weekly reviews signal a business that keeps completing jobs, and customers who mention your service and city inside a review are writing ranking content for you.

> Services and the landing page. Fill out every service with real descriptions, then point the website link at a page built for that service and city. The strength of the page you link to is part of how the profile ranks.

> Products. Turn your services into product cards with photos, descriptions, and price ranges. It's structured data, so it helps Google understand exactly what you offer, and language models can pull from it too. Bonus: on a phone the cards eat half the screen, which leaves less room for competitors.

---

THE CONVERSION LEVERS

These barely touch rankings. They decide whether the person looking at your profile calls you or the next company down.

> Photos. Real job photos from your techs, uploaded monthly. Authentic shots from actual jobs build more trust than anything a stock library sells.

> Description. Main service and city in the first two sentences, because nobody reads sentence three.

> Booking and quote buttons. Every tap you remove between "found you" and "contacted you" is money.

> GBP posts. They will not move your rank, so we use them as an indexing channel instead. Every post links to a new page on the site with a Learn More button, and Google crawls that page faster. Learn More, not Call Now. The click-through is the whole point.

---

Grade your own profile against this list. The ranking levers take an afternoon to fix and the conversion levers take a Saturday. One well-built profile can carry an entire business. I've watched it happen at 276 calls a month.
