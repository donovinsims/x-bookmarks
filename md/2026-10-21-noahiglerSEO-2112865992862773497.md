# Here's the exact sitemap I'd build for an HVAC and plumbing company that wants to show up in ChatGPT and Google's AI Mo…

## Post

Here's the exact sitemap I'd build for an HVAC and plumbing company that wants to show up in ChatGPT and Google's AI Mode.

It's the same base as the sitemap I posted last month, plus the pages AI search pulls its answers from.

(Bookmark)

The base stays the same:

> Homepage
> One hub per trade (cooling, heating, plumbing), with a page for every service under it
> A page for every city you serve, linked to the Google Business Profile that covers it

The Google profile is what ranks in the map pack. The city page backs it up with the same name, address and phone number, real job photos from that city, and the services you actually run there.

Then add the AI layer:

1. Cost guides for your money services
AC replacement, furnace replacement, water heaters, sewer lines, re-pipes. Give a real range, what pushes a job to the low or high end, and the monthly payment. Label it as an estimate, because the price in the home is the real quote.

2. Repair or replace pages
Homeowners with an old unit or old pipes all ask the same thing: fix it or replace it. Answer it with the math, using age, repair cost and efficiency.

3. Brand comparison pages
Carrier vs Trane vs Lennox, tank vs tankless. Say what you install most and why, in your own words as the installer.

4. A real questions hub
Pull the questions straight from your customer calls and answer each one on its own page. Nobody else in your market is answering them in your customers' own words.

5. Trust pages
Your team with real photos, licenses, warranties, financing and a reviews page. They show homeowners, and AI search, that you're a real company.

How it links together:

> Cost guides link to their service page and your top city pages
> On each city page, list only the services you actually run there
> From each service page, point to its cost guide and comparison pages

The fencing company we work with is one of the only companies in its market with real pricing pages, and it shows up all over AI search.
