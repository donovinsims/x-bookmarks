# Published 3 press releases for a plumber over the past 2 months.

## Post

Published 3 press releases for a plumber over the past 2 months.

He now shows up when you ask ChatGPT for a plumber in his city and got 2 jobs from it last month.

The topic of the articles doesn't matter that much. What matters is your business name showing up next to your service and city across news aggregator sites that LLMs pull from.

This was the first month where I've seen a surprising amount of ChatGPT leads across the companies we work with.

ChatGPT local search isn't even close to Google yet in terms of total volume, but wouldn't it be cool to get some leads from it?
