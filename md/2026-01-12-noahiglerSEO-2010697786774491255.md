# Link building is a waste of money for most local businesses.

## Post

Link building is a waste of money for most local businesses.

Here's why reviews are far more important for ranking locally:

70% of your leads come from the Map Pack.

Not your website.

Not your blog.

The Map Pack.

Links help your website rank in organic results.

But your website isn't what shows up first when someone searches "plumber near me."

Your Google Business Profile is.

And reviews are the #1 factor for Map Pack rankings.

Not backlinks (they influence the Map Pack to some extent, but not significantly).

Here's what actually moves the needle:
> Review velocity (how many you get per month)
> Review quality (keywords and specifics mentioned)
> Review recency (Google weighs the last 90 days heavily)

Most businesses only ask for reviews after completed jobs.

That limits you to maybe 10-15 review opportunities per month.

Here's how we get 25-30+ per month:

Treat inspections and quotes as reviewable moments.

Even if they don't hire you, they got value.

Train your team to ask after every estimate.

"If the inspection was helpful today, would you mind leaving a quick Google review?"

Lower pressure and higher conversion.

Stop chasing links from random blogs.

Double down on reviews.

That's what actually gets your phone ringing.
