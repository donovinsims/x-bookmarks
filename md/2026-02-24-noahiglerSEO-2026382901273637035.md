# Took over Google Ads for this plumbing company that was only running Performance Max.

## Post

Took over Google Ads for this plumbing company that was only running Performance Max.

Ad spend was about $15k/month and the p-max campaigns were running without past data on the ad account.

Lack of real tracking, no fine-tuned service targeting, high CPLs, etc.

Over the last month and a half, we've helped them scale past $30k/month at ~8x ROAS.

When we audited the account we found three problems.

30-40% of the budget was going to branded searches.

People who already knew the company name and were going to call anyway. This is a solid strategy but not at 30-40% of adspend.. (they also rank #1 for branded search).

Every service was lumped into one campaign. Google optimized for the cheapest click, which meant $150 drain calls instead of $3,000+ water heater replacements.

All ad traffic landed on the homepage. Someone searching "tankless water heater installation" saw a page about every service they offer. Conversion rates were sub 4% (pretty terrible).

We broke everything into segmented campaigns by service type.
> High ticket services got their own budgets
> Added negative keywords to kill some spend wasted on brand
> Built dedicated landing pages for each service

Then we weighted 60-70% of the budget toward the services that actually make money. Water heaters, repiping, and sewer work primarily.

Spend doubled and cost per lead dropped, all while making a larger return because they were getting more large jobs.

With some businesses, even the basics can bring insane change in marketing ROAS.
