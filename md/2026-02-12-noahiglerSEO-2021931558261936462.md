# After 6 months of SEO, I told this client we should pause everything.

## Post

After 6 months of SEO, I told this client we should pause everything.

I thought we were failing.

Here's the story of how we helped this construction company collect $2.2M in revenue in 9 months of SEO.

Competitive market, slow review velocity, building links was slower than usual.

I wasn't super stoked on the results 6 months in so I sat down with the owner.

I literally suggested we pause SEO until they get more reviews.

He said I was crazy and screenshared their CRM...

$2.2M in revenue attributed to their Google Business Profile in the past 5 months.

About 20% was cash collected from smaller remodels.

The remaining $1.7M was for a custom home build they closed from their GBP.

All because they rank top 3 (not even #1) for a design to build keyword...

Not only did this make my day, but it gave us the green light to continue SEO.

Now this client is one of our best case studies.

That conversation completely changed how I think about high ticket home services and local SEO.

When your average job is $500, you need a high volume of leads to make SEO worth it.

But when your average job is $50k or $100k or in this case $1.7M, you don't need many leads at all to make SEO mega-profitable.

They went from from ~ 20 to 80 calls per month in 9 months.

I love high ticket home services.
