# Premium local SEO should take work off the owner's plate in the first month.

## Post

Premium local SEO should take work off the owner's plate in the first month.

At $4k-$6k a month, the owner shouldn't get a stack of Word documents and become the project manager.

The experience should look more like this:

> One focused conversation gives the agency the business context it needs
> The team builds the strategy and first drafts before asking for feedback
> One strategist knows the market, priorities, data, and work in progress
> Updates explain what changed, what was learned, and what happens next
> Reporting ties visibility to qualified calls, booked jobs, and revenue when the tracking is there
> Every asset stays under the business's ownership

Deliverables still matter.

The bigger value is having a team make good decisions without making the owner manage the work they hired out.
