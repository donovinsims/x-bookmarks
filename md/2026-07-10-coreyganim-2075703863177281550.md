# The easiest way I know to sell a $2,000 a month AI service is to sell a $1,000 audit first.

## Post

The easiest way I know to sell a $2,000 a month AI service is to sell a $1,000 audit first.

Here's the ladder:

1) Free mini assessment. Uncover one pain point, show them one opportunity. Takes 15-30 minutes of your time.

2) Paid assessment, $1,000. Uncover 5 to 7 or more pain points across their business.

3) Then the upsell. When you deliver the assessment on the follow-up call, you upsell the $2,000 a month AI Concierge service.

If they're on the fence you can credit the $1,000 they already paid toward month one.

So the pitch becomes: the assessment is done, and if you move forward, it was free.

The assessment does double duty. It maps every  opportunity you'll tackle during the AI Concierge engagement.

And it qualifies the lead. Someone who pays $1,000 up front to have their business analyzed is not going to waste your time on the retainer conversation.

Foot in the door, then credit the door fee.

No-brainer offer.
