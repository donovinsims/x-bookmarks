# If you don't have a fully completed Yelp profile, you're probably invisible to ChatGPT for a lot of local searches.

## Post

If you don't have a fully completed Yelp profile, you're probably invisible to ChatGPT for a lot of local searches.

Plenty of SMBs doing $2M+ barely touch their Yelp profile.

I get it, Yelp has a bad reputation with business owners for a lot of reasons.

But here's what matters right now: ChatGPT cites Yelp constantly in local business recommendations. When someone asks "who's the best plumber in [city]," one of the first sources ChatGPT checks is Yelp.

If your Yelp profile has an outdated phone number, no photos, no services listed, and a few reviews from YEARs ago... the AI is going to skip you and recommend someone whose Yelp profile actually looks active and complete.

Same thing with Bing.

Most people don't realize that ChatGPT pulls from Bing's index of businesses. If your business information on Bing is wrong or you're not listed at all, that's another source where you don't exist to ChatGPT.

The fix takes 20 minutes:

Go to Yelp. Claim your profile if you haven't already. Update your phone number, address, hours, and website. Add photos from recent jobs. Fill out your service descriptions. Respond to any reviews sitting there unanswered.

Start getting a few reviews on Yelp every month without de-prioritizing your GBP.

Then go to Bing Places and do the same thing:
> Claim your listing
> Make sure all your information is correct
> Add photos and services

These aren't platforms you need to actively manage every week, but having complete, accurate profiles on both is a baseline requirement for showing up when people ask AI for local recommendations.

20 minutes of work for visibility on one of the biggest AI search platforms in the world.

Most of your competitors haven't done it...
