# Here's my master list on how to increase your website's conversion rate:

## Post

Here's my master list on how to increase your website's conversion rate:

(bookmark)

> Form no longer than 3 required fields
> Show average response time ("We call back in under 3 minutes")
> Big, clear call-to-action button
> Sticky call button that follows the user when scrolling
> Hero section: what you do, who you serve, why trust you
> Display "X jobs completed this month" as live social proof
> Display star rating and review count above the fold
> Show the Google logo next to your reviews for credibility
> Service and city in the H1 headline
> Add a 30-second owner intro video after the hero and reviews sections
> 3 reassurances near your CTA (licensed, warranty, same-day, etc.)
> Real photos from actual jobs, not stock images
> Lead form with a specific offer, not just "Contact Us"
> Phone number visible without scrolling
> Mobile load time under 3 seconds
> Remove navigation links on landing pages
> Compress all images to under 400KB
> Delete plugins you don't actually use
> One clear action per page
> Testimonials mentioning specific services
> Before and after photos from local jobs
> Remove "About Us" and company history from above the fold
> Add trust badges (BBB, industry certifications)
> Embed your GBP map on location pages
> FAQ section answering common objections
> Service process breakdown so they know what to expect
> Pricing context ("starting at $50") to pre-qualify leads
> Enable lazy loading for images below the fold
> Remove chatbots unless you can respond in under 2 minutes
> Test your site on mobile weekly
> Make your phone number tap-to-call on mobile
> Use a CDN like Cloudflare for faster load times
