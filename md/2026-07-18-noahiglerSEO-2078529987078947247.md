# I brought a roofing company $127K in jobs in 30 days, all from advanced link building.

## Post

I brought a roofing company $127K in jobs in 30 days, all from advanced link building.

Not the kind Fiverr vendors sell in bulk for $20.

Here are the links that actually move your local map rankings and exactly where to get them.

---

THE METRICS THAT MATTER

My tier list for judging any backlink:

1. Relevance. Local relevance or industry relevance trumps everything else.

2. Traffic. Real sites with real visitors.

3. Domain authority.

In an ideal world you build local or industry relevant links that carry some traffic and some authority on top. Chasing domain authority alone is the classic mistake. Yes, it inflates your DA score, and yes, it can nudge some SERP rankings. It barely touches your local map rankings though, and the map pack is where the business actually comes from.

---

THE LINKS WORTH CHASING

> Manufacturer and supplier pages. GAF and Owens Corning list their certified roofers. Carrier and Trane list dealers. Your distributors run locator pages too. High authority, perfectly relevant, and free, you just have to ask your rep.

> Local sponsorships with an actual link. Youth sports, charity runs, school fundraisers. The check buys goodwill and the link on their site buys rankings. Confirm the link goes live, because half of sponsors forget to add it.

> Local press. Give reporters something worth covering. Hiring 10 techs, a wild job story, free work for a veteran. Links from your city's news sites are the most trusted signals in your market.

> Chamber and trade associations. Paid, indexed, locally relevant, done in an afternoon.

> Partner trades. The restoration company you refer water jobs to has a website, and so does the electrician you call for panel work. Swap resource page links with businesses that already send you work.

---

WHERE TO POINT THEM

The homepage is fine. That's where most links land naturally anyway and Google expects it. The exception is multi-location companies. If there's a specific location page you want to push up the SERP rankings, build links straight at it.

---

BRAND MENTIONS

Link building matters less than it used to. Google has gotten good enough that it doesn't always need a clickable link. Another site simply mentioning your brand name, ideally your exact GBP name, passes value on its own now. Brand mentions are becoming just as important as backlinks, especially as search shifts toward language models that read everything written about you.

Links still count, so build them. Every unlinked mention of your business is working for you too.
