# The difference between a contractor getting 3 reviews a month and 15 has nothing to do with how good their work is.

## Post

The difference between a contractor getting 3 reviews a month and 15 has nothing to do with how good their work is.

It's the system behind it.

The contractors I work with who pull 15+ reviews a month all do the same things:

> They ask on site before they leave, not later
> They use a QR code card that links directly to the Google review page
> They ask after estimates and consultations, not just completed jobs
> They automate a text follow up at 12 hours for anyone who didn't leave one on the spot
> They follow up with an email at 24 hours
> They have someone on their team manually call happy customers who didn't leave a review 2 days after the job

The ones getting 3 a month are doing great work too. They just wait until the customer gets home, maybe send a text a few days later, and hope they remember.

Most homeowners are happy to leave a review right after a good interaction. The problem is nobody asks them at the right time.

A tech standing in someone's kitchen saying "hey if you don't mind scanning this real quick, it really helps us out" converts way higher than a text 3 days later when the homeowner has already moved on with their life.

The other thing that matters is treating consultations as reviewable interactions...If you're a remodeler doing 20 free estimates a month, that's 20 potential reviews you're leaving on the table by only asking after completed projects.
