# I talked to a $2M plumbing company this week.

## Post

I talked to a $2M plumbing company this week.

They shouldn't hire us for SEO yet.

The business has been around for roughly 30 years, but its Google profile is below 4 stars.

The map scan had it sitting worse than 20th for the plumbing searches that bring in calls.

Its strongest competitors have 1,100 to 2,500+ reviews with ratings above 4.7.

I could sell the owner more pages, links, and Google profile work. That would send more homeowners toward a profile they don't trust enough to call.

The first job is fixing the review system:

> Train every tech to ask in person before leaving
> Put a direct QR review link in every truck
> Follow up after the job when the customer forgets
> Track how many customers were asked, not only how many reviews came in
> Work toward a 4.5+ rating and a review count that looks credible in the market

Paid search can create jobs now while the company rebuilds its reputation.

SEO makes more sense once the profile is strong enough to rank and convert.

Sometimes the best advice I can give a home service owner is to keep us out of the budget for now...
