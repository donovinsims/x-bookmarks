7. How I Use It Every Week
sunday: the weekly review updates what works, then I say "plan my week" and get a plan with ideas
every morning: I open the dashboard, read the quote picks and the outliers, and pick what to post
every post: I rewrite the draft until it sounds like me and post it myself

#1 Nothing posts on its own
I really don't recommend fully automating this. Don't let it post on its own: always double check everything it writes and rewrite it the way you'd normally write
