# SEO tip: set up Bing Webmaster Tools, even if you hate Bing.

## Post

SEO tip: set up Bing Webmaster Tools, even if you hate Bing.

It has the only free report where the AI company itself tells you how many times it cited your site.

Microsoft added it in February and called it AI Performance.

Google's version in Search Console is still limited to some UK sites.

What the report shows you:

• Total citations across Copilot and Bing's AI answers
• Which of your pages get cited the most
• The exact questions that pulled your site in
• How all of it moves week to week

For a local business, the questions list is the part to read.

If your service pages never appear in it, AI isn't using them, and those are the pages to fix first.

Setup takes a couple of minutes (sign in at bing(.)com/webmasters and import your site from Search Console).

Give it a few weeks to fill in, then check which questions show up.
