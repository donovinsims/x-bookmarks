# Google may be building a weird new form of organic retargeting.

## Post

Google may be building a weird new form of organic retargeting.

Dumb theory we're testing: ask past customers to mark your website as a Preferred Source.

Customer acquisition costs keep rising. If you paid $300, $600, or $1,000 to win a customer, getting another job from that person makes the original marketing spend more valuable.

Reviews help the next homeowner trust you.

Preferred Sources could help Google remember that an old customer already chose to trust your site.

Imagine they forget your HVAC company's name 18 months later and search "AC replacement" or ask AI Mode who to call. Google already knows that person picked your site as a source they want to see more from.

There's zero evidence this helps Maps today or that Google will use it this way.

But Search keeps getting more personal, and we're testing it...
