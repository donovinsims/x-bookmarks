# Full A-Z guide on how to grow on X (works for small accounts)

Full A-Z guide on how to grow on X (works for small accounts)

For context, we took an account from 200 followers to 1,200 in about two months so this works.

First up:

When u post, X doesn't show it to ur followers. It shows it to a small group of strangers and watches what they do.

If enough react, it goes wider.

With no history on the account, that group is tiny. Ten, twenty people.

So u write something good, twenty strangers scroll past, and it's over.

That's not a quality problem. The best post in ur niche dies in the same room.

The whole job is not starting in that room.

Here’s 4 ways.

1. Quote RT maxxing

Ur post starts from nothing. A quote retweet starts inside something already moving, in front of an audience someone else built.

Three rules.

Get in early, while the original is still climbing. Quote something that peaked hours ago and nobody's there.

Never agree. "Great point" gets a like, and a like is worth almost nothing — a repost is worth around forty of them. Disagree or sharpen it.

Make it stand alone. Half the people who see it won't tap through.

5-10 a day.

2. Niche content

"How to grow ur business" reaches nobody. "If ur podcast is doing 400 views a clip" stops exactly one person, and that person has budget.

X also sorts accounts by topic so it knows who to show u to. Post about five things and it never works it out.

This account was a clipping offer, so everything pointed at clipping. It had u figured in weeks.

Every off-topic post resets that.

3. Article maxxing

Articles are the best growth lever on here. I've seen accounts under 1K followers do a million views on one.

Not because they get special treatment. Because they're long.

A normal post gets one shot. An Article is big enough to pull a different section out every day and quote retweet with a new angle. Each quote is a fresh post pointing back at it.

One afternoon of writing, seven runs at the algorithm.

And almost nobody writes them. The slot is empty.

4. An engagement network

The one everyone skips.

X decides a post on the first thirty minutes. That window carries roughly 1000x the weight of everything after.

Big accounts have history the algorithm trusts. U don't, so that window is all u've got.

U need a few accounts in ur niche ready to engage the second u post. Same niche, not a random pod — X reads topical relevance too.

A few real comments and quote RTs in the first half hour is the difference between 400 views and 40,000. Same post.
