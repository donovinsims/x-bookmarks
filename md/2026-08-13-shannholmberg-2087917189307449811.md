# how an ai writing harness works, visualized

## Post

how an ai writing harness works, visualized

1. create the skill that loads your writing .md files, voice-dna, forbidden patterns and examples

2. when you call the skill or prompt your agents to write, it loads in the skill and the reference files

3. it drafts the content, then spins up eval loops from other subagents to check the draft against your guidelines

4. your turn to evaluate, edit what you want, always hand edit before you ship (avoid slop), then either publish yourself or hand over to your orchestrator agents

5. save the approved content to a database, this becomes your reference library for future drafts

always stay under 50% context window to avoid hallucinations and quality drops
