# This local SEO trick helped a landscaping company we work with dominate their entire local market.

## Post

This local SEO trick helped a landscaping company we work with dominate their entire local market.

They've been with us for a while and gave us full control over their marketing, so we tried something different.

We made 100 yard signs and wrapped their new trucks with a big QR code. But the QR code doesn't send people to their website.

It sends them to a Google search for the company name.

When someone scans it, they see the company's Google Business Profile with hundreds of five star reviews right at the top. It's a way better first impression than any landing page could give them.

But that's not even the real strategy here.

Every single person who scans that QR code is creating a "branded search". Google is registering that someone searched specifically for this business by name.

When a company has a high volume of branded searches, Google interprets that as authority. Real people are actively looking for this business. That's one of the strongest trust signals that exists.

When Google trusts your brand... it starts ranking you higher for the non-branded searches like "landscaper near me" or "landscaping company in [city]."

So every yard sign sitting in a customer's front yard and every truck driving around town is slowly feeding Google a stream of branded search data. That, and that data is helping them outrank competitors for the keywords that actually generate revenue.

This also works for AI search. ChatGPT and Gemini both want to recommend companies that real people search for by name. Building a brand with real search volume behind it is one of the best ways to show up on Google Maps, organic results, and AI search all at the same time.

Yard signs and truck wraps have been around forever.

Most companies use them to put a phone number in front of people. This company is using them to build the kind of brand signal that Google and AI models reward with rankings.
