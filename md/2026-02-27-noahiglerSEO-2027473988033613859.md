# Local SEO tricks to rank higher in the Map Pack that 98% of your competitors aren't doing:

## Post

Local SEO tricks to rank higher in the Map Pack that 98% of your competitors aren't doing:

> File a DBA and update your Google Business Profile to an exact match business name. "Johnson's AC Repair Dallas" will outrank "Johnson's Heating and Cooling" for AC repair searches every single time. The DBA costs about $45, takes a week to process, and is completely within Google's guidelines as long as it's your legal business name. This one change alone can jump you 2-3 spots.

> Stop linking your GBP to your homepage. Build a dedicated landing page for the exact city your profile is registered in and link your GBP to that page instead. If your GBP is in Plano but your homepage says "Serving the entire DFW Metroplex," Google doesn't know what city to rank you for. A city-specific landing page with your NAP, local neighborhoods, and real job photos from that area tells Google exactly where you belong. CAVEAT: if your home page has massive authority and you don't have multiple GBPs, stick with your home page.

> Get your physical location as close as possible to where your customers actually live. Google heavily favors proximity in Map Pack rankings. A small office right in the middle of your highest-value residential area can completely change your rankings. $500-700 a month in rent pays for itself with one or two extra jobs.

> If you serve a large metro, stop trying to rank one profile for the entire area. Set up additional GBP locations in different parts of the city, each with its own business name, landing page, phone number, and review profile. A restoration company with one profile in North Dallas and another in South Dallas can show up in the Map Pack for both areas instead of being invisible in one of them.

> Respond to every single Google review within 24 hours and mention the service and city naturally in your response. "Thanks for trusting us with your AC repair in North Austin, glad we could get your system running before the heat wave" tells Google what you do and where you do it. A lot of contractors either don't respond at all or copy paste "Thank you for your kind words!" on everything. Every review response is a free relevance signal and you're wasting it if you're not including keywords.

> Set your GBP hours to match when people actually search for your services. If you're an emergency plumber or HVAC company, list your hours as 24/7. Google deprioritizes profiles that appear closed at the time of search. Most of your competitors list 8am to 5pm, which means after-hours searches are wide open for anyone willing to answer the phone. The person searching "emergency plumber" at 9pm isn't price shopping. Their toilet is overflowing and they need someone now. Get an offshore CSR or set up an AI agent that fields these calls.

> Add every single service you offer to your GBP with keyword-rich descriptions. Not just your primary service. Every variation. A plumber should have drain cleaning, water heater installation, water heater repair, emergency plumbing, sewer line repair, toilet repair, faucet installation, and anything else they do listed individually with a short description that mentions their city.

> Pay your techs per each review they get. This is literally the easiest way to get a higher review velocity without gaming the system. Velocity of reviews is (in my experience) the most important ranking factor.
