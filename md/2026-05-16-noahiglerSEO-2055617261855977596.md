# The GBP services section is one of the most underrated ranking levers in local SEO.

## Post

The GBP services section is one of the most underrated ranking levers in local SEO.
 
Most home service business owners list 5 or 6 generic services and call it done. "Plumbing."
"HVAC repair."
"Roofing."
 
That's leaving dozens of long-tail rankings on the table.
 
Google uses your services list to match your business with specific searches. If someone searches "tankless water heater installation" and that exact phrase isn't listed in your GBP services section, you're less visible for that search even if you do that work every week.
 
20 minutes of cleanup here can move profiles from page 2 into the top 5 inside of a month.
 
Here's how to do this right:
 
> List every single service you offer as a separate item, not bundled
> Include service variations and the keywords customers actually search
> Write a 2-3 sentence description for each one with natural keyword placement
> Add specific brand names if you service them ("Trane installations", "Rheem water heaters")
> Update seasonally. Add "AC tune-ups" before summer. "Furnace inspections" before winter.
 
A few examples of what "every variation" actually looks like:
 
A plumber should have 30-50 services listed:
> Drain cleaning, sewer line repair, sewer line replacement, water heater installation, water heater repair, tankless water heater installation, tankless water heater repair, sump pump installation, sump pump replacement, gas line repair, garbage disposal installation, leak detection, slab leak repair, repipe services, water softener installation, etc.
 
An HVAC company should have 25-40:
> AC repair, AC installation, AC maintenance, ductless mini split installation, heat pump installation, heat pump repair, furnace repair, furnace installation, ductwork installation, ductwork cleaning, indoor air quality services, thermostat installation, commercial HVAC, emergency HVAC, etc.
 
A landscaper should have 20-30:
> Lawn care, lawn mowing, lawn fertilization, landscape design, landscape installation, hardscape installation, retaining walls, paver patios, outdoor lighting, irrigation installation, irrigation repair, snow removal, leaf cleanup, mulch installation, etc.
 
This isn't keyword stuffing. These are services you actually perform. You're just making them visible to Google so they can match you to the right searches.
 
20 minutes of work, one afternoon, free.
