# The first thing we fix for a $10M+ home service company is the tracking.

## Post

The first thing we fix for a $10M+ home service company is the tracking.

At one HVAC company we just started with, Google Ads showed about 1,000 conversions a month.

The real number was about half.

A 5-second call counted as a conversion, and a 25-second call counted twice. Their Google profile showed the main office number, so the CRM only credited the profile with 5 leads.

It's been the same story at 5 of the biggest companies we work with. These owners know their close rates, their tech numbers and their average tickets cold. Somewhere between the click and the sold job, the numbers broke anyway:

> One plumbing company's CRM logged calls at one of two fixed values, and 95 booked jobs showed $0 billed
> A roofer's Google Ads counted YouTube subscribes and page views as conversions, and only 5 of 128 booked appointments fed the bidding
> Another HVAC company had 10 to 20 phone numbers in one campaign, and its organic and direct calls couldn't be separated
> At a second plumbing company, lead source was whatever the CSR heard on the call, and all 5 Google profiles linked to the homepage with no tracking

One owner asked me, "Have I just gotten lucky that I have enough sales coming in from everywhere else that I didn't realize that my ROAS was bad?"

So before we touch rankings or bids, we set up:

> A tracking number for each source, with the same number on each Google profile and its citations
> Tracking tags on every Google profile link
> Qualified calls as the main conversion to start, not every ring
> Every sold job matched in the CRM to the lead that started it
> Sold jobs uploaded to Google Ads as the primary conversion ONCE there's enough data, about 30 leads in the past 2 to 3 months
