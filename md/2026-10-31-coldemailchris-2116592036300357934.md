1/ Pick a market that lives on Google Maps

Landscapers, roofers, HVAC companies, dentists, gyms.

Plenty of these owners have no LinkedIn profile at all, so Apollo barely has them.

That's a list your competitors can't pull.

### 3/11

https://x.com/coldemailchris/status/2116592060069462173

2/ Scrape it zip code by zip code

Search "landscaper in Phoenix" and you get the first page of results.

There are 32,000+ US zip codes, and searching each one brings back a full set every time.

OpenWeb Ninja returns up to 499 businesses per call (Serper gives you 20).

### 4/11

https://x.com/coldemailchris/status/2116592076959928578

3/ Read their Google reviews before you email them

Owners never post their problems, but their customers do it for them.

For Eden we scored 69,204 landscaping businesses, and two of the heaviest signals were reviews complaining about slow quotes and a spike in new reviews in the last 90 days.

A landscaper whose reviews keep mentioning slow quotes has told you what to sell them.

### 5/11

4/ Email the addresses everyone else deletes

40-70% of a local list scraped from Google Maps is gmail or info@ addresses.

Run them from a separate EmailBison instance on Google inboxes, so your main sending never takes the risk.

No owner name? Drop the first-name variable (nobody notices).

### 6/11

https://x.com/coldemailchris/status/2116592100909482210

5/ Sell the result they already want

"We build AI agents" is one of the worst-converting pitches in the market right now.

A roofer wants more jobs booked and more 5-star reviews.

Put one local detail in the first line, like their review count or a competitor down the road, and reply rates go up 2-3x.

### 7/11

https://x.com/coldemailchris/status/2116592112624160911

6/ Send on Saturday

Trade owners work 10-12 hour days, 7 days a week.

Weekend sends work on them.

Corporate buyers are checked out by 2pm on a Friday.

### 8/11

7/ Call the same list

The email warms them up, and they pick up when they've seen your name.

For Eden, calling booked 62% of the meetings off 44% of the touches.
