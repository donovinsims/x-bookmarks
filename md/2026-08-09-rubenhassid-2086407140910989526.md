# You don't need an expensive Claude course.

## Post

You don't need an expensive Claude course.

You just need 20 mins a day for 26 days:

(I put together a whole calendar)

The red X on Sunday is on purpose.
Start on Monday.

Day 1 is not "download the app". That's day 7.

Here's the 26-day plan:

1. Add "ask me questions first" to any prompt. Claude hands you a form. You click your answers.

2. Model picker, bottom right. Fable 5 for your first message. Opus for the rest.

3. Stop sending tasks. "2 invoices unpaid, they went quiet, get me paid without burning them" is a goal.

4. Upload a 200-page PDF. Ask for 1 page, with page references so you can check it.

5. Paste 3 things you wrote: "write like this". Then one you hate: "never write like this".

6. Give up one Saturday. 3 free Anthropic certificates. Every paid Claude certificate is a scam.

7. Now download the app. Chat asks. Cowork works. Code builds.

8. Point Cowork at your messiest folder. Ask what's in it and what it could do. Walk away 15 min.

9. Add to any spreadsheet request: "list your top 10 assumptions, then execute".

10. Turn on Research, in the +. Ask a decision, not a search. 250 sources, 11 minutes.

11. Never type "no, that's wrong". Edit your first prompt. The bad answer costs you every message after.

12. Code tab. Bypass permissions on. Screenshot a site you like: "build me this, for my thing".

13. Build a mini-app that keeps your data. Put a Claude coach in it. Share the link.

14. /skill-creator. Teach it one task you repeat weekly. Call it with a slash, forever.

15. Delete your about-me file. I was wrong about files. They rot. Make it a Skill.

16. One Project per client. Contracts, emails, what you agreed. No leaking into other chats.

17. The test your team will get wrong: teachable to a human = Skill. One client = Project.

18. Connect 3: notetaker, email, team chat. Then ask one question that needs all three at once.

19. Add Drive & Notion, then switch most connectors off. Each one loads into every message you send.

20. Talk with Wispr flow, don't type. You speak 150 wpm. You type 60. And you leave the mess in, which it needs.

21. Feed it your 100 best posts. Ask for the report, then the SOP. Turn the SOP into a Skill.

22. The day it clicks. One slash, no prompt. It reads your notes, inbox and Slack, then asks YOU questions.

23. Ban the tells: "it's not X, it's Y", delve, leverage, seamless. Make it a Skill that audits you.

24. Drop a DESIGN .md into Claude Design. Everything you make lands in one visual system.

25. Privacy settings, training off, incognito for work. Never paste client data in a personal account.

26. Share your Skills & Projects. Then convert one colleague. The one drowning. Use their own work.

If you only ever do two of these: day 1 and day 14.

Day 1 stops you from prompting badly.
Day 14 stops you ever repeating yourself.

The welcome email has my Claude Skills library, so day 14 is already done for you.
