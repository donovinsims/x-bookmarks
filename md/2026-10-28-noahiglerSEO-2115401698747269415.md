# This $11M plumbing company's website hasn't booked a single job online in a year.

## Post

This $11M plumbing company's website hasn't booked a single job online in a year.

We're rebuilding its homepage.

Every section on the new one, top to bottom, and why it's there:

1. Reviews and a big call button first
The brand, star rating, what they do and where they do it, then a large call button and a book online button. Someone landing from Google needs those before anything else.

2. A header button we can swap
The "Book it now" button can switch between a coupon and straight booking, so we can test which one books more jobs.

3. 6 to 8 services, not the whole list
Big tiles that work on a phone. Toilets and faucets fold into one "Leaks" tile, and slab leaks, well pumps and gas lines get their own.

4. Price ranges without a form
For water heaters, sewer lines and water softeners, a slider shows a price range right on the page. No name or email needed to see it.

5. A 3-column comparison
The one-truck plumber, the big box store, and them. The guy running the company told me, "Everybody, we get to the house, they've already been to Home Depot or Menards."

6. Their own video
Homeowners want to see the people they're letting in the house.
