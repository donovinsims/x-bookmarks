# This is Google's new AI agent for your Google Business Profile.

## Post

This is Google's new AI agent for your Google Business Profile.

It answers customer questions, offers open appointment times and sends a link to book.

Here's how it works, and what I'd set up before you turn it on:

Customers tap Message on your profile in Google Search or Maps, and the chat opens in the regular texting app on their phone. The agent answers from what's on your profile and sends the harder questions to you.

On your end, every conversation shows up inside your Business Profile, split into "Needs your attention" and "Handled by your AI agent." You can take over any chat whenever you want.

Two catches:

> It's still a pilot. Google calls it early access at no cost, and not every business has it yet.
> The AI only works when the customer's phone and carrier support RCS. Everyone else's message goes to your business phone as a regular text.

Google is in this race on both sides of the call now. Since July 2025, its AI can call local businesses for searchers to check prices and availability. Now it's offering to answer for you too, and the same messaging tool has a Promote tab that ties into Google Ads.

My read is that Google wants the first conversation between a homeowner and your company to happen inside Google.

What I'd set up first:

> Fix what it reads first. Add every service you run with a real description, and make sure your hours and the towns you cover are right, because it can only answer as well as your profile does.
> Turn on the handoff alert, and decide who on your team owns the chats it can't answer. A chat that sits for an hour is a job someone else can book.
> Point the booking link on your profile at your real booking tool, with tracking tags, so you can see what these chats book.
> If your profile shows a tracking line or a landline, find out where those regular texts end up.
> Test it from your own phone. Ask what a homeowner would: can someone come out today, do you install tankless water heaters, what does a new AC cost.
