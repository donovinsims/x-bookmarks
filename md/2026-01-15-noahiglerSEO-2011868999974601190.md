# Competitor A has 47 review and you have 230.

## Post

Competitor A has 47 review and you have 230.

They still outrank you. Here's why:

Total review count is only one piece of the puzzle.

Google weighs several other factors just as heavily.

Sometimes more.

Here's what's probably happening:

1. They're closer to the searcher.

Proximity is one of Google's biggest ranking factors for local search.

If their GBP is 2 miles from the searcher and yours is 8 miles away, they win.

Doesn't matter how many reviews you have.

2. Their review velocity is higher.

Google cares more about your last 90 days than your total count.

If they're getting 15 reviews per month and you're getting 3, they look more active and trustworthy.

Fresh activity beats old volume.

3. They have keywords in their business name.

"Johnson's AC Repair Dallas" will outrank "Johnson's Heating and Cooling" for AC repair searches.

It's not fair, but it's how the algorithm works.

You can do this legally with a DBA.

4. Their GBP links to a city-specific page.

Your GBP links to your homepage that mentions 12 cities.

Theirs links to a page specifically about their primary city.

Google sees theirs as more relevant to local searches.

Stop obsessing over review count alone.

Fix the other factors and watch your rankings climb.
