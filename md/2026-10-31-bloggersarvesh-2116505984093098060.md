# LOCAL SEO MAXXING WITH GROK BOT:

## Post

LOCAL SEO MAXXING WITH GROK BOT:

1. Export GSC queries sitting in positions 5-15 and let Grok isolate the money keywords.

2. Feed it your service pages + GSC queries and find terms you already rank for but barely mention.

3. Compare your service page against the top 3 ranking pages and find missing sections.

4. Give Grok 3 competitor pages and make it identify what all of them cover that you don’t.

5. Export competitor backlinks and let Grok group them into local, niche, PR, directories, suppliers and guest posts.

6. Find domains linking to multiple competitors but not you.

7. Feed competitor reviews into Grok and pull out the services customers mention most.

8. Extract cities and neighborhoods repeatedly mentioned in competitor reviews.

9. Find the exact words customers use when describing their problem.

10. Turn customer review language into service-page sections and FAQs.

11. Export title tags + impressions + CTR and find pages getting seen but not clicked.

12. Give Grok your Screaming Frog URL export and find pages targeting the same intent.

13. Use it to spot service pages competing with location pages.

14. Export internal links and find money pages getting almost no internal authority.

15. Find strong pages on your site that can internally link into pages stuck on page 2.

16. Give Grok your GBP categories + competitor categories and look for patterns among the businesses ranking above you.

17. Feed it your service list + average job value and prioritize keywords by revenue potential.

18. Compare current rankings with job value to decide what deserves attention first.

19. Export the last 3 months of GSC data vs the previous 3 months and find pages quietly losing clicks.

20. Find commercial keywords gaining impressions before they start ranking properly.

21. Give Grok your city pages and find which ones are basically duplicates with the city swapped.

22. Compare location pages and identify where real local proof is missing.

23. Feed it customer reviews and pull out objections people had before hiring you.

24. Feed it sales-call transcripts and find questions prospects ask repeatedly.

25. Turn those questions into supporting content around your money pages.

26. Give Grok competitor title tags and find the angles Google keeps rewarding for the same query.

27. Compare H1s, title tags and page intent across the top-ranking results.

28. Feed it your backlink export and flag sites that look like obvious link farms.

29. Separate backlinks actually sending traffic from links that only look good on paper.

30. Find competitor pages earning links and figure out why people are linking to them.

31. Feed Grok old + current versions of a declining page and compare what changed.

32. Give it ranking winners and losers from the same site and look for patterns.

33. Export all indexed URLs and find pages getting zero impressions.

34. Find pages worth merging instead of creating more content.

35. Give Grok your main service page and ask which GSC queries deserve their own section.

36. Find informational queries that can naturally feed internal links into money pages.

37. Analyze review velocity across competitors instead of only comparing total reviews.

38. Compare the businesses ranking in Maps with the businesses ranking organically and find overlap.

39. Feed it the sites ChatGPT keeps citing in your niche and find places worth getting mentioned on.
