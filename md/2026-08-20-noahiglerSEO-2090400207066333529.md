# I've audited 10 home service websites built with Lovable or Claude Code in the past month.

## Post

I've audited 10 home service websites built with Lovable or Claude Code in the past month.

Most looked good at first glance.

Underneath the design, the SEO was horrible. I kept finding the same problems:

> 200 service and city pages built from one prompt
> The same AI copy on almost every page
> Missing or conflicting canonical tags
> Sitemaps full of junk URLs
> Most pages never indexed
> No real local projects, reviews, or useful information
> A confusing site structure with no real depth around the main services

Lovable and Claude Code are great website tools. Publishing 200 pages with no SEO plan and no QA creates the mess.

Use AI to build faster.

Have someone who understands SEO plan the structure, redirects, content, internal links, and launch.

It will take months for these companies to recover their SEO lol.
