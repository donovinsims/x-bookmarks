# The highest-margin line item in my $2,000/month AI concierge offer costs nothing to deliver.

## Post

The highest-margin line item in my $2,000/month AI concierge offer costs nothing to deliver.

Unlimited Voxer access.

The client hears "an AI expert in my pocket, any time I need one." That perception is what makes them pay top dollar.

The reality: they barely use it.

High perceived value. Near-zero delivery cost.

>

>
> 5) Add unlimited Voxer access. Feels like an AI expert in their pocket, so they pay top dollar. They barely use it.

> 7) Standardize on one ecosystem. Everything runs in Claude Cowork building Claude skills. No custom stack per client, no vendor lock-in.
>
> 8) Target owner-led service businesses doing $3M to $10M. Repetitive work, visible bottlenecks, real budget.

> 9) Your first client is in your phone. Text 10 business owners you know. 10 texts equals 5 to 6 free assessments, 2 to 3 paid, 1 concierge client.
>
> 10) Raise your price $250 with every yes. My next client won't be under $2,500 a month.

>
> 1) Boundaries are the offer. Free stops at the roadmap, concierge stops at two calls. Everything else is billed separately.
