# Claude Can Now Create the Mascot that Fits your App Best

## Post

I made a free Claude Code skill that designs a mascot for your app, draws every pose, animates every move and hands you the files for Xcode and the web.

after launching 2 apps, the part that always felt empty to me was the onboarding, the empty states, the loading screens, the "you did it" moment.

the app looked finished, but there was no character in it.

and when you try to fix that with AI, you usually get a cute blob with big eyes that could belong to any app on the store.

cute, but it means nothing.

so the skill is called Mascot Designer, and it doesn't start from cute. it starts from what your app stands for.

it starts with meaning

first it asks you four quick questions, written for your app.

for a savings app the answers could be things like money that grows, calm about money, a coach that pushes, or a friend who cheers.

then it designs the meaning: one idea, one signature feature, and a shape you can still read at 64 pixels.

the example on the page is Penny:

"Penny is a piggy bank with a sprout growing from its coin slot: your savings, growing."

that's the whole point. every shape has a reason.

you pick from three

it doesn't commit to the first idea.

it gives you three different ideas for the same meaning, you pick one, and only then it draws every pose of that one.

every move is already animated

this is the part I care about most, because a static mascot is just a sticker.

it comes with 8 moves:

→ hello

→ thinking

→ celebrate

→ oops

→ sleeping

→ pointing

→ love

→ appear

with anticipation before every jump, squash and stretch, blinks on their own clock and confetti in its own colours.

it's tuned once and works the same in SwiftUI and on the web:

PennyView(move: .hello)

<mascot-view src="/penny" state="hello">

six looks

the look is chosen from who your app is for and how it should feel:

→ soft clay

→ plush

→ fuzzy

→ designer toy

→ flat line

→ retro sticker

no openai account? start from 20

if you don't have an OpenAI account, there are 20 finished mascots, one for each common kind of app, every pose already drawn.

Drop for hydration, Penny for savings, Luna for sleep, Hoot for learning, Byte for an AI helper, Biscuit for pets, and 14 more.

the skill picks the one that means the most for your app, renames it and recolours it to your brand.

what you get

→ a 1080p film of your mascot inside an iPhone app and a Mac app, doing every move

→ a character sheet with name, meaning, palette and every pose

→ an Xcode asset catalog at @1x, @2x and @3x, plus a SwiftUI view (iOS 17+, macOS 14+)

→ one script and a folder for the web, same moves to the frame

→ nine poses as transparent 1024px PNGs

→ a guide for where each move goes: onboarding, empty states, waits, wins, errors, tips, thank you

so you see your mascot living in a real app before you write a single line of code.

how to get it

it's free.

download it here: www.tobiadonadon.com/projects/construct/material/setups/mascot-designer

open a terminal in the folder, type claude, then hi. it installs itself

in your app's folder, type "make a mascot for my app"

give your app someone to love, and send me what you make.
