# I told two home service owners this week not to hire me.

## Post

I told two home service owners this week not to hire me.

Both had the budget ready and neither was a fit.

The reasons why apply to a lot of owners reading this.

---

UNDER $2M IN REVENUE

At $3K to $4K a month with a 4 to 8 month ramp, SEO retainer math punishes a smaller company. Every dollar at that stage should go where it returns fast:

> Run more ads. Whatever channel is already producing, put more money into it. Boring advice that outperforms every clever alternative.

> Pick channels with quick payback on purpose. Collection cycles in the trades are rarely fast, so the marketing you buy needs to return this month, not next spring.

> Stack reviews the whole time. Reviews are the one thing no agency can do for you. We can build the system and the automations, but the asking happens on your jobs, and every review you bank now makes every future channel work better.

Cross $2M with a sales process that converts and the picture changes. Around that size you have the bandwidth to support an investment that's a longer-term play, and that's when SEO stops burning runway and starts compounding.

---

WHEN LEADS AREN'T THE PROBLEM

I've audited shops where qualified calls doubled and revenue still dropped, because 40+ leads never got booked and one estimator was closing at 10%. More marketing pours water into a leaking bucket. Any agency that takes your money without checking booking and close rates first is selling, not diagnosing.

---

WHEN AN AGENCY IS THE RIGHT CALL

Past $2M, sales process converting, and you want the compounding channel without hiring, managing, and training for it yourself. At that point you're buying speed and pattern recognition from people running this play across dozens of markets at once. That's where we earn our fee, and it's the only situation where I want someone paying it.
