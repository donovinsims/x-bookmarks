# Stop trying to rank for "plumber near me."

## Post

Stop trying to rank for "plumber near me."

It doesn't work the way you think.

I see contractors stuffing "near me" into their homepage title, H1, and meta description.

"Best Plumber Near Me | 24/7 Emergency Plumber Near Me | Call Now"

This looks spammy AND it doesn't help your rankings.

Here's why:

Google handles "near me" searches differently.

When someone types "plumber near me," Google uses their location to find nearby businesses.

The results are based on proximity (on top of trust and relevance), not whether you put "near me" on your website.

A plumber 2 miles away with zero "near me" keywords will outrank you if you're 10 miles away.

Due not only to having more reviews or citations, but proximity.

What to do instead:

Target your actual city name.

"Plumber in Austin, TX" beats "Plumber Near Me" for two reasons:

1. You can actually rank for city-specific keywords through on-page SEO.

2. It doesn't make your site look like keyword-stuffed spam.

Build pages for each city you serve.

/austin-plumber
/round-rock-plumber
/cedar-park-plumber

Google will show these pages to "near me" searchers in those areas automatically.

You don't need to tell Google you're "near" anyone.

Just tell them exactly where you are (and where you serve).
