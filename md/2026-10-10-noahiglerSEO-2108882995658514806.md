# We're testing a new box on a couple of HVAC websites, and it's crushing.

## Post

We're testing a new box on a couple of HVAC websites, and it's crushing.

It says "Same-day availability. Next available appointment: 1:00 PM," with a big Book Online button right under it.

Here's the full first screen we build on mobile, piece by piece:

A homeowner on a phone decides in a few seconds whether to call you or go back to Google. The first screen has to show what you do, where you do it, why they should trust you, and how to book, all before they scroll and all big enough to tap with a thumb.

1. Header: your logo and a big call button

The call button sits top right, where a thumb can reach it, and opens the dialer with one tap. Skip the full menu up here. A menu icon is enough.

2. Rating bar: your real Google numbers

"Rated 4.7 · 12,700 Google reviews" with the stars, right under the header. Use your actual numbers. A vague "5-star service" badge doesn't tell anyone anything. If you don't have many reviews yet, pair your rating with one strong recent review.

3. Headline: your services and your market

"AC Repair & Installation in [City]." It tells the homeowner they're in the right place, and it's the page's H1, so it helps you rank for those searches too. A slogan like "Your Comfort Is Our Mission" tells them nothing.

4. Two reassurances

Licensed and insured, same-day service, upfront pricing, a real guarantee. Pick the two your customers care about most. Six icons in a row turn into background noise.

5. Two sentences

Who you are, and what happens when they call or book. "Family-owned since 1998. A real person picks up 24/7 and confirms your visit." Anything longer can go below the fold.

6. The availability box

"Same-day availability. Next available appointment: 1:00 PM." Under it, a big Book Online button that goes straight into ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro, so they can pick a time without calling.

It doesn't need a live connection to your CRM. A simple rule that shows the next window your team can actually honor does the job.
