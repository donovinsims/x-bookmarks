# This AC repair company invested $32k with us.

## Post

This AC repair company invested $32k with us.

They booked $580k in jobs from Google Maps.

Here's what we actually did.

When they came to us, the profile looked like most do. Generic setup, one broad category, a handful of old photos, and reviews trickling in whenever someone remembered to ask.

We rebuilt it around how Google actually ranks profiles.

> Changed the primary category to Air Conditioning Repair Service instead of HVAC Contractor, so they'd rank better for AC repair searches. HVAC Contractor became the secondary category.

> Built a review system their techs actually use. They went from averaging 12 reviews a month to over 45 per month, steady weekly reviews that mention the service and the city instead of a big batch once a year.

> Removed their general HVAC services page and built out individual pages for each main service. AC repair, AC installation, emergency AC, each with its own page, so Google gets a clear answer about what they do instead of one page trying to rank for everything.

> Turned their main services into product cards with photos, descriptions, and price ranges, so Google understands exactly what they sell and the cards take up screen space that would otherwise show competitors.

> Got backlinks from every one of their suppliers, which jumped their organic rankings within 5 to 6 months.

> Uploaded real job photos from the field every single month. Zero stock images.

None of this is complicated. It's just done completely, in the right order, and maintained every month while the competition sets their profile up once and forgets it exists.

They put in $32k and took out $580k, and their GBP is only starting to compound.
