# A plumbing company came to us ranking outside the top 10 on Google Maps for their core services.

## Post

A plumbing company came to us ranking outside the top 10 on Google Maps for their core services.

8 months later they rank #1 in the Map Pack and ChatGPT recommends their business first.

Here's what we did:

1. GBP overhaul.

> Primary category was wrong. Fixed it to match the top 3 in their Map Pack.
> Filled out every service with detailed descriptions.
> Uploaded 10+ real job site photos in the first month.
> Started posting weekly with before/after content (not a massive needle mover)
> Rebuilt citations in 50+ directories. Their NAP was inconsistent everywhere.

2. Review system.

> They were getting 3 to 4 reviews a month. We built a system around text follow-ups and QR code cards.
> They now average ~22 a month with keyword-rich content. Customers mention specific services and neighborhoods.
> Every reply includes service + city naturally.

3. Website rebuild.

> Went from one generic services page to dedicated pages for every core service.
> Built location pages for every suburb they serve. Each one unique, 500+ words, targeting real local keywords.
> Added FAQ sections to every major page answering the exact questions homeowners type into ChatGPT and Google.
> Hero section rebuilt for conversion. Big call button, reviews, reassurances above the fold.

4. Link building + listicles.

> Local organization partnerships, supplier backlinks, industry directories. These links are a total pain to build but are also the most impactful for local rankings.
> Wrote quite a few "best plumber in [city]" listicles that started ranking on pages 1 and 2 of Google.
> Published their listicles through press release distribution on news sites.

5. AI search visibility.

> The combination of FAQ content, listicle placements, review content, and press releases got them recommended on ChatGPT within 4 months.
> Made sure Yelp and Bing profiles were complete and accurate (among other citations).

They now rank #1 for their highest-value services in the Map Pack, show up in AI Overviews, and get recommended by ChatGPT by name.

Lead volume from Google has more than tripled!
