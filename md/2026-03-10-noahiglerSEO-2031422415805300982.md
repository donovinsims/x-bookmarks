# Met a contractor with over 900 Google reviews who doesn't even have a website linked to his GBP.

## Post

Met a contractor with over 900 Google reviews who doesn't even have a website linked to his GBP.

Still ranks top 3 in his market.

I looked at his profile expecting to find some genius strategy behind it.

There isn't one.

He does great work and has been consistently getting reviews the entire time.

No content strategy or intense link building. And their website isn't even linked to the GBP because they are worried it will suspend the profile.

And he's outranking companies who have spent tens of thousands on web design and SEO.

Here's what that actually tells you.

Google reviews and your Google Business Profile carry an insane amount of weight for local rankings. More than most people realize. If you have a massive volume of reviews with strong velocity and your GBP is set up correctly... you can rank without a lot of the other stuff that agencies tell you is mandatory.

Now do I think he's leaving money on the table by not linking his website?

Absolutely.

He's only allowing people to call in, but many people prefer to submit a website contact form.

But his profile proves something that a lot of business owners need to hear: If you're stressing about your website, your blog, your backlinks, and all the other stuff... but you only have 40 Google reviews and you're getting 2 a month... you're focused on the wrong thing.

Reviews first, everything else second.
