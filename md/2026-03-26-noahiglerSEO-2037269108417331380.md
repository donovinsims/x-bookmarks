# Google doesn't rank local businesses by who has the most reviews.

## Post

Google doesn't rank local businesses by who has the most reviews.

I know that sounds wrong. But I watch/help businesses with 50 reviews outrank businesses with 300+ every day.

Here's how Google actually decides who shows up first in the Map Pack:

There are 3 ranking factors that matter.

Most contractors only know about one of them.

First is relevance.

This is whether Google thinks your business matches what the person searched for. Your primary GBP category is the biggest lever here.

If someone searches "ac repair near me" and your category is "HVAC contractor" but your competitor's is "air conditioning repair service"... they have an automatic advantage.

Your services list, your website content, and even your business name all feed into relevance too.

Second is reputation.

This is where reviews live, but it's not JUST reviews. It's also your backlinks, your citations across the web, how long you've been in business, and your review velocity (how many new reviews you're getting per month).

A business with 80 reviews getting 10 a month will often outrank a business with 300 reviews getting 1 a month. Google cares about what's happening NOW, not what happened 3 years ago.

Third is proximity.

This is the one you can't fully control. If your competitor's GBP address is physically closer to the searcher, Google gives them an advantage.

You can expand your ranking radius by nailing the first two factors... but you can't beat someone who's a mile closer if everything else is equal.

The fix isn't always more reviews (though those help). It's checking your categories, filling out your GBP completely, and being honest about whether your location is working against you.
