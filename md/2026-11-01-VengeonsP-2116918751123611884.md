# your primary GBP category is what you sell most. not the one that sounds closest

## Post

your primary GBP category is what you sell most. not the one that sounds closest

add only the secondary categories you actually sell. three to five is plenty

fill every attribute. scroll to the bottom. yes or no on each one

write every service into your profile, with two or three sentences under each

use the full 750 characters of the description. say what you do and where

show your real address if customers come to you. a service area if you go to them

link your profile to the page for its city, not to your home page

keep your hours true. mark holidays closed before Google asks

upload real photos of real jobs every month. no stock

send customers the direct review link, not your profile. put it on a QR code at the counter

ask within 24 hours of the job. follow up once, then stop

ask them to name the service in the review. "fixed our boiler" beats "great service"

answer every review. the bad ones first, within 48 hours

GBP posts barely move rankings. post when you have news, then get back to reviews

your profile ranks for one or two keywords. local pages win the rest

one page per service per city. one template, real details on each

only build the cities you actually serve. ten real pages beat 200 empty ones

put the service and the city in the title tag and the H1

write the way customers say it on the phone, not the way your trade says it

read your competitors' reviews. the words their customers use are your keywords

a real photo of the job on every city page, its file name saying what it shows

put your reviews on the page, with the name and the date

LocalBusiness schema on every location page, with the same name, address and phone as your profile

same name, same address, same phone on every listing. letter for letter

every service page two clicks from the home page

a services hub and a locations hub, each linking to every page of its kind

one intent, one page. two pages with the same intent and angle fight each other

every blog post links to its service page, the service as the anchor

a page no internal link points to gets found late, if ever

pick www or non-www and use that exact version on every listing

check Search Console for 404s every month. redirect the ones that had links

your first links: local press, suppliers, sponsors, your trade association. earned, never bought

google your brand name. what shows there is your second home page

ask ChatGPT for the best [service] in [city]. the pages it cites are the lists you need to be on

if your business has a season, publish three months before it

the quote form is one tap away on mobile. every extra click costs you a lead

track calls and form fills, not rankings. rankings don't pay you

do this for six months. most of your competitors won't do it for six weeks
