# Here's exactly how I rank home service businesses #1 on ChatGPT and Google AI Mode.

## Post

Here's exactly how I rank home service businesses #1 on ChatGPT and Google AI Mode.

(Bookmark this)

Most home service business owners aren't even thinking about AI search yet (not everyone is on Twitter like us). But people are starting to use ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Mode to find local service providers. And the companies showing up in those results right now are getting some of the easiest calls possible.

Here's the exact playbook we use for our clients:

1. Get your SEO fundamentals right first

None of the advanced stuff matters if your website foundation is weak. You need clean site structure with dedicated service pages for every core service you offer, strong on-page SEO with the right keywords in your titles, headers, and meta descriptions, and really solid page content that actually answers the questions your customers are asking.

AI models pull directly from web pages that Google already considers authoritative. If your site is missing the basics, you'll have trouble showing up super well in LLMs.

2. Add FAQ sections to your service pages

This one is huge and almost nobody does it WELL. Add a detailed FAQ section to every major service page on your site. Think about the exact questions a homeowner would type into ChatGPT... "how much does a roof replacement cost in Dallas" or "how long does it take to repipe a house."

When your page directly answers those questions in a clear Q&A format, AI models are significantly more likely to pull from your site when someone asks that same question. Write them the way a real person would actually ask, not the way an SEO agency would keyword-stuff them.

3. Publish monthly press releases

Every press release should tie your business name to your core service and city. "Dallas Roofing Company Completes 500th Roof Replacement" type of stuff. These get picked up by news sites and syndicated across the web, which gives AI models a TON of fresh, authoritative content to pull from when someone asks for recommendations.

More recently, pushing out research-style articles on news sites has been CRUSHING for our clients. Even if you think it doesn't make sense for your industry, believe me, it doesn't matter.

4. Create "Best [Service] in [City]" listicle articles

Publish these on your own blog but also in press releases (not the same article). Write a legitimate article about the best providers in your area and include your company in it (first of course). AI models pull heavily from listicle-style content when answering recommendation queries. If your business name keeps showing up in these articles across multiple sources, you're training the model to associate your brand with that service and location.

5. Get featured in local and industry publications

Guest posts, interviews, local news features, industry blogs. Every mention of your business on a third-party site with real authority feeds into how AI models understand your company. The more places your business name shows up in context with your service and city, the stronger the association becomes.

6. Build up your Reddit and forum presence

AI models pull heavily from Reddit. Find your local subreddit and industry-specific subreddits. Answer questions, provide value, mention your company naturally when relevant. If someone asks "who's the best plumber in [city]" on Reddit and your company gets mentioned multiple times... ChatGPT is going to pick that up.

Go get some aged Reddit accounts and start posting in local subreddits from your "customers" perspectives.

7. Stack Google reviews with keyword-rich content

Reviews that mention specific services and locations carry weight. "They replaced our entire roof in 2 days, best roofing company in Dallas" gives AI models way more to work with than "great service, 5 stars." Encourage your customers to be specific about what you did and where.

Google reviews still remain king for all around online presence. Higher velocity is arguably more important than total number or star rating (assuming those are comparable to competitors).

8. Branded search volume

This one ties everything together. The more people searching for your business by name on Google, the more AI models interpret your company as a trusted, well-known brand. Social media presence, truck wraps, yard signs, all of it feeds branded search. And branded search is one of the strongest signals for both Google rankings AND AI recommendations.

The companies investing in this right now have almost zero competition. This window won't stay open forever, but right now it's incredibly ripe for the picking.

About 85% of our clients rank top 3 on LLMs for most search terms. It's a result of mass-testing new AI strategies and many months of great SEO.
