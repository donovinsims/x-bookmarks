## Post

every speed to lead bot needs a qualify. md file (at minimum):

who we serve

-industries
-company size
-order volume or revenue band
-geography

who we don't

-the 3 lead types that waste the most time
-what to say when one shows up

the 3 questions

-the only things the bot needs before booking a call
-written in the owner's words

handoff rules

-what gets booked automatically
-what gets a human first
-what gets a polite no

tone

-how the owner actually writes
-2 real emails they've sent

The bot is 10% of the build. The context is the other 90%.
