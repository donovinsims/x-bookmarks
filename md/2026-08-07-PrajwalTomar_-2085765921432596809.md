# One Claude skill just KILLED a $79/mo subscription and nobody's talking about it.

## Post

One Claude skill just KILLED a $79/mo subscription and nobody's talking about it.

Higgsfield is basically a wrapper on image models you can hit directly. One /generate skill in Claude Code + a pay-as-you-go key and you only pay for what you make.

Images stay on your machine. Prompts get logged. You own the output.

The skill does 4 jobs:

→ picks the cheapest working provider
→ writes the prompts from your brand refs
→ generates the images or video
→ saves everything locally so nothing disappears when you cancel

GPT Image 2 at 2K was ~5¢ on the cheap aggregator vs ~31-34¢ inside the subscription.

That's the whole game.

At this point I'm pretty sure if you already live in Claude Code, paying a monthly creative wrapper is just burning money. Build the skill once and stop renting the middleman.
