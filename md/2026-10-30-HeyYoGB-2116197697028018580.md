# How to Build Your Own AI Content Team With Grok Bot + Typefully

## Post

grok bot can write your posts all day. on its own, it cant schedule them or post them.

so the content dies in a chat or a doc. you end up copy pasting every draft into X, then LinkedIn, then Substack, by hand, or it just sits there.

typefully is the connector that fixes that. grok bot drafts straight into typefully for X, LinkedIn, and Substack Notes, and you review, schedule, and press publish in one place. no moving text between apps by hand.

to be clear about how mine runs: the bot drafts, i approve and publish. it never auto posts. nothing goes live until ive read it.

heres the whole setup, written so you can copy it. if you want to follow along, grab typefully here: https://typefully.com/?via=HeyYoGB

this is for you if youre already using grok bot (or about to) and you want one place where drafts land, a loop that tells you what actually worked, and zero chance an agent posts something you didnt read.

What You'll Have When You're Done

by the end of this youll have:

X, LinkedIn, and Substack Notes connected on one typefully social set

grok bot connected to typefully, so agents can drop drafts straight into it

a small team of jobs, each with one task: read the numbers, write short posts, adapt for LinkedIn, write long-form

a feedback loop where your published posts decide the next angles

a weekly rhythm that tells you what to open and when

and one rule holding all of it together: the bot drafts, you publish.

honest limit up front: this is a prep system. it saves the blank-page part and the organizing part. it doesnt replace your taste, and it doesnt grow an account just because its connected.

The Setup

grok bot has its own computer and can run jobs on a schedule. typefully is where drafts wait, get edited, and get posted. connect the two and you get a desk instead of a chat history.

each job is basically a small bot with one task. one reads what performed. one writes short posts from that. one turns X posts into LinkedIn posts. one writes the long stuff. they hand off through dated files in one folder, then the writers create drafts in typefully.

this is the same capture → create → compound loop i use for everything. capture what worked, create from it, compound by feeding the results back in.

the reason it matters: posting straight from a chat works once. then the draft gets buried, theres no queue, and you cant compare what you planned against what went out.
