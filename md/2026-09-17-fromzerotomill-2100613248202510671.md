# If I had to start again from 0 followers, here's exactly what I'd do.

## Post

If I had to start again from 0 followers, here's exactly what I'd do.

I've written over 20,000 posts on this platform, generated tens of millions of views and made tens of thousands of dollars from it.

1. Pick a niche with a real TAM.

This kills most accounts before they post. Ur niche needs enough people in it who have money AND are on this platform.

"Helping local plumbers" fails both. There aren't enough of them here and the deal sizes are small.

Agency owners, coaches, ecom founders, SaaS, clipping, AI: big populations, active on X, real budgets.

2. Pick one lane and stay in it for a year.

X sorts accounts by topic. Post about five things and it never works out who to show u to. This alone kills more accounts than bad writing.

3. Post 2-3 times a day from day one.

Not because volume is magic. Because most posts won't land, and X shows every post to a small test group of strangers regardless of ur follower count. More posts, more tests.

4. Write everything to be forwarded.

The only question before u post: would somebody send this to another person?

A like is worth almost nothing. A repost is worth around 40 of them. Nobody has ever forwarded a nice thought. They forward numbers, decisions, and takes they think are wrong.

5. Never leave a post alone.

X judges it on the first half hour. Answer every comment with a real answer, because each reply feeds the same post while it's being measured.

Then quote retweet ur own post the same day with a different angle. One post runs for a week instead of four hours.

6. Write one Article a week.

Almost nobody does. The slot is empty. And one Article gives u seven quote retweets, so it's the highest leverage thing u can write.

7. Post ur first result the week u get it.

Whatever it is. Small is fine. One real result posted immediately does more than six months of teaching.

8. Reply to every DM immediately.

None of the above is where u get paid. Nobody sends u $5K without talking to u first, and interest has a shelf life of about an hour.

9. Do it for 90 days before u judge anything.

Author authority builds with time on the platform. Month one is uphill. Month four is the algorithm carrying u.

That's it. No hacks. Nothing here requires talent.

The reason it works from zero is that the platform doesn't care who u are. It cares whether strangers stop.
