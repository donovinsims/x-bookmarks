# Between 6pm and 9pm is when most homeowners start searching for services.

## Post

Between 6pm and 9pm is when most homeowners start searching for services.

It's also when most home service companies STOP answering the phone.

We built an agent that handles every after-hours lead automatically.

Here's how it works:

When a lead comes in after hours through any channel, the agent picks it up within 60 seconds. It reads their message, identifies the service they need, and responds with a relevant follow-up question via BLUE texts to keep them engaged.

If someone fills out a form at 8pm saying their AC stopped working, the agent responds with something like "How old is your unit? Is it blowing warm air or not turning on at all?" It's gathering qualifying info while the lead is still warm and actively looking for help.

When the office opens the next morning, the team doesn't have a list of cold leads to chase. They have warm conversations already in progress with all the qualifying details they need to book the estimate.

Most companies handle after-hours leads one of two ways.

1. They let everything go to voicemail and call people back the next day.
2. They use a generic auto-reply that says "thanks for reaching out, we'll get back to you during business hours."

Both lose jobs.

By the time you call someone back at 8am, they've already booked with the company that responded at 8:01pm the night before.

The data from our first clients using this is pretty clear.

After-hours leads that get an immediate, personalized response convert at nearly the same rate as leads that come in during business hours. Leads that sit until the next morning drop off significantly.

The math is simple. If you're generating 30+ leads a month and even 30% of them come in after hours, that's 9 to 10 leads you're probably losing every month. Multiply that by your average job value and that's likely more than enough revenue to justify building a system around it.

Your competitors close at 5pm. THAT'S your opportunity.
