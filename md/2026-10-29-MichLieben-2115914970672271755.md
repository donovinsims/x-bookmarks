# Your TAM shows you the whole market. Your TAL tells you who to email first.

## Post

Your TAM shows you the whole market. Your TAL tells you who to email first.

Here's how to build both in Claude Code, from your CRM to the first send, in 6 steps:

1. Start in your CRM

Your closed-won and closed-lost deals already show who buys and who doesn't, which also tells you who to leave out.

Ask Claude Code to pull both from your CRM (we use folk) and compare them on industry, size, region, tech stack and who signed. That's your ICP model (or at least the simplified version of it).

2. Build the full account list

Go wide here, on purpose. Segmenting & tiering come next, so for now you want every company that could fit.

Cast a broad net in ColdIQ data first. Lookups are unlimited, so over-including costs nothing. Then access standalone providers within our unified API for extra coverage (Prospeo, AI Ark, Apollo...).

Remove current customers and open deals before anything else, to avoid cold emailing them by mistake.

That list is your TAM (total addressable market).

3. Split it into segments

Tag every account by region, industry and company size, which gives you segments like UK Tech or DACH Services. Then have AI validate those filters. Is it SaaS or agency? Self-serve or sales-led? SMB or enterprise buyers?

If you sell to both a US SaaS and a German manufacturer, they can't get the same email. Each segment gets its own offer later on.

4. Pick a path per segment

Path A is for the big segments: blast first. A free offer goes to the whole segment and you only enrich the ones who reply. I'd recommend Instantly for email & Expandi for LinkedIn.

Path B is the other way around, for when every account counts: enrich first. Filter the segment down, add intent signals & tech stack data (PredictLeads, BuiltWith), then enrich everyone left.

5. Score every account

Have Claude score each account against your ICP model and sort it into 3 tiers. This is where your TAM becomes your TAL (target account list).

T1 gets priority on every channel.
T2 gets volume, cold email only.
T3 gets nurtured and re-scored every month, which is where an unlimited data endpoint comes in.

6. Find the right people

3 personas per account, in the best case: who signs (the decision maker), who uses it (the champion) and who weighs in (the deal influencer).

Enrich the people with unlimited data first. Then get their emails, and even phone numbers if necessary, from single providers in our waterfall (Prospeo, FullEnrich, Findymail...).

T1 × Persona 1, the decision maker, gets the first email.

Hope this helps you build a TAM/TAL you can (finally) trust.
