# speed-to-lead is one of the clearest AI services you can sell.

## Post

speed-to-lead is one of the clearest AI services you can sell.

the average response time in B2B sales is 42 hours. If you (or your client) can cut that response time to 5 minutes, they're 21x more likely to qualify the lead.

(real stat - Harvard did a study. Google it).

the 80/20 of a speed to lead workflow:

one: capture the inquiry
two: qualify intent
three: send a relevant first response
four: assign an owner
five: create the follow-up task
six: report response time

what your'e selling is a faster, measurable path from inquiry to sales conversation.
