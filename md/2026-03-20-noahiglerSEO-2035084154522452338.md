# The homepage hero section on 90% of home service websites is killing their conversion rate.

## Post

The homepage hero section on 90% of home service websites is killing their conversion rate.

I audit SMB sites every day. 

Here's what I usually see: a stock photo of a smiling family, the company name in big letters, and a tagline like "Quality Service You Can Trust" or "Your Local Experts Since 2005."

None of that converts. The homeowner who just landed on your site from Google has one question: "Can these people help me with my problem right now?" If your hero section doesn't answer that in 2 to 3 seconds, they're hitting the back button.

Here's what should be in your hero section:

A big click-to-call button. Not tucked in the corner of the navigation bar. A large, obvious button that says "Call Now" or "Get a Free Estimate." On mobile, this needs to be impossible to miss. Most of your visitors are on their phone.

Who you are, what you do, and where. Not a clever tagline. Something clear like "Portland's Top-Rated Plumbing Company" or "HVAC Installation & Repair in Dallas." The visitor should immediately know they're in the right place.

Reassurances. Licensed, insured, years in business, number of Google reviews, any industry awards or certifications. These need to be visible immediately, not buried on an about page nobody visits.

Your Google review rating. Pull your star rating and review count directly into the hero section. "4.9 Stars - 340+ Google Reviews" builds instant trust. Most of your competitors don't do this on their homepage.

Below the hero, the page should flow into your services, then reviews/testimonials, then your process (how it works in 3 steps), then an about section. Every section should have a CTA.

This isn't complicated. But most home service websites are still running a hero section that was designed to look pretty instead of designed to CONVERT visitors into calls.

If your website gets 1,000 visitors a month and converts at 1%, that's 10 leads.

Fix the hero section and the page structure and push that to 4 to 5%, now you're getting 40 to 50 leads from the same traffic.

That's the difference between SEO "not working" and SEO printing money...
