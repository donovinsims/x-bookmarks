# I've reviewed location pages from some of the biggest home service brands in America.

## Post

I've reviewed location pages from some of the biggest home service brands in America.

The best ones all hand Google the same local proof.

> A clear H1 names the service and location.

> NAP matches the connected Google Business Profile, including the tracking number if that's what the profile runs.

> Real projects, reviews, and photos come from that market.

> Local copy covers conditions that actually change the service: weather, permits, housing stock, equipment.

> Internal links connect the page to the relevant service pages.

> A sticky call or booking bar stays visible on mobile. Insane amount of CTAs as well (strategically placed).

Google needs a page built around the market, not a template with the city swapped.
