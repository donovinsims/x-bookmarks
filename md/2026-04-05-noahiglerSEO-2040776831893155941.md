# A garage door company came to us stuck at position 8 on Google Maps.

## Post

A garage door company came to us stuck at position 8 on Google Maps.

We got them to #2 in 9 weeks by fixing 3 things.

Their primary category was set to "Handyman/Handywoman." Google had no idea they were a garage door company, so it wasn't showing them for any of the searches that actually bring in calls. We changed the category to match their core service. Immediate improvement.

Second, their business name on Google was their legal LLC name. No mention of garage doors anywhere. Their competitors had "garage door" right in their profile name and were getting a relevance boost on every single search.

We helped them file a DBA and updated the business name to include their target keyword. Completely legal and one of the most underrated ranking factors in local SEO.

Third, they had 39 reviews total and hadn't gotten a new one in 4 months. Google looks at your review activity over the last 90 days more than your total count. A profile with zero recent reviews looks stale.

We got their team asking after every job with a QR code. Within 6 weeks they had picked up another 18 reviews and the velocity alone helped push them from 8th to 2nd.

No new website, better backlinks, or major content marketing. Just three foundational fixes that should have been done from day one.

Sometimes the basics are enough.
