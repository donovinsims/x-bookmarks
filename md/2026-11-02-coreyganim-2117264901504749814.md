# One of our biggest competitive advantages that I rarely talk about:

## Post

One of our biggest competitive advantages that I rarely talk about:

Our Signal Stack.

It's a short list of sources Claude Code/Codex reads every week so that we're always up to date on the market, our competitors, trends, etc.

Here are the sources we use + the setup:

Customers:

-Where our buyers talk about the problems we solve
-Their words
-Their venues
-Their tone

Competitors

-5-10 competitors or substitutes
-What they're shipping
-What they're charging
-What they're hiring for

Market

-Regulatory shifts
-Sector economics
-Events that change what buyers prioritize

Internal

-CRM
-Fathom call transcripts
-Slack
-Customer feedback

Every source has to pass 3 tests:

-Specific: covers our problem space, not "AI" generally
-Recent: not old/outdated
-Decision-relevant: we can make informed decisions based on the data

The setup:

1) We have Claude read our business context files
2) We had it interview us about our market (10 questions)
3) It then sorts our sources into 4 buckets, each with a one-line "why this exists"
4) DON'T SKIP THIS STEP: It cuts every source without a "why"
5) We schedule a brief for Monday at 8am
6) It gets a monthly check for dead URLs, drifted sources, and duplicates

Highly recommend you set this up for yourself, also something good to set up for clients for an additional fee or just a value-add on top of your existing service(s).
