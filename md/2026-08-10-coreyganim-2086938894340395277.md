# want to find businesses that need an AI service?

## Post

want to find businesses that need an AI service?

look for visible bottlenecks:

1: Google reviews mentioning slow responses or missed follow-up
2: website forms that collect almost no useful context
3: downloadable PDFs still used for intake
4: quote requests that take days to answer

find the leak, offer a focused assessment, upsell the fix.
