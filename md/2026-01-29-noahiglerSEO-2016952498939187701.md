# Your competitor has 47 reviews.

## Post

Your competitor has 47 reviews.

You have 230.

They still outrank you, here's why:

Total review count is only one piece of the puzzle. Google weighs several other factors just as heavily, and sometimes more.

The first thing I'd check is proximity. This is one of Google's biggest ranking factors for local search. If their GBP is 2 miles from the searcher and yours is 8 miles away, they're probably going to win. Doesn't matter how many reviews you have if you're physically further from the person searching.

The second thing is review velocity. Google cares way more about your last 90 days than your total count. If they're getting 15 reviews per month and you're getting 3, they look more active and trustworthy to Google. You might have 230 total, but if most of those came four years ago, they're not helping you much anymore.

Third is their business name. If their GBP name is "Johnson's AC Repair Dallas" and yours is "Johnson's Heating and Cooling," they're going to outrank you for AC repair searches in Dallas. It's not fair, but it's how the algorithm works. You can do this legally with a DBA.

Fourth is their connected landing page. Your GBP probably links to your homepage that mentions 12 different cities. Theirs might link to a page specifically about their primary city with the service and location in the H1, local neighborhoods mentioned, and NAP matching their GBP exactly. Google sees theirs as more relevant to local searches.

Stop obsessing over review count alone. Fix the other factors and watch your rankings climb.
