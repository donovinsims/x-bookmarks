# Most GBPs I audit don't need a secret trick.

## Post

Most GBPs I audit don't need a secret trick.

They need someone to finish the setup and keep the information accurate.

This is the checklist I use.

FOUNDATION

> Make sure the owner controls the profile and at least one trusted manager has access
> Use the real business name shown on the building, vehicles, website, and legal documents
> Keep the address or service area accurate instead of stretching it into places the business does not serve
> Set the real phone number, website, appointment link, and business hours
> Only advertise 24/7 hours when someone can answer or book the customer

RELEVANCE

> Pick the primary category that matches the actual service you most want to grow
> Add secondary categories only when the company truly performs that work
> List every real service separately and write a short description for each one
> Use the business description to explain the main service, market, and what makes the company different
> Complete the attributes that accurately apply to the business

THE PAGE CONNECTED TO THE PROFILE

> Link each GBP to the strongest page for that exact location, not one generic homepage for the whole company
> Match the business name, address, and phone number on that page to the profile
> Put the main services, service area, real reviews, photos, and a clear call button on the page
> Give every location its own useful content instead of copying one template across the metro

PHOTOS AND POSTS

> Upload real jobs, technicians, vehicles, equipment, and before-and-after photos throughout the month
> Keep the logo and cover image clean enough that the profile looks like the same brand customers see offline
> Use Google posts to show current work and link the Learn More button to the relevant service or location page

REVIEWS

> Build the ask into the job closeout instead of hoping the customer remembers later
> Make the link easy to open with a text, QR code, or NFC card
> Respond to every review in a normal voice and mention the actual work when it fits
> Track review volume by month and by location so a new GBP does not get ignored

LOCAL TRUST

> Make the main directories match the GBP exactly, including the tracking number used on the profile
> Clean up old listings, duplicate profiles, and wrong phone numbers that confuse customers and Google
> Keep the same core business information across Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing, BBB, and trade-specific directories

TRACKING

> Add UTM tags to the website link so GBP traffic shows up correctly in Analytics
> Use call tracking without breaking the number consistency across the web
> Track calls, forms, bookings, and revenue from the profile
> Run map scans from different parts of the market instead of checking rankings from the office

Do this before touching anything obscure.

Most profiles I audit need cleanup and ownership before they need an advanced tactic.
