# everyone in AI is fighting over the same 10,000 customers.

## Post

everyone in AI is fighting over the same 10,000 customers.

meanwhile there's a plumber in your city doing $2M a year who's never heard the word "agent."

that's the whole opportunity. the AI niche is crowded, disloyal, and churns the day a new tool drops. the boring trades one town over have demand that shows up every single day...and competitors who can't spell SEO.

you don't have to invent a market. you point the skills you already have at one that's been sitting there the whole time.

two ways to spend your AI skills 👇
