# Google just accidentally revealed how AI Mode picks which local businesses to recommend.

## Post

Google just accidentally revealed how AI Mode picks which local businesses to recommend.

We’ve been doing exactly this for months.

Here’s how we got one local business showing up across 4,000+ searches in ChatGPT and Google’s AI Mode

If you run a local business and want to see how you show up in ChatGPT and AI Mode, book a call and we’ll look at it together: 

http://alventramarketing.com

1. AI doesn't give you 10 results.

Google gives you 10 links. ChatGPT and AI Mode give you one or two names.

If you're not one of them, you don't rank lower. You just don't exist in that moment. No impression, no click, no call.

2. It's already happening.

People are typing things like:

"Who's the best plumber near me?”
 "Who should I call for roof repair?"
 "Best auto repair shop in Dallas?"

And AI answers with real businesses. Just not always yours.

3. AI doesn't rank, it repeats.

When ChatGPT has to give one answer, it doesn't guess. It goes with the business that feels safest to recommend.

And safe, to an AI, just means familiar. It has seen that business in a lot of places, described the same way.

So the question stops being "how do I rank?" and becomes "where does AI keep seeing my business?"

4. The auto shop.

This was a local auto repair shop. Good work, decent reviews, and basically invisible whenever someone asked an AI who to call.

Their Google rankings weren't the problem. The problem was that when AI went looking for an answer, they weren't in any of the places it pulls from.

5. What AI pulls from: listicles.

"5 Best Auto Repair Shops in Dallas.”
"Top 5 HVAC Companies in Phoenix."
"Best Plumbers in Austin."

These pages already answer the exact question the user is asking, and they do the comparing for them.

To an AI model, a listicle isn't content. It's a verdict.

Ahrefs found that "best" and comparison pages make up 43.8% of the pages cited in ChatGPT answers. That's the page type AI trusts most when it has to pick.

6. What we did: Entity Injection.

We got the shop into multiple "Best auto repair shops in [city]" pages, on different publishers, not one site.

Same business, same core facts, slightly different wording each time.

The goal wasn't backlinks. It was memory. We wanted AI to see the same name, in the same context, in lots of places, until recommending them felt like the obvious answer.

7. Then we fixed their own site.

Their website only sold. It never compared.

We built pages that explained why people choose them, referenced the alternatives honestly, and used the same language as the lists. That way what AI found on their site matched what it had already seen elsewhere.

When the story is the same everywhere, it's easy to repeat.

8. The result.

Across 4,000+ searches on ChatGPT and Google's AI Mode, that shop now gets recommended.

Same business, same shop floor. The only thing that changed was how often AI sees them, and where.

9. Why this matters for your business.

Nobody sends you a warning when this starts happening. Your Google rankings can look fine while AI sends your calls to someone else.

You don't get a drop in the dashboard. You just get fewer calls and no explanation.

What to do about it:

- Make your name, address and services identical everywhere they appear

- Get listed on real "Best [service] in [city]" pages

- Build one clear page per service per city

- Put real proof on those pages: jobs, photos, reviews, neighborhoods

- Check regularly who AI actually names in your city, and why

Anyone can ask ChatGPT who it would recommend. The hard part is knowing why your competitor gets picked and what to change to flip it.
