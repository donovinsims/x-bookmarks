# If You Want to Rank on Google, Post on Social Media

## Post

Social media used to be completely separate from search rankings.

That changed about six months ago.

Google started pulling social content directly into search results. And the ripple effects are bigger than most local business owners realize.

If you want to rank higher on Google Maps and in local search, posting on social media is no longer optional.

Here is exactly why this matters and how to use it.

Google Now Shows Social Content in Search Results

This is the surface level change most people have noticed.

Search for almost any topic now and you will see social posts embedded directly in Google results.

TikTok videos. Instagram Reels. YouTube Shorts. YouTube videos. LinkedIn articles. Reddit posts.

Google is actively pulling content from these platforms and displaying it alongside traditional search results.

For local businesses, this creates a new opportunity.

When someone searches for "how to maintain your AC unit" or "signs you need a new roof," your social content can now appear in those results.

But here is the catch.

Optimization still matters. Your video titles, descriptions, hashtags, image text, and even file names influence whether Google pulls your content.

A TikTok titled "Day in the life of a plumber" probably will not rank for anything useful.

A TikTok titled "How to tell if your water heater is failing" has a real shot at appearing in search results.

Same effort. Different outcome based on how you optimize.

This surface level change is interesting. But it is not the real reason social media now impacts your Google rankings.

The Real Reason Social Media Affects Search Rankings

Here is what actually matters.

When you post valuable content consistently, you build a following. Some percentage of those followers will eventually need your services.

When they do, they search for your company by name.

"ABC Plumbing reviews" "ABC Plumbing Austin" "ABC Plumbing phone number"

These are called branded searches. And branded searches are one of the strongest signals you can send to Google.

Here is why.

When people search specifically for your business name, Google sees that as a trust signal. Real people are looking for you by name. That means you are probably legitimate and worth recommending.

This improves several things at once.

Your click through rate goes up because people searching your name are already interested in you specifically.

Your branded authority increases. Google starts associating your business name more strongly with your service and location.

And Google starts distinguishing you from competitors more clearly. You are not just another plumber in Austin. You are the plumber people are actively searching for.

The formula is simple.

People trusting you leads to Google trusting you.

Social media is one of the fastest ways to build that trust at scale.

Every video you post. Every tip you share. Every behind the scenes look at your work. It all compounds into an audience that knows your name.

And when that audience needs your services, they search for you directly.

That search behavior tells Google you matter.

This Is No Longer Theoretical

A year ago, I would have said social media has an indirect effect on search rankings. Maybe. Probably. Hard to measure.

That is no longer the case.

This is now directly influential on how you appear in search results.

Google is pulling social content into search. Branded searches are proven ranking factors. And the businesses building audiences on social are seeing the benefits compound over time.

You do not need to go viral. You do not need millions of followers.

You need to show up consistently with helpful content that makes people remember your name.

When they need a plumber or HVAC tech or roofer, they will search for you specifically.

And Google will notice.
