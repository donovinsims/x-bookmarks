# Don’t look for businesses that “need AI.”

## Post

Don’t look for businesses that “need AI.”

Look for visible demand, then find the process leak close to revenue.

Strong reviews + no obvious booking path is a better prospecting signal than a generic local-business list.

Then WALK IN THE FRONT DOOR and pitch face to face.
