# Every new Google Business Profile we launch goes through the same setup.

## Post

Every new Google Business Profile we launch goes through the same setup.

We've done it 40+ times for home service companies, including for 10+ eight-figure brands.

Here's the whole thing, in order:

Before the profile exists:

> A real office, with the lease in the business name, a permanent sign with the exact business name, and keys to the door. It's still the most reliable way to get a profile verified and keep it live.
> If the name will have a keyword in it, the DBA gets filed first, and that same name goes on the lease, the sign, invoices and citations
> Proof ready before you ask Google to verify: the lease, a utility bill or customer invoice at that address, and a shot list for the verification video

During verification:

> You don't need every field filled in to get verified. On one live video verification, the Google rep wouldn't approve a profile until the owner deleted every service area. Google also called the office to ask whether customers walk in.

Once it's verified:

> Primary category for the trade that office runs, plus secondary categories for its other services
> Every service it runs, each with a real description in the words people search
> Its own tracking number, with the same name, address and number on 50 core directory listings
> A website button that goes to that office's city page, with tracking tags
> Booking turned on and pointed at the booking tool
> Real photos from jobs in that area
> Posts twice a week, each with a Learn More button pointing at a service or city page, which helps Google find those pages
> Reviews built up over a few months: about 15 in month one, 25 to 30 in month two, 30 to 35 in month three, then 40 to 45 a month
