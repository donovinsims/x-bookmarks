# Growing on Twitter gets simple once u understand stages of awareness.

## Post

Growing on Twitter gets simple once u understand stages of awareness.

In any market, the biggest group is people who don't know they have a problem. The smallest group is people who know exactly what they want and are choosing a supplier.

Most people write for the wrong one and wonder why nothing works.

Unaware — the biggest group, and where virality lives. "Ur competitors are getting deals u never hear about." They don't know it's happening to them yet. Broad enough that the stranger test group stops.

Problem aware — they know something's wrong. "U're good at what u do and nobody can find u."

Solution aware — they know content is the fix, they're weighing options. "Posting daily isn't working because nothing lands in the first 30 minutes."

Most aware — smallest group, all the money. "We took a client from 200 to 1,200 followers in 2 months. DMs open."

Here's the mistake.

People post only at the most aware stage, because that's where the money is, and get 300 views because almost nobody in a random test group is there.

Or they post only unaware content, go viral, and sign nobody.

U need both running. Unaware content for reach. Most aware content for revenue. Roughly 70/30.
