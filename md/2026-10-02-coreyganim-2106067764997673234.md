# We're in a bubble on X.

## Post

We're in a bubble on X.

We just assume that the rest of the world knows what Dot, Muse, GrokBot, Hermes, and OpenClaw are.

We're still in the top of the first inning in terms of AI acceptance by the general pop.

If you've spent even 5 minutes tinkering with agents you're ahead of 99.9% of the world.
