# Give me 10 seconds with a home service website on my phone.

## Post

Give me 10 seconds with a home service website on my phone.

The first screen tells me whether it was built to get calls or look expensive.

I check the same things in the same order:

> Service and city are clear without scrolling
> A phone number and booking button are easy to tap
> Reviews, years in business, and the guarantee sit near the main action
> Real work or the real team appears in the main photo
> The form asks for the bare minimum
> Paid traffic lands on the exact service it searched for
> Sticky call or quote buttons stay on screen while the visitor scrolls
> Fast loading holds up on a normal cellular connection

Then I keep scrolling.

The page should answer the next buying questions in plain English:

> The next step after I call
> Pricing or the factors that change it
> Reasons to trust this company inside my house
> Service areas
> Real customer feedback about the work

I don't care how good the desktop animation looks if the mobile page hides the phone number behind a menu.

We rebuilt the site for an electrical company doing about $40k a month.

Their leads roughly doubled, and we don't even run their marketing.

The page stopped making interested people work so hard to become a lead.
