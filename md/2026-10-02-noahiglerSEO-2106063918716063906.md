# Local SEO advice I used to give home service owners that I don't anymore:

## Post

Local SEO advice I used to give home service owners that I don't anymore:

> Post on your Google Business Profile every day. We tested daily posts against no posts for 3 months on big profiles. Rankings didn't move.
> Work keywords into every review reply
> Launch 50 to 150 city pages on day one
> Rely on press releases to get you into AI search

What I tell them now:

> Use GBP posts with a Learn More button to link to the pages you want indexed
> Get reviews that mention the service and the town
> Start with 8 to 10 real city pages with local info, then add more once they're indexed
> Get reviews and full profiles on Yelp, BBB, Angi, and Facebook, since AI tools read them
