# You can figure out exactly how competitive your local market is in about 10 minutes. 

## Post

You can figure out exactly how competitive your local market is in about 10 minutes. 

Here's the process I use:

Step 1: Search your keyword and study the Map Pack

Open an incognito window. Search your main service + your city. Look at the 3 profiles in the Map Pack.

For each one, write down their review count, star rating, whether their GBP name is exact match or a generic brand name, and when their last photo or post was uploaded. If the top 3 have 500+ reviews each, you're in a competitive market. If they have under 100, you might be in the green...

Step 2: Check their GBP names

If the #1 ranked profile has a name like "Dallas Roof Pros" and yours is "Johnson & Sons," you're at a structural disadvantage before you even start.

Exact match names get a relevance signal that generic brand names don't. If 2 out of 3 in your Map Pack are exact match, factor that into your timeline.

Step 3: Check their domain authority

Go to Ahrefs or any free DR checker. Pull up each competitor's website. DR 5 to 15 across the board = wide open market. DR 30 to 50 = serious competition, serious budget needed.

Step 4: Are they actively building links?

This is the part almost nobody checks. In Ahrefs, look at how many new referring domains each competitor has acquired in the last 90 days.

A competitor adding 5 to 10 new domains per month is actively investing in SEO. They're not going anywhere. A competitor who hasn't gained a single new link in 6 months is coasting. They ranked, stopped investing, and they're vulnerable.

That's your first target.

Step 5: Audit their on-page SEO

Check four things.

1) Is their target keyword in their meta/page title?

2) Does their H1 tag include the keyword and city?

3) Do they have individual location pages for suburbs and surrounding cities?

4) Does their page content multiple topics within the keyword they are trying to rank for?

If they are missing any of these, you can beat them.

Step 6: Look at the organic results below the Map Pack

If Yelp, HomeAdvisor, and directory sites are occupying the top organic spots, that's actually a good sign.

It means real business websites with decent authority haven't claimed those positions yet.

This entire process takes 10 minutes and tells you exactly where you stand, how hard it will be to rank, and who's vulnerable.

Run it before you move into a new market (or acquire a new biz).
