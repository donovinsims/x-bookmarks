# We're about to test a new review link with a few home service companies.

## Post

We're about to test a new review link with a few home service companies.

Instead of sending every customer straight to Google, it gives them a few places to leave one.

Google still gets almost all of it. Once a business already ranks well on the map, the split we're aiming for is about 85 to 90% Google, with the rest spread across BBB, Angi, and Facebook.

AI search pulls from more than your Google profile. In our tracking, Yelp, Angi, BBB, and Yellow Pages get pulled into AI answers more than any other directories, and the reviews on them help.

How it works:

> One link on the tech's QR card and in the follow-up text
> Google shows up first, with the biggest button
> BBB, Angi, and Facebook sit under it for customers who have accounts there. Most don't, so those pick up a few here and there.
> Every customer sees the same page. Nobody gets asked if they were happy first.
