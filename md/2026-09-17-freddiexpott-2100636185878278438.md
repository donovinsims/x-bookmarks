# Met a guy at a rooftop bar in Bali last week who books 40 qualified calls a month without a single sales rep on payroll

## Post

Met a guy at a rooftop bar in Bali last week who books 40 qualified calls a month without a single sales rep on payroll

28 years old
Runs a 2-person AI agency
Works maybe 3 hours a day

Asked him how he's getting that kind of volume with no team behind it

"I built a Claude agent that wakes up every morning, pulls 200 prospects from Apollo, reads their last 3 LinkedIn posts, finds the one thing they're visibly struggling with right now, and writes them a message in under 4 seconds that references it specifically"

Showed me the dashboard on his phone

25,847 messages sent last month
31% reply rate
$340,000 in closed revenue this year

"How long did it take to build?"

"A weekend. Maybe 11 hours total. I haven't touched it since March"

Turns out he'd never hired a setter, never run a paid ad, never posted a single piece of content

Just a Claude agent running in the background and a calendar that books itself

I asked what the hardest part was

"Convincing people the messages aren't written by a human"

Most people are trying to hire their way out of a volume problem

He just stopped being the bottleneck
