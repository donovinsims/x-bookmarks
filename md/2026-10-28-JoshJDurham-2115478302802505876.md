# How to get creators to make free ads for your brand on Trybe

## Post

If you have been to any DTC conferences or scrolling DTC Twitter you likely have heard “Trybe” getting thrown around in different circles.
Maybe like you, I've been asking: what's the difference between Trybe and just normal whitelisting/UGC creators?
My honest answer is that Trybe isn't all that different from any influencer SaaS tool, but they've coined a specific business model.
CREATORS ON TRYBE MAKE CONTENT FOR FREE AND GET A CUT OF REVENUE OR AD SPEND
With the rise of TikTok Shops, UGC creators want to make money from producing content, and content that works in paid social.
Trybe has coined the model where the creator makes content for free and gets anywhere from 5-10% of Trybe revenue or 5% of ad spend, depending on the size of your brand.
This makes it super easy to add whitelisting without going back and forth on paying someone $500 and not knowing if it'll convert.
MY FIRST WEEK ON TRYBE: ABOUT 15 CREATORS ONBOARDED
I have been heads down using the platform myself for a new brand I've launched.
In the first week, I onboarded about 15 creators to the platform.
This was a very light lift on my end.
I reached out to probably 15 creators in the discovery section, and a ton of creators applied to the program after finding the brand through the public brand listings.
The things I've liked about Trybe are messaging influencers back and forth in the chat (faster than DMs and emails), quickly adding whitelisting access, and setting payouts to weekly vs. net 30, which helps creators get a quick hit of cash.
TRYBE IS AN EASY WAY TO GET PERFORMANCE CONTENT FOR PAID SOCIAL
Currently, here is where I'd place Trybe when it comes to the influencer marketing ecosystem.
Trybe is a super easy use case to get performance UGC creative.
It's not necessarily going to be something where you are working with a massive influencer on a new product launch or campaign.
The typical creator in there is a newer UGC creator and has opted in to hopefully make a great side income.
If I were a brand in the $0-6M/year revenue range, I'd use Trybe to make all my UGC for paid social and grow my ad account.

TRYBE IS MISSING ORGANIC REACH AND RANKING IN CHATGPT
What Trybe is currently missing is two things:
1. Organic reach and posts help generate TOF demand and trust with the consumer.
I think there's a great play for using Trybe for performance UGC, and additionally layering in organic product seeding to work with actual influencers who generate content that gets posted on IG Reels and stories.
The organic trust you build helps drive conversion rates and new ad angles, and helps your brand show up everywhere. We did this for HexClad for years, and did over 400 million views in just one year. Think about influencer seeding like free advertising.
2. Ranking in ChatGPT.
I think 2027 will be the year of the influencer (not UGC creator) because third-party trust is so much more important as the development of AI content continues to scale.
But also ChatGPT, Claude, etc. are searching social profiles to cater to your searches in your preferred AI tool.
If you only have UGC creators dark posting content for Meta, then you will only have about 25% of the value in working with influencers.
THE TOOL DOES NOT DO THE WORK
This is my current take on Trybe and it could change as things develop and the tool changes.
The last thing I'll leave you with is that the tool does not do the work. The right tool in the right hands of people who know what they are doing is going to get you the outcome you want.
