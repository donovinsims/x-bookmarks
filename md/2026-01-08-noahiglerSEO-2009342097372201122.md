# If you're a business owner trying to DIY your local SEO, here's the 80/20.

## Post

If you're a business owner trying to DIY your local SEO, here's the 80/20.

Ignore everything else until these are done:

1. Get reviews consistently. Train your team to ask every customer in person. Shoot for 15 to 20 per month, depending on your industry. This matters more than any technical SEO trick.

2. Respond to every review within 24 hours. Mention the service and location.

3. Fix your NAP consistency. Your business name, address, and phone number should match exactly across all directories and online profiles.

4. Add all your services to your GBP with descriptions. Takes 20 minutes. Google and LLMs needs to know what you actually do.

That's it.

Absolutely NO blogs.

No advanced link building either (you'll probably waste money).

Get those 4 things right and you'll outrank 90% of your local competition.
