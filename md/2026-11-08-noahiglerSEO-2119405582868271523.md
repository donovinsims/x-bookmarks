# If you run a plumbing, HVAC or electrical company, these are the directory listings we recommend you get on.

## Post

If you run a plumbing, HVAC or electrical company, these are the directory listings we recommend you get on.

We set up 50 core listings for every home service company we start with.

Here's the list, and why each group matters:

(Bookmark this)

One rule covers all of them: use the exact same business name, address and phone number as your Google Business Profile. If your profile shows a tracking number, that same tracking number goes on every listing.

1. The maps
> Google Business Profile
> Apple Business Connect, which controls how you show up in Apple Maps, the default map on every iPhone
> Bing Places, which feeds Bing Maps and Bing search

2. Where homeowners check you out before they call
> Yelp
> BBB
> Facebook
> Nextdoor
> Angi and HomeAdvisor, which share a parent company but still run as two separate sites
> Thumbtack

3. The data aggregators
> Data Axle
> Foursquare
> TransUnion's Digital Business Profile, which used to be Neustar Localeze

These push your info out to navigation systems, apps and smaller directories. BrightLocal reports that Foursquare's data is used by apps like Uber and Nextdoor, and by ChatGPT.

4. The older directories that still show up in search
> Yellow Pages, which shares a network with Superpages and DexKnows
> Manta

5. Your trade and your town
> The dealer or installer locator for every brand you install, like Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem or Generac
> Member directories for the associations you belong to, like ACCA, PHCC, IEC or NECA
> Your local chamber of commerce

Why it matters:

> Google checks your profile against the rest of the web. When your name, address and phone match everywhere, there's less for it to doubt.
> Homeowners look you up on Yelp, the BBB and Nextdoor before they call.
> ChatGPT and Google's AI answers pull business info from sites like these, not just from Google.

How we set them up:

> Clean up old addresses, old phone numbers and duplicate listings first
> Then build the core 50, all matching the Google profile
> Do Yelp and the other sites that verify by phone by hand, so they don't hold up the rest
