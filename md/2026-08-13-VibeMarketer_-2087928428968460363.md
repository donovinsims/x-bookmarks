# Greg Isenberg’s advice for building your first useful agent is simple: let it prepare the work, then wait for approval.

## Post

Greg Isenberg’s advice for building your first useful agent is simple: let it prepare the work, then wait for approval.

outbound is a perfect place to apply the same approach.

you can build a Hermes agent that handles the hours of work surrounding every message.

it finds the account, researches the buyer, checks the evidence, watches for the right moment, and places the finished outreach in front of you.

your job is reduced to the decision that matters: approve, revise, or reject.

every decision gives the system another example of what good looks like. once a step becomes consistently reliable, Hermes can take ownership of it while you keep control of the actions carrying real risk.
