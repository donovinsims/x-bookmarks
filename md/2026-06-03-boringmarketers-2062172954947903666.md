# playbook to grow on X:

## Post

playbook to grow on X:

find the wave early. ride it. weave into the next one. repeat.

1. find the wave: stay on top of what's trending and engaging on X. That's the first decision gate.

2. ride it and own it: identify your voice and authority within that wave. study what formats successful creators are using to ride it.

3. document and share: the content strategy is just sharing what you're learning as you learn it.

4. take validated use cases and inject your perspective: big tech use cases already proven. repurpose and reformat them for your niche.

5. carve out one tinkering day a week: one full day just to experiment with whatever genuinely interests you. What you build that day becomes your tweets, articles, and videos.

6. define exactly who you're talking to: pick a specific audience group, stay within a topic they care about, and bring them use cases that are directly relevant to their world. broad topic plus specific person plus real use case. that combination is how you cut through the noise and own a lane on X.

7. ride the next wave with what you already know: the tool changes. your expertise doesn't. what changes is the trend you're covering. what stays the same is your audience, your perspective, and everything you've learned. You are not starting over, you're showing up to the next wave already ahead.

8. overcome imposter syndrome with the one chapter rule: you don't need to be the world's foremost expert. You just need to be one chapter ahead of your audience.
